## 스크립트

With over 25 years of experience working with academia, the AMD University program empowers educators, students and researchers with AMD technology. Along with our hardware and software donations, our worldwide technical team offers a wealth of open-source teaching material, tutorial labs, advanced training and technical support. AMD offers researchers access to state-of-the-art hardware through programs including our AI and HPC cluster, HACC program and donation program. The AI and HPC cluster puts computational power into the hands of those conducting groundbreaking research. To date, the fund has donated over 25 petaflops of computing power to over 40 grantees. The grants are used to power projects from AI and machine learning to HPC modeling and simulations. Over 6,000 researchers have been able to use AMD computing power through our program for their projects and discoveries. Our donation program provides AMD hardware and software directly to universities to deploy in their labs for both teaching and research. AMD also funds novel research in heterogeneous compute acceleration for high-performance computing. Our heterogeneous accelerated compute clusters, or HACCs as we call them, is a program in place at some of the world's most prestigious universities. Each HACC cluster is comprised of high-end compute nodes powered by AMD EPYC CPUs, Instinct GPUs, Alveo and adaptive SoC accelerator cards, and high-speed networking for scale-out experiments. The HACC program fosters a community of leading academic teams to conduct state-of-the-art research in heterogeneous systems, architectures, tools, and applications. Our program is a catalyst for collaboration and innovation, empowering research teams to advance their research. The research we enable is so important because of its high impact across the sciences and is helping shape a better future for all of us. Research institutions and universities can apply for a new round of grants through the AI and HPC Fund. Researchers working in relevant areas are invited to apply to join the HACC clusters. Accelerate your research together with AMD.
