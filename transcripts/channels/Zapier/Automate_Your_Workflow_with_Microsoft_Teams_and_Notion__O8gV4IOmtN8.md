## 스크립트

Do you want to be able to send your team channel a message whenever you create a new notion database item? Let me show you how to do it automatically using Zapier. To do this task, I want to create a Zap. In Zapier, a Zap is a simple way to connect different apps and make them work together automatically. With Zapier's Noode app builder, I can create my Zap from scratch, or with Zapier's new AI co-pilot, I could just describe what I want. I could say something like, "When a new Notion database item is created, send a channel message to our Microsoft Teams." and it'll build the workflow for me. My zap will then be created. On the lefth hand side, the copilot will tell me the steps that I need to take to complete my zap. And if I need to tweak my prompt or troubleshoot anything, I can just type right into the co-pilot. It walks me through the process. To start, select your trigger. Our trigger is an event that starts any zap. In this case, our trigger is notion. I'll need to log into my account before configuring my steps. To do this, click on the account tab. The first time we set up notion, we'll need to connect it with Zapier. If it's your first time logging in, a link will pop up for you to log into your account. And don't worry, Zapier will only perform the actions you tell it to in a given Zap. We'll then need to fill out our trigger apps data. Select the data source you'd like to use for your Zap and then click continue. In order to finish setting up the trigger, we'll need to pull in some example data. To do that, click test trigger. Testing our steps ensures that the data we entered is working. You're all set with the first step. Click continue with selected record and we can start creating our action in Zapier. An action is the task that happens automatically after our trigger is set off. Your action event should already be set. So log into your Microsoft Teams account just like you did with your notion account. From there, we can start to assign our trigger apps data into the action apps fields. Copilot will automatically fill out our configuration steps with our source data. And if my data isn't there, I can ask the copilot to fill it out for me. With that, I could select from the dropown menu or add my own text to customize my zap even further. Once that's complete, I'll test the step. Great. Let's check our team's channel to make sure this worked. Yep, there it is. This is ready to turn on. Now, whenever a new database item is created in Notion, a message will automatically be sent out to our Microsoft team. So, whether you're managing a sales pipeline, automating a marketing campaign, or building your own custom business app, Zapier makes it easy to build applications without coding.
