## 스크립트

(serene music) - I think sometimes, I forget to take a breath. You know, the world is just upon you and I forget to breathe, and you get out there and everything just goes away. And so you just, you exhale. (serene music) (trickling water) I deployed to Iraq in '04, and injured and medevaced in '05. And then 17 months in a hospital. I had been gone for two years and left my family. We were really close. I think I was a really good mother. And I came home (weeping) sorry. And I came home, and I wasn't the person I was when I left. (gentle breeze blowing) Project Healing Waters was everything I was looking for. I think if you've been in the military you're drawn to those Pages on Facebook. These volunteers do rod building, casting, fly tying, all the way down to how to pack your bag. Good morning. These men and women have been living with post-traumatic stress disorder for decades. (cranking fishing reel) They've got their ghosts and so do I. - Down here a couple hundred yards. - We all share our fish stories or fundraisers or outings, whatever's going on, and it's just this bond that happens, and it happens very quickly. I went from can't help myself, to helping myself, to helping others and putting a family back together. - Oh nice, beautiful. - Never caught one out here before. - It gives you hope, when for a very long time, there wasn't any hope. Oh! Did you see that one? (laughing) It gives you hope, yeah. (gentle music) (laughing) Woo! (laughing)
