## 스크립트

I'm here today at the Austin Q2 Stadium, teaching some young women from the Ann Richards School how to build computers. It's a great way to celebrate STEM Day. It's really important to support young women in STEM, to give them a leg up on how to work with technology or mathematics or wherever their passion is. Those opportunities traditionally haven't been available to them. When I started, you just didn't see women around, so seeing this group of young women today was very inspirational. There's no better time in the industry right now with AI and machine learning to get young people interested in STEM. It can really kind of revolutionize the way they grow, the way they think, the way they learn. It's been really exciting to watch them just kind of light up. My advice for any young woman, whether it's coding, whether it's hardware engineering, you know, if you find something you're passionate about, you're going to really excel. Happy STEM Day. Go out and celebrate STEM Day building a computer. Just like we did today.
