## 스크립트

Working closely with local communities in Japan Nissan supports areas where the population is shrinking and aging by leveraging technology and services that bring innovative solutions Namie, a town in Fukushima Prefecture, is an example By speaking with residents we've identified needs and solutions and with our partners we're bringing services such as Smart Mobility and Smart Mobility Kids to help residents and visitors move more freely around the town thereby revitalizing and energizing the community Thanks to years of engineering and field tests the services are intuitive and easy to use Nissan has more than a decade of EV expertise Our energy management system helps provide clean energy and supports the supply of power in emergencies making residents lives easier Nissan aims to expand the reach of these services for a cleaner, safer, and more inclusive world
