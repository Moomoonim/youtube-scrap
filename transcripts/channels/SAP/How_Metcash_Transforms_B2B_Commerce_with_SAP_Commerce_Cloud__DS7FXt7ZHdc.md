## 스크립트

Metcash is Australia's leading wholesaler for independent businesses. In the last four to five years, we have been partnering with SAP Commerce Cloud. Before SAP Commerce Cloud, just too much paperwork, a lack of transparency. Life with Commerce Cloud, totally different. We're able to serve up to our customers line-level detail of new lines that they are looking to procure for their business. What Sorted is, is our B2B e-commerce solution where we partner up with SAP Commerce Cloud. It's a basically a two-sided marketplace. If you're an independent business and you're looking to grow your turnover, inspire new customers to enter your store, Sorted is where you go. We know that over 75% of our customers use the search bar to find new products. AI for us is a critical next step. Our customers do not have an abundance of time. They are running really busy businesses. So the role of search, the role of personalization AI to drive and prompt recommendations for you is absolutely critical. At the moment, Sorted is doing around about 48,000 transactions per week, and we have over 3,500 different suppliers on the platform. We did some recent case studies, and ordering through Sorted created 75% time saving for our customers, which we think is pretty phenomenal. Partnering with SAP and what we've achieved as a business with Sorted has just galvanized just a new sense of momentum and excitement in terms of what is possible, how we engage with customers in a simpler better and way more efficient way that's been better than ever before.
