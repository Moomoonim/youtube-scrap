## 스크립트

Southern Methodist University is a private research university with a rich history and close ties to the vibrant culture and business of the downtown Dallas metro area. Accelerate your vision to become a smart campus. SMU is a globally known institution with really high marks. We were pretty on the outside, but our insides were aging faster than we were taking care of. For me, the vision has always been for the campus to run as efficiently and as effectively as it can. From a cost standpoint, but also from a comfort standpoint. SMU is like a small city. There are over 100 buildings now, and the connectivity of each of those buildings to our systems with Building X and our operations manager, we interconnect all of these facilities out here. What I'm looking for is true building performance. What we did first was ID tag everything. There's different measuring components over 12,000 of them out there across the campus, from air handlers to steam traps that basically tell you there's going to be a problem or there is a problem real time. You can remotely solve those problems without rolling a truck. We collect data every 15 minutes from 65,000 points across campus, which I then can take on demand and analyze. We then can have that insight that we need into the campus and make decisions, data driven decisions. It's helping them to keep the students satisfied, comfortable, safe, and really understand how their buildings are living and breathing, so to speak. All of our investments have really paid back in huge ways. We save several million dollars a year in energy bills alone that allow us to reinvest back into the university's infrastructure.
