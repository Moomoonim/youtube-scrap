It’s not a linear relationship It’s not like with every hour of sleep that you lose your accident rate gradually increases In fact, it’s more like a hockey stick It’s an exponential risk Imagine this scenario You call a taxi to go to the airport and two taxis show up And I say that the taxi on the right-hand side has a 30% higher likelihood of getting into a crash than the one on the left Which one would you like to choose? The answer is obvious But that’s the position we put ourselves in when we neglect our sleep My whole work with Nissan is to improve the driving experience to make any driver behind the wheel a happy person to make this going from A to B a positive experience And there are many ways of doing it You can have a car that drives for you or I can help anybody become a better driver and have the skills to have a positive driving experience
