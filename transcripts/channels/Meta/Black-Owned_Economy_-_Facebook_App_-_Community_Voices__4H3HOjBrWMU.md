## 스크립트

- Everything I do is for my kids, really. I've got two boys and a girl and I know that at some point in life, they're going to experience racism. And so, I just want them to be able to see that there is support and there is a community, and it's even better that mommy created something. I wanted to give black owned businesses an opportunity, a level opportunity. So, Black Owned Economy is dedicated to creating social, economical and financial equality for the community. - Build generational wealth and to be the first generation to do that as Black women. - If you like, look in the group, a few people put I literally thought I was going to have to give up my business during the pandemic. I didn't know how I was going to survive. I didn't know how I was going to pay my rent. And then they posted in the group and they just sold out or they've got like more subscriptions or they've got more customers. So it gives them a new lease of life. I just want my kids to be born into at least some sort of a progress. Do you know what? I am proud of myself for creating something, and it just makes me feel hopeful that things can change and will change.
