You can then continue to use the spare key without having to worry that your vehicle will be stolen with the lost key. The new A-Class Sedan is available with three gasoline and one diesel engine. All gasoline engines now have a starter generator (RSG) and 48-volt power supply. Depending on the situation, the RSG provides an additional boost of 10 kW for a short time. The RSG acts as a mild hybrid, providing a temporary power boost as well as hybrid functions such as sailing and recuperation. The advantages of 48 votes? More power and more efficiency. The A-Class Sedan is available with the Driving Assistance package, which features a number of technologically advanced systems specifically designed to keep you safe on the road. The optional Active Lane Keeping Assist system can warn the driver when they unintentionally leave their lane. The keypoint is the switch from the previous ESP intervention to the new steering intervention, which increases comfort by being gentler and by giving the driver an improved feeling of control should a situation like this arise. Active Distance Assist, what we call DISTRONIC, acts as a very smart cruise control because it can automatically maintain a preferred distance from the car driving ahead.
