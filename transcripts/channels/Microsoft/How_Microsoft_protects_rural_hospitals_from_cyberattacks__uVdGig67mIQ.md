## 스크립트

Summit Healthcare serves a 3000 square mile area. The next major hospital is nearly three hours away. There's so much to tell about Summit. It's a very special little hospital. Our town is not huge, but we offer services that you will find in a big city. I started coming as a patient in 1983. I've had the same primary care physician, for the last 40 years. My husband goes to the same doctor, my mother-in-law, my mother. He's our family doctor. In rural Show Low, Arizona, we had 170,000 cybersecurity attacks last year. If Summit Healthcare had a disruption of service, it would hugely impact our community. We are a smaller hospital, and so we are basically the resource. Cybersecurity is extremely important to our patients and our physicians. All it takes is one attack, and it can completely collapse our system. Microsoft began providing cybersecurity support to Summit Healthcare in June 2024 and is committed to providing cybersecurity solutions to rural hospitals in the US. This partnership that we're entering into with Microsoft is absolutely critical for our future. We get access to the most advanced cybersecurity tools in the marketplace. It's vital that we provide these services to our rural community because, folks have to travel a long ways to get to alternate forms of care. Summit's partnership with Microsoft keeps our system safe, so we can focus on what matters most.
