## 스크립트

I realized I belonged at Siemens when I saw other young women coming to ask for me to be their mentor, for me to be a coach and a guide. And it changed my life because what I was doing not only had an impact on Siemens, but it also had an impact on people and that let me know I was in the right place. My name is Ayana Nathan, and I'm the Vice President of the healthcare vertical market for Siemens Smart Infrastructure here in the US. Siemens really transforms the healthcare environment not only for patients, but visitors, their family and friends, clinical, the facility staff. We help patients heal in a more healthy, safe environment. Growing up, I was a black child in a predominantly white school, and back then, you know, it was a little difficult. However, I remember wanting to feel like I belonged. I worked hard because I wanted people to see my value, I wanted them to see my skill set, that I had something to contribute. And that is what drove a lot of my success today, is just wanting to be seen. I grew up in a small town called Sanford, Florida and straight out of college, I came to Siemens. As a sales trainee, I thought I'd be selling products, right? However, I was good at a lot of things, and throughout my entire career, people would just tap me on the shoulder and say ‘you seem to be good at this. Have you considered this?’ It really was because someone saw something in me that I might not have even seen myself. So thanks to those taps, I'm where I am today: Vice President of a market. I have a really close family. ‘What’s up?’ When me and my siblings get together it's laughter. There's a lot of food always, and there's a lot of fun. We love hearing about each other's successes and how we can encourage each other. ‘Amazing experience. My family's like 80% of my life.’ One of my biggest passions outside of work is kickboxing. It’s a great full body workout, it's a great stress reliever, but it became my go to sport because it really just helped me to focus. It helps me to get in the zone to really know that ‘listen, you're tough, you're a fighter, you can really accomplish anything’. And that has given me a lot of joy and energy. It's what I love. Throughout my career, I found that we didn't have a lot of women in leadership, especially in sales and business roles. You see a lot of women in the support roles, which is great, but to see strong leaders who are knowledgeable about the industry, that's really important for the women that are coming along in Siemens and outside of Siemens to see. One of the things about being seen is being heard. I want my team to feel like they are heard. I really believe in the power of inclusion. If we're going to achieve innovation, you have to have ideas from everywhere, not just from a few.
