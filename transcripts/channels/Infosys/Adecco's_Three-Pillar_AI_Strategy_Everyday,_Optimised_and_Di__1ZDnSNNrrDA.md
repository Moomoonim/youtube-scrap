## 스크립트

Now, in terms of Adecco, AI is big turn oil. AI is there to stay, so we cannot ignore it. So, what we're doing is we're taking advantage of it, right? And we can take advantage of it in different ways. One of the things we started to do is to create an AI strategy. And actually, an AI strategy with three pillars. The first one, we call it everyday AI. The second one, optimizing AI. And the third one, disrupting AI. And the first one is really for every single employee in the company being capable of leveraging all the capabilities to be able to be more more productive, get more knowledge, and get faster and better in everything they do. The second one, which is optimizing with AI, is really looking at personas. So, people, whether they are in the front office like our recruiters, our consultants, our people in finance, HR, typical back office, is looking at every single of those jobs and being capable of leveraging AI to be more productive, deliver with more capability, and with more quality as well. Now, the last one, disrupting with AI, is the most interesting one, probably. Is where you look at a process end-to-end, and where you redesign the process, where you say, "This element of the process will stay human because the human experience and the human judgment is the most important." But then, you may have other blocks of your process that you say you can totally identify. And then, some other elements where you bring the human and the AI together to be able to be an accelerator in a certain sense. Essentially, at the end of the day, shareholders, board, executives, they all look always at two key measures. What do you do with the revenue growth, and what do you do in terms of improving the profit? Potentially, a third one about cash is also important. But essentially, it's very difficult to measure immediately those the effect you have with AI on those typical measures. So, what you do is you go one layer lower and you look at what you could call in-process measures. And then those in-process measures have either a direct impact on revenue or a direct impact on profit through the productivity. So, the measures we use, seeing that we are kind of a recruiter company, is fill rate, time to fill, effectiveness of our recruiters. And let me stay there, but you have those in-process measures that then directly correlate. One of the interesting measures as we redesigned our recruiting process is that we can see that about 60% of the conversations that we are having through chat and other ways are happening outside working hours. So, you can imagine the productivity. You go home in the evening, you launch a certain number of job request, candidate request, and you come back in the next morning and 50% of your job has been done. So, it is very rewarding in terms of productivity. And then you take every element separately and then you add it end-to-end and you can get up to 50% of productivity if you are really redesigning the end-to-end process and not only one element. Let me talk about two spaces where we work with Infosys. One is on the application maintenance and support and the other one is to redesign the core processes in finance. If you take the application management and support, you can imagine with all the knowledge that is being captured that AI can really now make the difference. And so, Infosys has been able to take all the capability they have to actually improve drastically the response rate on those incidents and then also make sure what we always look at is avoid those incidents to happen so that we have the measures in place beforehand. Exceptional partnership on that front. The second partnership is on turning around all our finance systems. Creating templates where we implement for all the countries we're in. It's on the Microsoft Dynamics platform. And Infosys is kind of handling the one deployment after the other and has been capable of industrializing the deployment. So, meaning that the first ones you struggle a little bit on what you do, but then the other ones you go at the speed of light. And we're looking forward to that because we need to move many, many, many countries to the same platform and honestly, for us this is the foundation to be able to leverage AI afterwards.
