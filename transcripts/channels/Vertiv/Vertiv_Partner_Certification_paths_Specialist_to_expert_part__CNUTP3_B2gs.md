## 스크립트

The certification is a step-by-step growth plan. It starts with a learning the specialist foundation, progresses to the professional level with live interactive session and focused technical activities, and culminates in the aspect of sighting measure at Vertiv Academy here in Bologna. Each step increases practical exposure and full access. And crucially, expert level partners continue to receive follow-up session and exclusive support. So, they stay up-to-date and keep consolidating their skills. It's not just a program, it's a partnership where our technical sales team and trainers continue to stand by their side. A structured path from specialist through professional to expert supports partner in growing, gaining confidence, and thus learning into a competitive advantage. Certification is more than a badge. It's a proof of expertise, access to Vertiv tools, and a clear differentiator in the market. It shows customers that our partners are prepared, professional, and supported by Vertiv's global expertise.
