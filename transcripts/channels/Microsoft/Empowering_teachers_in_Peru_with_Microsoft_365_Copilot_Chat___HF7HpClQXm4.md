## 스크립트

In the Lima Metropolitan area, the number of children at risk of not receiving a quality education totals just over 445,000 students. Teachers face a very diverse classroom environment and must consider how to ensure that all students learn, even at very different academic levels. The World Bank is actively working to improve education. And in collaboration with the Lima Regional Directorate we decided to train teachers in the use of artificial intelligence, because AI has the potential to transform education. Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat has been made available to more than 390 schools and 482 teachers in Metropolitan Lima, to improve learning in our classrooms. Copilot helps me create personalized activities for my neurodivergent children without it being my specialty in a way that they feel integrated, which was not the case before. I think that's phenomenal—I'm happy. I was scared of artificial intelligence. I thought it would replace teachers and I didn't believe I was good enough to handle these types of tools. But through this training we've had, Copilot is making me feel empowered. It suggests very creative and personalized strategies for each of the students. As a result, their learning is improving. That makes me feel accomplished. Now I can give more to my students.
