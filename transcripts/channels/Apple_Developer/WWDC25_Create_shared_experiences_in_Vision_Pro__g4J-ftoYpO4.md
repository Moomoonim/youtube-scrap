## 스크립트

There’s something magical about being able to experience virtual content with people halfway across the world. We have seen this capability unlock remarkable use cases, ranging from enterprise, productivity, to entertainment, and more. But what if you want to bring someone who’s physically next to you into a spatial experience in Vision Pro? In visionOS 26, Nearby Window Sharing enables you to build shared spatial experiences for people co-located in the same room. Like Defenderella by Rock Paper Reality, a tower defense game that comes to life in your own living room. You can take advantage of Nearby Window Sharing in your existing SharePlay apps with no additional code.
