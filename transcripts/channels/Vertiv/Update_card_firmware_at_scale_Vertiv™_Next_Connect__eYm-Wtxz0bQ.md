## 스크립트

Welcome to Vertiv NXC. In this short walk-through, we'll show you how to bulk update firmware across multiple supported Vertiv devices quickly and efficiently. Start by navigating to the equipment tab, then go to the devices submenu under your partner, customer, site, or group. Anywhere you can see a list of devices. Now select two or more devices of the same card model. This is important. Firmware updates must be applied to identical cards only. Click the bulk provisioning icon. The bulk provisioning window opens showing a summary of the devices and models you selected. Double-check to make sure the models match, otherwise the firmware update option will be disabled. Now choose the action. Apply firmware. You'll see a list of available firmware versions. Vertiv recommends applying the latest version unless specific documentation says otherwise. Once you've selected the firmware, click run action to begin the update. You'll get a confirmation prompt. &gt;&gt; [music] &gt;&gt; Click to proceed. The operation log will show the status of the firmware update for each device. Each update takes about 10 to 15 minutes per device. This includes transferring the firmware, flashing the memory, and rebooting. A few important notes. Firmware updates can run in the background. You can safely navigate away from this page. Devices should ideally have a unique IPv4 address for smoother updates. You can update monitored and unmonitored devices, including those with only IPv6. And that's it. You just completed a bulk firmware update in Vertiv NXC. For more tips and support, visit our help center or reach out to your Vertiv NXC representative.
