## 스크립트

Summer time can really turn up the heat in the car. Introducing Nissan's cool paint technology. An innovative paint that helps keep heat out and your car cool. Let's compare these two cars under the blazing sun. Getting into the car. This driver is feeling the heat, while this driver feels cooler. The secret lies within the metamaterial embedded in the radiative cooling paint. Normal paint has substances that make the car hotter. Nissan's radiative cool paint has tiny particles that prevent the temperature from rising. It has two microstructure particles that react to light. One reflects near-infrared rays in sunlight that cause normal paint to produce heat. The other creates electromagnetic waves that redirect the rays through the atmosphere, and back to space. Together, they reduce heat being transferred to the roof, hood and doors. Helping to consume less energy by reducing air conditioner use and emitting fewer carbon emissions. Nissan's cool paint technology is now on trial in the open tarmac at Tokyo's Haneda airport under Japan's scorching summer sun for a real world test. Bringing us closer to a more sustainable future.
