## 스크립트

Artificial intelligence is deeply changing data centers. We're witnessing a shift in data center power infrastructure requirements. As instead of the steady and predictable power loads we're used to, we now see rapid power fluctuation that may challenge traditional power systems. That's why we developed the vertive AI load simulator, an industry first innovation that's changing the way we validate the power infrastructure equipment with AI applications. This patented technology allows us to replicate realworld AI workload conditions, allowing us to test and validate UPS and power systems before deploying them on site with AI workloads, increasing the overall reliability and developing the next generation AI power systems. The simulator offers a unique flexibility in UPS testing in AI workload conditions. Through UPS firmware, we can either minimize the impact on the batteries, handling those steps over 100%. Or use the batteries for input power smoothing to reduce the stress on grid and generators. Due to growing customer demand, we are now making the simulator available also for on-site testing. Customers can now test directly in their facilities, UPS system, and their power distribution equipment.
