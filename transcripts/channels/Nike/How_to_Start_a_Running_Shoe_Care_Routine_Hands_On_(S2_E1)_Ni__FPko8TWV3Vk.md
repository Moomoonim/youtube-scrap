## 스크립트

- [Production Crew] Take one. - Oh, you stay. - This is Hands On, How to Clean Your Running Shoes. My name is Raj. I'm a Senior Apparel Designer at Nike. I create clothes. I've worked on Supreme, Stussy, the USA medal stand uniform and the others I can't say. As a designer, I like to keep the environment in mind being conscious of the amount we buy. I think even small gestures can add up over time. It can be as simple as just maintaining your running shoes. With every mile you can get out your shoes, you're wasting less. It's good for the environment. With that in mind here are the steps you should take after every run to give them that extra bit of life. The least you can do is rid yourself of any major debris. Step in mud or something more sinister. One super advanced technique I use is a handy stick to get that (beep) off your shoes. After my run, I take my shoes off. Sometimes I can just chuck them off but I have to respect them. So I undo the laces. I tap them. So getting any access mud off. I like to let them dry out. Especially if you've run in some puddles bacteria can build up. I don't like a stinky shoe. It's a big turn off. It's pretty important to give your shoes a rest day. It gives the underfoot foam a chance to fully decompress and re-expand. Ready for your next run.
