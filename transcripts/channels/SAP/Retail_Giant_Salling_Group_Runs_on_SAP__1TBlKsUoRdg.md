## 스크립트

I think the Autonomous Enterprise looks very exciting. There is definitely a lot how we can improve the way we run our business every day. Salling Group is Northern Europe's largest retailer. We have around €12 billion revenue. And we are present in six countries in Denmark, Germany, Poland and the three Baltic countries. And we run plus 2.100 stores. The reason for AI transformation is we want to serve our customer better in the future. We want to make it easier for our employees to work in our company. And then of course, we also want to do things more efficient. SAP has been helping us for the last 20 years. We have a broader ERP system across Salling Group, and everything is now upgraded to the new SAP S/4HANA Cloud And we have also signed a SAP RISE contract. And we are now moving the last system to RISE with SAP. So everything is then in place. We also want to make every day better for our employees. So the tools and the system they work with, we need to do it as intuitive and easy for them to do their daily work. The impact internally is huge. We run SAP Warehouse Management in all our 29 distribution centers. And supply chain is crucial for us. We deliver to stores every day on time to serve our customers. Next for our organization is how we adopt AI. It's all over, but how do you really make sure to get the business benefit out of it? Both for our customer, for our employees, and for our company?
