## 스크립트

Since 2019, when we participated in the Dela programme with IKEA Social Entrepreneurship and Ashoka a lot has changed Doing the home renovations ourselves like we did in the early days of Vivenda was important not only for us to understand the fundamental parts of the business but also for us to understand the real impact it has on the lives of families. Personally, I need to be home a lot because I have two children with speical needs. My goal was to have a bathroom for my children to use. I lived the transformation Vivenda brought to my house and my home. It was a great experience. To scale and offer this benefit to more people, we understood that we had to change our role and seek a more indirect impact. This growth is only possible because we put together a first class team. I believe that Vivenda has a very important role in making the impact happen through innovation. We have over 400 million people in the country living in inadequate conditions. This has an impact on people's health, safety, and especially on their development. We never forget what we do. A house is a basic right, it's our home. And without it, there is no future. Before the platform, Vivenda grew on average at a rate of 7% per year. After the platform strategy, in the past two years, this rate increase to 80% per year. But there's still a lot to do. The platform developed by Vivenda allows architects and other contractors to work together to deliver more renovations to low-income familes across Brazil. I always felt like doing home renovations was our purpose, but it was never easy. On our own, we couldn't attract customers, offer financing, and manage all the stages of the renovation. With the support of Vivenda, we can now dedicate ourselves to what we know best. Commercially speaking, doing business is good. I need it. It's my life. But that's not all. The social part is wonderful. When you see people's smiles. Vivenda could only become a home renovation organisation today because of technology But we understand that technology has to be purpose-driven. Now Vivenda is strengthening our technological infrastructure. so that our solution can be offered across all of Brazil. Our impact has been so great that we have managed to attract the private and public sector to collaborate with us. This is part of our job, developing models to fit them both. Since 2020, we have delivered hundreds of renovations and impacted thousands of people. But what motivates me the most, is to know that in the end every day we are delivering a renovation to someone. We are directly impacting the life of a family. We all deserve a pleasant and welcoming home environment. The original contribution from IKEA Social Entrepreneurship and Ashoka was very important not only for Vivenda, but also for many other organisations. The Dela programme has supported 45 social entrepreneurs around the world. Fernando is one of them.
