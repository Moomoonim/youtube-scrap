## 스크립트

Fans don't really send me Fanletters but after every game I get some love in the DM's in the form of a "GG well played". From my surroundings I get rather mixed reactions. I'd say the younger generation is very interested in it and the older generation not as much. I always encounter a lot of positivity ^when I talk of Counter-Strike. I appreciate a lot being able to share the moments I experience in Counter-Strike with my entourage, regardless if these are good or difficult moments. I have only gotten a fanletter once from my biggest fan Nöwe. He once sent me a plushie. My family unfortunately doesn't support me as much. I also moved out to expand my career. I get support from my mother, she informs herself a lot about Esports and Brawl Stars, also to see which Jobs are available in this field. The most reactions from my surroundings are mostly more like "Wow". They also often ask how much time I spend in front of the PC. My friends are very proud of me, they say that a lot, which gives me motivation to continue.
