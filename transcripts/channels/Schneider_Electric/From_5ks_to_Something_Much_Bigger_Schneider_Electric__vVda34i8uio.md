## 스크립트

(soft music) I picked up running just to try to get a little healthier. I started with 5Ks. If I wanted to continue running as I was older, I needed to slow down a little. And maybe slower meant I could go longer distances, so I started training for a half marathon. I enjoyed that, so I said, why not try a full? Boston, it will be my second marathon. I think there is a correlation ... between distance running and sustainability. It's important to focus on the longer-term effects of ... what you're doing today so that you can enjoy tomorrow. Schneider is injecting newer technology ... and new efforts that will help the B.A.A sustain this race for the future. I'm raising funds for Dana-Farber Cancer Institute. I've had cancer and been treated there. My daughter's had cancer and been treated there. My wife, mother. We're fortunate that we live so close ... to such a great institution. Crossing the finish line, it's a real moment where you can say, alright, I actually did this thing. I put my mind to it and did it. Pretty awesome.
