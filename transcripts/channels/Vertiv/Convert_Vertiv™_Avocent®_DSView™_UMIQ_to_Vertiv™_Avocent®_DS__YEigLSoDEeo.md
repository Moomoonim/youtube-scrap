## 스크립트

This video will cover the Vertive DS solution UMIQ conversion to the IPiQ. Before we begin, make sure the RM1048 is running firmware of version 1.66.1 or newer. Also make sure to note that only the UMIQ version 2 are eligible for conversion, not the UMIQ version 1. Also, once converted, the module cannot be reverted back to the UMIQ. To begin, start by connecting your UMIQ to your RM1048. Then log in to the RM1048 CLI with your username and password. In the root CLI menu, select 11 for diagnostics. In the diagnostics menu, select 12 to manage the UMIQ to IPIQ conversion. In the UMIQ conversion menu, you'll have the option to return to the root menu, enable the conversion service, disable the conversion service, or view the conversion logs. Here, we'll select one to enable the conversion service. Once enabled, wait a few minutes for the system to register the change. To view the changes, enter option three to view the conversion log. You'll be able to view the port number, the MAC address, the IP address, as well as the conversion status. You can now press enter to refresh the menu. Once complete, you should view one under the completed conversions menu. Your um IQ has successfully been converted to an IPIQ and is now ready for use.
