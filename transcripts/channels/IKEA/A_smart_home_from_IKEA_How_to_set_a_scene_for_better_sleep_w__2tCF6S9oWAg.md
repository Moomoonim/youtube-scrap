## 스크립트

A smart home from IKEA: How to set a scene for better sleep with DIRIGERA hub The hub is the heart of a smart home as it enables you to connect and control your smart products in the IKEA Home smart app. Did you know that a smart home can help you wind down before bedtime? Follow us to learn how to create a scene in the app. To start with, these are smart products that can help to create great conditions for good sleep, and here’s why: The air purifier can improve the bedroom air quality before bed. Block-out blinds can keep out the glare of city lights. Warm, dimmed light can help sleepy eyes to relax. And your favourite podcast can be streamed to help you wind down. After deciding on what each product should be scheduled to do, let’s head over to the IKEA Home smart app and save those settings as a ‘scene’. Scenes can be triggered with timers, manually in the app, or by pressing a shortcut button. Dark room, dimmed lights, cleaner air and sleepy tunes. The scene is set for better sleep. …and a good night! Make your life at home a little bit more colourful, effortless, and a little bit smarter.
