## 스크립트

Thanks to everyone for joining. It's great to meet so many colleagues from around the world. I am really proud of the work we have been doing in Colombia to raise hygiene standards during the pandemic. We are working with local partners to provide vital necessities to those who have not had access to food, water or basic hygiene products during the lockdown. Now it will be great to hear about how you have been supporting communities around the world during the pandemic. Devi in Indonesia, would you like to kick things off? It's an honour to be part of this journey. At RB, we want to show our recognition and also our appreciation to all the frontliners out there who fight against this COVID-19. We want to contribute. We are in this together no matter how small. We salute you. Thank you. As a brand that exists to protect, we felt impelled to support frontline workers. As such, we created and donated 150,000 care packages for NHS staff across the UK. Well, the UNAIDS Region Support Team really appreciates the partnership, the collaboration and the support that has been provided by RB. Through which, we have been able to provide hygiene kits to over 200,000 people in 20 countries. We partnered with Meals on Wheels to encourage Australians to support our most vulnerable community. We work with international organisations, government and civil society, focused in three areas - maternal and neonatal health, sexual education and economic development for indigenous communities. I'm so proud to be in the team to fight for the safety and health of the pregnant women in Wuhan, and provide them with the prevention gears, online consultancy service and access to the delivery bags in hospitals when the city locked down. In Kenya, we've been educating children on the importance of hand washing. Every day we're in schools educating children on why it is important to wash their hands in order to just keep disease away. The partnership we now have with Water.org will take us further in that fight and make us RB proud.
