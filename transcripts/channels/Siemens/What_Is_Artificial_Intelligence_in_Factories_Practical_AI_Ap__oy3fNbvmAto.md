## 스크립트

I'm here with Felix, an expert on industrial AI applications. Felix, when we talk about AI, artificial intelligence, it often sounds very abstract. Can you explain in simple words to someone who maybe never worked with AI what it is? Sure. Artificial intelligence is a field in computer science that tries to enable machines with human capabilities like decision making, pattern recognition, or natural language processing. So, AI is basically already around us. Can you give us some everyday examples where we maybe use AI without even noticing? Sure. For example, if you buy something online, an algorithm based on AI is checking if your credit card payment might be a fraud or not. Or if you're watching a movie on a streaming service, um the recommendations you see are based on what you have seen in the past, what other people with similar interests have seen, um and an AI algorithm then predicts a good recommendation for you. And what about the people here in the factory? How does AI make their jobs easier? For an operator at the line, it's the promise to produce better quality. For an engineer, it is the promise to simplify their um task of operating dozens of product variations. For example, the station we are standing in front of, uh we use AI to detect the position of the part in the bin and let a robot pick it. So, we do not have to sort it by hand into each position. That sounds high-tech. So, I guess what everyone wants to know is is AI going to take their jobs? In our experience, it doesn't take away their jobs, but it simplifies their tasks and let them focus on more complex things. I Basically, we want to do more with less work time. Cool. That's good to hear that we're not going to be run over by robots anytime soon.
