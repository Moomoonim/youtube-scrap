## 스크립트

CASE, a subsidiary of Credi Agricol, safeguards trillions of euros as a leading European asset servicing firm. For them, client conversations are the business. Partnering with Vast Data and Nvidia, Cases builds AI agents that capture the true meaning behind every exchange without visual context. AI transcription missed over 60% of what their client meetings were really about. Now AI agents extract context from multimodal data sources using Nemo Retriever. Once vectorized, Llama Neatron creates two outputs, one with full details and one with personal info redacted. This lets agents use the data while preserving access control. A client relations agent with full access to the details, detects sentiment, analyzes objections, and triggers follow-ups to help leaders coach. A product analyst agent works with redacted data and demographics to identify VIP feature requests and generate Jira tickets. With the NVIDIA Nemo data flywheel and the vast AI operating system, agents continuously learn and improve. Together, VAST and NVIDIA help costs build custom AI agents for every employee. [Music]
