## 스크립트

A few years after joining the company, I felt I was not the type to become a leader. I even thought about quitting several times. Dear younger self, Just before you graduated, the company that offered you a job went bankrupt. I know you felt lost. But don't worry. One year after, you’ll join a global technology company. A few years in, you’ll struggle. You’ll compare yourself to your colleagues who are charismatic and outgoing, and wonder if you are missing that special something. But it's going to be okay. Your next manager will accept you exactly as you are. That's when you will understand you don't need to be outgoing. When you work sincerely and honestly, people will support you and your work will become truly fulfilling. One day you’ll become an account executive for one of the major banks. When their project hits trouble, and the go-live date has to be postponed, you will see the project manager break down in tears. In that moment, you will see how deeply your customers care about their mission. Business isn't just about buying and selling, it's about trust. Trust is the foundation of everything. At home, you will be blessed with a loving wife and a son. You’ll enjoy travelling with them and feel happiness as you watch your son grow. In many ways, his way of thinking, how he reacts to others and the things he loves, ...just like yourself when you were young. They will bring many wonderful days into your life. Dear younger self, be your one and only because each person is already special in their own unique way. Your sincerity and dedication will open doors to an extraordinary future. Keep going and believing in yourself just as you are.
