## 스크립트

Your parcel cannot be delivered because the address has been lost. Have you ever received a text message like this? Today we’re talking about cybersecurity, i.e. how to protect your data from hackers. Mostly, hackers are looking for credit-card information or passwords, which they then use fraudulently themselves or sell on the digital black market. Think this couldn’t happen to you? In 2023, over 50,000 cases of cybercrime were reported. That’s about 5 per hour – and the number of unreported cases is probably much higher. The most common form of hacking is phishing. This is when hackers send fake emails to try to get you to enter your credit card details on a fake website or open malware – i.e. malicious software – in your inbox that can steal data. Our tip: Pay particular attention to spelling errors, non-existent authorities, unfamiliar URLs. Important: Nowadays, such attacks don’t only reach you via email, but also as a link on social media, a text message or even a phone call. So what should you do? 1. Protect yourself with strong passwords, two-factor authentication or biometric protection. 2. Carry out regular software updates to close vulnerabilities. 3. Surf safely: Be vigilant and, if in doubt, don’t click on anything. 4. In the event of an attack, ask experts for help – such as Swisscom myService.
