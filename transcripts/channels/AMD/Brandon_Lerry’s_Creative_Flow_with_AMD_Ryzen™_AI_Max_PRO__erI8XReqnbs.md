## 스크립트

As a 3D artist, I have to have tools that will keep up with my creativity and that's why I love this machine. My name is Brandon Lerry and I'm the founder of Lunchbox 3D. I got my start in documentaries, making branded films and capturing live events. I always loved being behind the camera, but I could never quite capture the visuals that were in my head. I kept seeing more and more 3D artists pop up in my feed, and their work just blew me away. The fact that they could create entire worlds just from their computer it was super inspiring. I started posting some of my early 3D work on TikTok, and one of my spec car ads took off, and then a major car company reached out to me. That's when everything changed. I like to weave nostalgia into my work. When I look at a car, I'm thinking, how does the paint reflect light? Or how did the decals age? I can shoot reference photos on the spot and then drop that straight into Adobe Substance 3D sampler. I can also use Substance Painter to make adjustments, add texture, and with AI helping accelerate material generation and lighting suggestions, I spent way less time tweaking and more time just creating. The Ryzen™ AI Max+ PRO processor handles Adobe Substance and Unreal Engine better than my old desktop. Wherever I work, I can handle massive textures and files locally, bouncing between apps and tabs and AI Max just rips through it all. And with Adobe Substance 3D Stager, clients can just make notes and improve textures all on the fly. Whether I'm out of the studio or on vacation and a client wants changes, I can just hop in and get it done. It's all about performance. We're seeing this evolution from big muscle car-like systems into smaller, smarter machines that punch way above their weight. And AMD is leading the pack. 3D used to really intimidate me, but now it just feels like second nature. What started as an idea is now a career I can carry in my backpack.
