What did you think of me before I began to speak? I know on the outside I look very still, but on the inside I am always moving. When my mom and I went for walks through the parks, she explained the seasons to me. That tree is budding because it's spring. And in autumn, we collected colorful leaves for me to draw at home. It was a good childhood. I always imagined I would grow up, marry, and have children. happy enough. I gave her a bottle. &gt;&gt; I did become a mom. I lived and loved and sang and danced. But in life, you can't have good things without the bad. I'd had some tests at the hospital and now it was time for my diagnosis. Motor neurone disease progressive incurable disabling and terminal. I kept looking at my shrinking left palm and was terrified of disability. The changes came rapidly. As I held my newborn baby at night, my mind raced with worry. I found it difficult to speak, especially at night or in a crowded room. My voice was quieter and my tongue struggled with the words in my mouth and I felt off balance. God, this is depressing, isn't it? Cheddar, you told me we were going to have fun, and I've made them all cry. This is supposed to be a show full of drag queens. I don't know about you, but I want to have some fun. When things get really dark, you have to make your own light. Don't Go first. M andd has been a difficult and transformative journey. I lost so much of the woman I thought I would be. But I discovered that inside me there were lots of other amazing people I could become. I've had experiences that I would have never had met people that would have passed me by. I'm a full-time artist and my work has been exhibited in galleries all over the world. My world is full of movement, excitement, and love. &gt;&gt; My voice was created from a home video of me with my daughter, Aviva. &gt;&gt; I felt like crying when I first heard it. And my children, who couldn't remember my voice, were so happy to hear the real me. I even have my slight list back which I don't hate like I did before. I welcome it. Now Aviva is grown up and performing in my show. I don't need to walk, run, or even speak. I make magic with my gaze. I can do anything with my eyes and I'm excited for what the future may hold.
