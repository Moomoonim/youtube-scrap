## 스크립트

I think I hardly know anyone who has n't been in a psychological crisis. 18 million people in Germany suffer from a mental illness every year; that's one in five, and yet nobody talks about it because they're afraid of being stigmatized, afraid of losing their job in the worst-case scenario. The work environment is still so difficult to talk about mental health issues. [Music] I want people to be able to get a sick note for mental health issues, to be able to say something normal like, "I can't today," or "I have back pain." And I want managers in their companies to also talk about the fact that they're not always doing well. And my first digital depression application, which has been certified and can be prescribed like a prescription, is low- threshold help, and for some, a somewhat anonymous first step without having to go directly to the person posting on LinkedIn. People have to deal with their mental health issues so that they know they're not alone and can publicly acknowledge them. And that's what happens on LinkedIn; people suddenly comment, and we talk about what we can achieve together. [Music]
