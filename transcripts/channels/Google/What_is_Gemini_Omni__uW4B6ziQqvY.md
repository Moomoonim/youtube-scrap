Make the violin invisible or change the camera angle. I can give Omni a drawing with visual instructions and it knows how to incorporate them into a full video. Because Omni is built with Gemini's world knowledge, Omni can just do cool things like generate an object for each letter of the alphabet. Previous models really struggled with this.
