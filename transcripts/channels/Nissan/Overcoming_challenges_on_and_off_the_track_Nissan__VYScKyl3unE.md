## 스크립트

When you put on your helmet you don’t see what the gender is Motorsport is a male-dominated sport At first I was like, “Oh my god, I don’t have my place here” “I don’t fit in this” But you just have to prove yourself on the track and prove to them that you have your space there If it wasn’t for racing I don’t know what I would do Racing helped me a lot with my developmental language disorder I was hiding in my trailer because I was too shy to talk to other people Since the past five years I became more confident, I talk more There are not a lot of women in motorsport It’s always nice to talk with them and see that girls are actually interested in motorsport
