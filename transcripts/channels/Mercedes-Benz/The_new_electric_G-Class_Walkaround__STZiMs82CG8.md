## 스크립트

And here it is, the all-new Mercedes-Benz G 580 with EQ Technology – the very first all-electric G-Class. We are looking forward to showing you everything you need to know about this amazing car. My name is Toni and I welcome you to join this exclusive walkaround. The all-new G 580 with EQ Technology. The G-Class has one of the most iconic silhouettes Mercedes-Benz has ever built. And as you can see with our EDITION ONE, the G 580 is still very much a G-Class. It was important for us to keep what makes this model so unique, while also making it stand out from the combustion engine models. Exterior Highlights One of the big visual highlights is right here at the front with the new black panel grille, that carries the G-Class-specific radiator grille into the age of electromobility and ensures an unmistakable appearance. It is fitted with an LED strip that displays an amazing light animation. This brand-new colour MANUFAKTUR south sea blue magno is only available for the electric G-Class. And on top, we offer the EDITION ONE in four additional colours. Every one of them a perfect match for this EQ specific edition with all its blue details we will discover in the next minutes. For example, the protective strip, which is complimented by a blue stripe. The rims have also been renewed and aerodynamically improved. On the EDITION ONE, they are 20 inches in size and completely black. We also introduced blue brake calipers that complement the EQ-specific appearance of this special version. And as we are already close to the wheel, I'd like to say a few words about the electric drive concept of the G 580: Because one out of four individual electric motors is directly connected to one wheel. As they are individually controllable, the vehicle offers unique driving characteristics both on-road and off-road. We'll go into more detail later. One small but very cool detail is located right here in the doors. The EDITION ONE gets black doorknobs with an embossed G. Let's move on to the bonnet, which we have raised slightly for the all-electric G-Class. By doing this we have optimised the aerodynamics and the pedestrian protection of the car. Furthermore, there is an additional spoiler lip on the roof that helps with optimising air flow. Coming to the side of the car you can see another aerodynamical adjustment we made: On the rear wheels we integrated so-called air curtains. These cut-outs in the wheel arch extensions ensure better air circulation. Coming to the rear, we have the iconic spare wheel, of course. But wait – one thing has changed. With the EDITION ONE, we are introducing the new G-Class design box. As you can see it is perfectly adjusted to the colour of the car and the window of the rear door. What’s inside you are asking? Let’s have a look. It’s the perfect compartment for your charging accessories! That is what leads us to our next topic: Charging &amp; Range. And since this is the first ever electric G-Class, we should definitely talk about it. The G 580 with EQ Technology has enough battery and range for your daily adventure. Of course, we don't let it compete against range champions like the EQS, but it competes to be the best electric off-road vehicle in the world. With one charge, our legendary Mount Schöckl in Austria can be conquered more than 13 times. That corresponds to an altitude higher than Mount Everest. And after an extensive off-road tour, you can charge your G-Class with your Mercedes-Benz Wallbox, with up to 11 kW, comfortably at home. If you need it to be a little bit faster, you can use DC chargers with speeds up to 200 kW. Interior Highlights Let’s jump to the interior, where the G 580 awaits you with a ton of cool new features. But first of all, let’s listen to the amazing sound experience That’s what we call G-ROAR, and it brings the typical G-Class sound into the electric future. And besides this cool aura and several event sounds, we will delight you with a very emotional G-Class-specific driving sound for this all-electric model. As I already mentioned, there are five different colours available for the exterior. Meanwhile, the interior of the EDITION ONE always comes in leather Nappa in the new colour silver pearl black. The blue details of the EDITION ONE exterior are also reflected in the interior with these blue carbon trim parts and the really cool blue stitching. There are also some essential features in the MBUX of the G 580. For example, the EQ Screen, that shows you essential information like your state of charge, range and consumption. Another cool feature is the electric intelligent navigation that computes the energy demand for the calculation of the route. The topography, route, heating and cooling requirements, as well as charging stations along the route and more are all taken into consideration to plan the perfect trip. But now, let’s talk about THE HIGHLIGHT of the electric G-Class, its off-road capabilities. Let’s move to our G 580 in the colour desert sand and a more off-road-oriented configuration to learn more about it. Off-road Capabilities So, obviously we’re not making any compromises when it comes to the off-road capabilities of the electric G-Class: The 4x4 qualities of the G, which have always set the highest standard, will not just find their way into the age of electric mobility, but will push boundaries even further. The chassis design remains extremely off-road capable, typical G-Class: ladder frame, an independent suspension at the front axle and a newly developed rigid rear-axle, for the integration of the all-electric powertrain. Accordingly, the G 580 comes with a slope climbing ability of up to 100%, is stable at tilt angles of 35° and has a maximum fording depth of 850 mm when driving through water. That’s 150 mm more than with the combustion models. With the Low Range button, we activate a two-gear transmission with an off-road reduction like in any conventional G-Class. This means more torque, very efficient recuperation and more off-road performance! And as every G-Class comes with differential locks, so does the electric one. But here we are not talking about mechanical components, here we are talking about virtual locks, which are realised by torque vectoring. That means if one of the four electric motors has no grip, the electric powertrain shifts automatically from one to another within milliseconds. And to top it off the G 580 with EQ Technology comes with various other helpful features for driving on unpaved terrain. By now, most of you probably already know the G-TURN, which allows the vehicle to turn on the spot. This not only looks impressive but is also incredibly helpful when an obstacle appears in front of you. You can also look forward to G-STEERING, which can be used to minimise the cornering radius on narrow trails and in tight curves. And with Off-road Crawl, we’re providing you with a new kind of off-road cruise control where you set the speed and the G 580 is riding up- and downhill automatically. You can just focus on steering the car. But that’s not all about these features. We will have deep dives on several topics coming to the G-Class Private Lounge – so stay tuned for more! I hope all of you are just as excited about the first electric G-Class as we are. Also check out the other walkarounds for both the G 500 and the Mercedes-AMG G 63. But for now, thanks for joining - and see you next time!
