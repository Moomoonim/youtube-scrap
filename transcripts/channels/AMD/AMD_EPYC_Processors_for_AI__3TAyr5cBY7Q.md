## 스크립트

AI is everywhere. It's being used across automotive, manufacturing, retail, financial services and medical industries. AI techniques such as classical ML, large language models and recommendation systems are finding their way into uses across the enterprise. As business leaders are developing their plans for AI, there are a few things to consider. First, you may need to modernize and make space for AI solutions. Second, core workloads that are critical to business operations must continue to be supported. And third, adopting a data center infrastructure that enables optimization across core training inference and mixed workloads can be critical in optimizing short and long term costs. Consolidating existing workloads freeing up space, power and cooling while lowering TCO that can free up budget for AI solutions. An important consideration is choosing a platform that provides end-to-end capabilities from training to inference. AMD EPYC processors deliver leadership performance across core business applications, cost-effective inferencing and incredible scalability to host GPU accelerators for training. They provide an efficient foundation for your AI journey and work well with AMD or third-party GPUs through the entire AI lifecycle.
