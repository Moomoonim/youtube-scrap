## 스크립트

This is the new Google Pixel 9 Pro XL. And I’m about to show you what this flagship device can do. The phone is powered by a Google Tensor G4 processor, which offers higher processing speeds and greater efficiency. The processor allows for AI features, better photos and videos and generally more support for your everyday life. According to Google, the battery lasts for up to 24 hours and up to 100 hours in Extreme Battery Saver mode. This is particularly handy when you’re on the go and don’t have a charger. If you do need to charge your phone, you can charge up to 55% in just a half an hour – that’s twice as fast as the previous model. What I really love is that it’s also super easy to share battery power with your friends – like this. Gemini, Google’s new AI model, has had a major update. And now they’ve combined all the AI features into one app that you can use in daily life. It’s an AI chatbot that understands text, images, videos, sounds and also spoken language. You can paste a YouTube video link and ask it to summarise the most important parts. In just a few seconds you have the answer. Another feature is that you can upload a photo of your plants and Gemini will give you advice. So you can take good care of them even if you have a brown thumb. So it can make your life easier in a tonne of different ways and your interactions in the chat help Gemini to continually improve. According to Google, Gemini can understand and analyse a lot more information more quickly than the competition. Gemini Nano is the first local AI of its kind on the market. I know, the Circle to Search feature came out at the beginning of the year, but it’s just too legendary not to mention again. Never heard of it? Ok, so, let’s say you’re on TikTok and you see a great pair of glasses but you don’t know what brand they are or where you can buy them. So you can simply circle them without having to switch apps. Then Google will spit out lots of search results that could match the product. And voilà. I’ve already got them on. Sure, you can use keywords to search for the glasses on Google, but that takes time. And maybe you won’t find these exact glasses. A gamechanger is that it also works in the camera app. That means you can basically circle and search for things in real life. Check it out. For photo and video enthusiasts, the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL offers an amazing photo experience with a 30x photo zoom and a 20x video zoom. With these specs, you can take great photos and videos. So, let’s say you’re taking a group photo, that means you won’t be in it. But with “Add Me”, you can avoid this problem by simply adding the missing person. Being there is everything. If you want to enhance a photo, you can use the Magic Editor and Best Take. The AI-powered tools make your photos pop. The Google Pixel 9 Pro XL is innovative and comes with lots of new updates. But is it right choice for you? The device is for everyone who likes taking great photos and videos and is interested in image editing. The AI-supported features offer you tonnes of new possibilities. And, thanks to the Super Actua display, you can edit photos in direct sunlight with no colour distortion. The phone is also for anyone interested in digital innovations and using AI in their everyday lives. Starting now, you can preorder the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL at swisscom.ch/google.
