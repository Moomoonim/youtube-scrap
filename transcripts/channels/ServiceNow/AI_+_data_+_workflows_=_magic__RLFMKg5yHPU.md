## 스크립트

Agentic AI really excites me because what it is is taking all of the discrete pieces of AI that have been developed over time, all of the discrete workflows that have been developed over time and orchestrate them together as a singular process. You can harness a tremendous amount of process insights as well as third party application insights right here in the Service Now platform and therefore you can drive action. Decades of digital transformation yielded us so much process automation. Every time the process runs, it generates data. For the most part, we have been sitting on this data like squirrels. Process planning makes sense of this data and figures out where are the inefficiencies across the processes. So, Raptor DB is purposebuilt for data and workflows on the Service Now platform. It is really a gamecher for us when we think of harnessing the best of our transaction processing capabilities as well as the analytics processing capabilities. Service Now has massive ambitions for what we can do for our customers and therefore the growth that we can create. The only way we get there is through deeprooted datadriven understanding of our customers, their journey and their growth path with us. so that we can make our customers successful and in turn make service now successful as a business. AI enabled means figuring out a growth path for every single of our customer. What is the next best action we need to do to help grow the business and also how do we unlock productivity across many different roads across the company and bringing it together that's our AI mission.
