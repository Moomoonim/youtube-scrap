## 스크립트

Hi! I’m Steven from the team at Slack,&nbsp;&nbsp; and I’m going to show you how&nbsp; to pause Slack notifications.&nbsp; During important meetings or when you&nbsp; just need some quiet time to concentrate,&nbsp;&nbsp; you can pause Slack notifications. Let’s say you have a customer presentation&nbsp;&nbsp; this afternoon, and you’d like to pause your&nbsp; notifications to finish your preparations. Simply, Click your profile picture.&nbsp; Hover over “Pause notifications,”&nbsp; and select the amount of time that&nbsp; you want to pause notifications. Let’s say two hours just to be safe! If you want to resume notifications&nbsp; sooner than that, hover over “Pause&nbsp;&nbsp; notifications” and click “Resume notifications” And for times when you routinely&nbsp; want notifications paused,&nbsp;&nbsp; you can set a notification schedule. To do this, click on your workspace name,&nbsp;&nbsp; open preferences, go to the notifications&nbsp; tab and visit notification schedule.&nbsp; And that’s it. Try pausing your&nbsp; notifications to enhance your focus time! Check out our other videos or&nbsp; visit slack.com/help to learn more. Thanks for watching!
