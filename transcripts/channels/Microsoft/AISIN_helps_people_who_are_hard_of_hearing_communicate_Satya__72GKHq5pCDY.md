## 스크립트

When I was two years old I lost my hearing due to an illness. I couldn't hear at all. So they taught me how there are sounds and voices in this world. I first met Ms. Kato after she began using YYSystem. She was deeply moved by the experience and ended up writing us a letter. She let us know that it had changed her life. The most difficult thing was not being able to have a conversation. In Japan, there are various regional dialects, with unique ways of speaking and unique expressions. There were many requests to convert users' voices into words and that's how MyEngine developed. We use Azure AI Foundry a lot. The main thing we use it for is to verify the logic used to correct statements and as a tool to check that items are being properly translated. We use the Azure Speech Service, in real time, for transcription. In addition, it is used to learn the speech of those who are hard of hearing to make customized speech models unique to those people. Thanks to the support of everyone, we are happy to say that our YYSystem's app has been downloaded 1.45 million times in Japan. I couldn't understand what my children and grandchildren were saying. Now I can. At my company they would be like, "Hey, Ms. Kato!" I didn't get it before. Now I can understand when they're calling me and I can communicate my thoughts. I was just an ordinary engineer but, since developing this app, now I feel that these users' voices are giving me energy and motivation to go on.
