I'm Jessica and I'll show you how to sign a document as a guest using a qualified electronic signature according to the Swiss standard. To do this, click the button on the document launcher. You will now be redirected to the document and can open it from within the document. You don't actually need to open the document in the opener. Here's the most important information about the signature in the image: Chris, when you want to add a visual signature or simply sign with the signature, then click through. Since this is a qualified electronic signature, it will appear that you need to link your identity. To do this, click on "Link identity" and you will be guided through the corresponding process . I'll explain exactly how this works in the video "signing K" and how it links to identity . In the video [music], Chris FR Boarding has already been completed. After identity verification, you can proceed directly with signing. However, if this hasn't been done yet, the process starts automatically in the Mycom app, and I'll explain how that works in video C. Registration [music] for signature release: a QR code will be shown to you, which you scan with your phone. You will be directed to Viscomb, and click "sign now" to release the signature. The signature is then added, and the process is complete. A PDF document is also available.
