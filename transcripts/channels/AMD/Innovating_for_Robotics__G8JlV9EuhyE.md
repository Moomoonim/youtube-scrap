## 스크립트

At AMD, we're driving exciting innovations in robotics from factory automation to advanced surgery. Our powerful and scalable embedded computing portfolio includes both X86 embedded processors and adaptive compute solutions that bring real-time control and AI to life. Our Ryzen Embedded processors powers factories and autonomous industrial robots with top-notch performance per watt. They also support simulations and digital twins for continuous optimization. Additionally, with AMD's adaptive computing products, like those in our Zynq and Versal portfolio, we're enabling vision AI, deterministic motor control, real-time networking, and sensor fusion. This means robots can quickly adapt to changes in their environment, making split-second decisions and operate with enhanced safety and security. At AMD, we're also deeply committed to open-source software. For robotics, we make it easy for customers to start building ROS 2-based systems using our modular and scalable Kria SOM platforms and leveraging the Kria robotics stack. The future of robotics will rely heavily on AI processing at the edge. In factories, AI-powered digital twins will boost efficiency, dexterity, and autonomy while predicting system failures before they happen. Additionally, physical AI, that is, AI interactions with the physical world, promises to transform robotics for applications ranging from industrial process optimization to healthcare and humanoid personal assistance. AI can revolutionize robotics by helping to make robots smarter, more versatile, and more capable. With what's called embodied AI, robots will learn through direct physical interactions with the environment around them. This learning can help make them safer and more efficient. Real-time sensor processing from AMD can accelerate ROS functions and help robots quickly adapt to new tasks and unexpected situations.
