## 스크립트

Our mission is to pioneer health and beauty to offer sustainable and life-changing dermatological solutions to all. We partner with health care professionals to solve skin issues for more than the 2 billion skin sufferers around the world. We also answer the specific aesthetic needs driven by the boom of beauty procedures. This is why our brands are among the most recommended brands by dermatologists in the world. Innovation. In our medical beauty market, innovation is key to push the boundaries of health and better address key consumers' unmet needs. 2022 was full of impactful innovations on many fronts. Acne, atopic prone skin, and UV protections. If I had to pick one, I would mention the game-changing UV Moon 400 solar product by La Roche-Posay. A breakthrough innovation that helps protect consumers from the number one cause of skin cancer, the sun. Complementarity of our four unique global brands, which perfectly address the key consumer quest for health, safety, and efficacy. La Roche-Posay and CeraVe answer pathological needs at different price ranges. On the professional aesthetic fronts, the newly acquired American brand Skinbetter Science perfectly complements Skin Ceuticals with its medical luxury positioning. Responsibility. Because, as the leader of medical beauty, it is our responsibility to develop sustainable dermatological solutions while also increasing access to skin health. In 2022, we started the rollout of our co-beauty score on our two biggest brands in Europe. This environmental and social labeling empowers consumers to make informed choices. I'm proud that 75% of La Roche-Posay and Vichy skin care products scored A or B. We also contributed to boost access to skin health through many partnerships with teleconsultation platforms and thanks to our collaboration with Galderma, the International Alliance for Global Health Dermatology, an education nonprofit which promotes access to skin health for everyone, everywhere. First, our anchoring dermatological beauty with products and services answering needs both on the pathological and aesthetic fronts. Second, our strengthened medical leadership. We now partner with 250,000 doctors. Our reach has increased by 13% versus last year. La Roche-Posay is the most recommended brand by dermatologists in the world. And in 2022, three of our brands ranked in the top four. Third, our geographic expansion at full speed. In 2022, all zones grew double digits. The division recorded an exceptional performance in the US and China, growing three and close to five times faster than their respective markets. Last, the digital amplification of our medical strategy. Our online medical advocacy superiority allows us to reach more consumers. La Roche-Posay and CeraVe are the top two most recommended skin care brands by doctor online. And e-commerce makes our product more easily available to consumers. In this channel, we gained significant shares in 2022. Well, beyond our outstanding growth, I'm so proud of our fantastic teams around the world. In an uncertain environment, they have been able to adapt with agility and resilience. They also showed creativity and a strong entrepreneurial spirit. They are behind our success.
