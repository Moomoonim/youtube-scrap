## 스크립트

I’m Victoria, I have been documenting my life and travels online now for 12 years. A road trip through the Scottish Highlands is something we have always had on the top of our bucket list. This is the week we finally made it happen. We have made it to the first stop of our Scottish road trip. We have driven four and half hours from Edinburgh to get here. All in the brand new fourth-generation Mercedes-Benz Marco Polo. It has four seats, a kitchenette, and it has two beds, technically one of which is in the retractable roof. So, we can store our food in here. We can cook in here. We can run water in here. This is like luxury glamping at its finest. This is Loch Lochy, and it is absolutely beautiful. The light is just coming down and hitting the lake, it is stunning. So, I wanted a second just to take it all in. The set up is complete. We have extended the roof. When you extend it, you can then push the inside up to give you a full standing space inside. Tonight’s dinner is going to be pasta, tuna, tomato mascarpone with cheese. This is the part of the road trip I have been most looking forward to: Glencoe. It has got the most scenic mountain scapes of the whole trip. So, we have come to the Glencoe Bistrot Center so we could look for some fudge and some tartan throws- just something typically Scottish. But we found one of the things that we really wanted to see on this trip: the Highland cows. Hi Mercedes! How can I help? Take us to the Three Sisters Viewpoint! Where do you want to go? Number one. Let’s go! The great thing about this road trip is that everything is quite close to each other on the map. You can do all the viewpoints in an hour’s journey. Another quick step off- literally, it is so beautiful here! You can just drive two minutes and then see another great spot. Stop two is the Meeting of Three Waters viewpoint. It’s like three different waterfalls coming together. Oh my gosh! Literally, the sun is just coming out, as we are walking to the wee white house. This could not be any more perfect! We landed at our final stop of the day: Kilchurn Castle. It is on the edge of Lake Awe, I think. Apparently, it was built in the mid-15th century, but now that it is owned privately, it is completely derelict and not even open. It is a landmark to come and have a look at. Imagine buying this and turning it into an incredible hotel- it is literally on the edge of this lake! Good morning it's the start of day three! We are on out way to Loch Lomond today. We had a fantastic night’s sleep we were next to this trickling stream, so you could just hear the water all night. Once again, I am in the driver’s seat as Alex is doing all the video footage as we drive around Scotland. It has been super comfortable! This is the updated new fourth-generation cockpit area; the seats are super comfortable! You’ve got such a huge windscreen to see everything, and then the interactive screen runs across the dashboard- you have everything at the touch of a button. Let’s get going for day three! We have been driving along the edge of Loch Lomond, and I saw a little turnoff and a little beach. So, we pulled off the side of the road. We found the most beautiful, secluded beach. Look how tranquil and beautiful this is! I googled where would be the best coffee-shop along Loch Lomond, and we were told this place. It's called The Coach House- and so far, it is pretty amazing! The cake fridge is absolutely brilliant! This is called a Stoacky; It is a type of sandwich barm which warms our hearts since a Wigan loves a good barm. Okay, we are on our way to our final stop of the trip. We are heading back to Edinburgh! It is an hour-and-a-half trip back from Loch Lomond, but we are going to make one final stop in Edinburgh at a Tartan Weaving Mill, an exhibition Center. We are walking up alongside Edinburgh Castle right now and are just going to make our way up to where this Tartan Weaving Mill is- maybe I might be encouraged to buy something tartan! This has to be one of the most enjoyable trips we have ever taken as a couple! The Mercedes V-Class Marco Polo was more than just a camper; this was a luxurious glamping experience on four wheels! There are so many internal features that made it fuss-free- super comfortable- and the little finishing touches like the yacht-deck-look flooring, the variety of storage areas for hiding away our luggage and the MBUX digitals screens that made navigating Scotland a total breeze. Then the MBAC allows you to control almost all camping functions of the vehicle- it was a compact home on-the-go: a brilliant travel companion and the perfect vehicle for everyday. We really hope you enjoyed our road trip through Scotland! There are so many more videos just like this available to watch on the Mercedes-Benz YouTube channel. Thank you so much for watching!
