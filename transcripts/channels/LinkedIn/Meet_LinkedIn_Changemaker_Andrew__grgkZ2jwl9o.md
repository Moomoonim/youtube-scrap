## 스크립트

I found myself being made redundant in 2019, completely out of the blue. It's a little bit like a grieving process, effectively, when you’re made redundant or you lose your job and you're not expecting to, so it was super, super tough. I've met lots of people that are suffering. And it's not just one person that's made redundant, it's a whole household, because it reverberates throughout families and throughout communities. Pretty quickly I started getting involved with some career coaching, helping people end their own job search misery and get hired quicker. Initially, I just started sharing content on LinkedIn to try and help people. So we set up a live broadcast called Job Search Hacks Live. We've been getting people from all over the world tune in live to that, which has just been tremendous. The community side has been incredible. It's less and less about me helping people with their job search, and more and more about people finding solidarity and connection with others who are also going through some kind of transition. LinkedIn has enabled me to get my message out to more people. Let's stop accepting job misery in return for job security.
