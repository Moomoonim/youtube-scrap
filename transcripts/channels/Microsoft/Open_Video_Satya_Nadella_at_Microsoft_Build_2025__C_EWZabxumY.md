## 스크립트

I think I started programming at maybe 15. I just couldn't pull myself away from these machines. I wanted to be a developer. I wanted to write code. I started creating my own video games. It sparked something in me. I started to really like programming. Becoming a programmer, it opened the world for me. I'm super excited about AI. That feeling of wonder that's the reason why I started developing software. I'm excited to see what we're going to build with such powerful tools. Programming with an agent is really empowering. It's like an extension of myself. It helps me go further. No project is too big, no idea is too big. It's magical. If you make artificial intelligence available to everyone to use, you can essentially make a superpower available to everyone. Knowing that my project is being used by people to make their lives better. To make an impact in the world. Solving the problems we want to solve. That's just the best feeling in the world. The ideas I have are limitless and endless. Everything that we do is empowering other people to make the world better.
