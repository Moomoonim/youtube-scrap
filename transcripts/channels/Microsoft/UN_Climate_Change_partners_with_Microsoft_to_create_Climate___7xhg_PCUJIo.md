## 스크립트

This new era of climate action is about turning ambition into real impact - accelerating implementation and spreading the vast benefits of climate action to people everywhere. Under the Paris Agreement's Enhanced Transparency Framework, countries share information on emissions, mitigation, adaptation, and support. Transparent climate data enables strong climate policies, guides investment, and builds trust. But with over 190 countries submitting climate reports in different formats, it hasn't always been easy to access, analyze, and compare information. That complexity has slowed learning and collaboration. That's why UN Climate Change has partnered with Microsoft, EY, and NEDAMCO Africa on the Climate Data Hub Project - a modern AI-powered platform designed to unify, simplify, and make climate data accessible to all. With its intuitive data-driven interface, users can explore information using simple queries, easily transforming raw data into actionable insights. For countries, the Climate Data Hub opens opportunities to share experiences, learn from one another, and accelerate collective progress. It's not just a data platform, it's a bridge between science, policy, and implementation. Policymakers can validate decisions, benchmark progress, and learn from peers. Asking, for example, "How have other countries supported renewable energy transitions?" and get clear, contextual answers drawn directly from national reports. Researchers can explore official data sets, generate tailored analyses, and strengthen their studies and models. The Climate Data Hub is a centralized, authoritative, global source of climate information - open to all, empowering everyone to turn climate insights into action. With the Climate Data Hub, we're making climate data transparent and accessible in a whole new way. You don't need to be a data scientist, you just need a question. So now, the question is yours. What will you discover? What will you change? What solutions will you shape?
