A smart home from IKEA: How to get started with DIRIGERA hub and the IKEA Home smart app To help you get started with your smart home ideas, we've put together four steps to guide you through the basics of setting up a smart home, and what the IKEA Home smart app can do. To start with, visit us online or any of our stores to find DIRIGERA hub and our wide range of smart products. The hub is the heart of a smart home as it enables you to connect and control your smart products in the IKEA Home smart app. The hub requires a Wi-Fi network to function, and connects to your router with the provided cable. You then connect the power cable to the hub and continue the installation on your mobile or tablet device. In the next step, your hub connects to the IKEA Home smart app. The app is available on App Store and on Google Play. Following simple steps in the app, the hub will be found and connected. And speaking of the IKEA Home smart app… Follow us to explore the main app features! After adding the hub and smart products to the app, the first tab you’ll see when opening the app is this: Home. Use the Home screen to sort your smart products by room. Quickly see what’s turned on or off, and the name of that track playing on repeat upstairs. Let’s tap over to the overview of all smart products, where you control individual products or groups. Dim the lights, lower blinds, mute speakers, or increase air purifier fan speed. Another useful feature is to save your favourite product combinations and settings as different scenes, and the right mood is just a tap away! Trigger scenes with schedules or manually in the app or with a shortcut button. Scenes can be used to schedule lights to be turned on when you're away, for the air purifier to clean the air before bedtime, to turn off all lights at once, or for the blinds to roll down to block out warm sunlight. Make your life at home a little bit more colourful, effortless, and a little bit smarter.
