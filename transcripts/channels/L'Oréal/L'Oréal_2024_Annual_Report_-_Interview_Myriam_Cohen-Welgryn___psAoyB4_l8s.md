## 스크립트

At L'Oreal Dermatological Beauty, we lead skin care innovation partnering with 310,000 health care professionals to provide life-changing products and services for all. First, innovation. Our powerhouse research has once again created life-changing innovations that have truly made a difference. This year, I will quote Melasyl. Our biggest ever brand innovation. It's multi-patented Melasyl molecule matches the efficacy of dermatological golden standard in hyperpigmentation while ensuring high tolerance. Then, gratitude. I want to thank all the health care professional with whom we create hand in hand innovations that change people's lives around the world. And thank you, of course, to the incredible L'Oreal Dermatological Beauty teams. Despite the turbulences across the world in 2024, their resilience and agility have made the difference. 2024 was another record year for the division. L'Oreal Dermatological Beauty achieved a plus 9.8% like-for-like growth, crossing the 7 billion euro mark, and reached a historic market share of 25.5%. Beauty tech is revolutionizing access to skin health, making expert diagnosis more accessible than ever. VICHY is at the forefront of this transformation with a revolutionary device for personalized scalp diagnosis, Scalp Consult pro, which is already launched in 11 market. Similarly, Skin Ceuticals is pushing the boundaries with its one-to-one pro service, providing virtual consultations with medical aesthetic professionals. Preparing for the world of tomorrow, we are not just selling products. We're providing solutions that empower consumers to own their skin health. I'm proud of our ongoing efforts to make beauty more and more inclusive. According to studies, only 15% of the cases presented in medical textbook show skin rich in melanin. This lack of representation in education and research creates knowledge gaps that can impact diagnoses and treatment. This is why we decided to launch the universal dermatology of all colors, an initiative to better understand the unique needs of people with pigmented skin and ensure access to the right skin care for all skin type. This initiative aims at improving research, education, and making sure that our innovations are developed to meet these specific needs. To name a few examples, La Roche-Posay's Melasyl 3 with its revolutionary Melasyl molecule delivers superior anti-pigmentation efficacy on all skin tones. And Vichy's Dercos shampoo is proven to be efficient on all hair types and scalp colors. Around 2 billion people suffer from skin issues around the world. Every year, this problem intensifies due to stress, an aging population, the impact of climate Struggling with skin health issues has a significant impact on people's daily lives. It is not just seeking healthier skin. It's a battle against stigmatization. And to make matters worse, there's a shortage of dermatologists making access to skin health difficult. That's why we are launching L'Oreal Act for Dermatology, a commitment of 20 million euros over the 5 years to further access to skin health worldwide through four transformative pillars. Knowledge, education, awareness, and sharing of skin health solutions. In 2024, to boost knowledge, L'Oreal launched with the ILDS the Global Access to Skin Health Observatory. This is the first benchmark study in 194 countries investigating the distribution of dermatologists, patient barriers to dermatological care, and access to surrogate providers in dermatological deserts.
