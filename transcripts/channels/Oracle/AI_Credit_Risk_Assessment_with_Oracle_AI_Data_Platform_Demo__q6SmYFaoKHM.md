## 스크립트

In the financial services industry, risk teams at fast-growing retail banks often spend hours manually pulling data from multiple systems to review credit applications. These teams need speed, control, and full auditability. That's exactly what Oracle AI data platform delivers. With AI data platform, teams work from a single platform, one place for both their data and their AI. Instead of juggling disconnected tools, they simply ask, "Show loan applications that need attention." Instantly, a prioritized list surfaces. They drill into a flagged customer and request a credit risk summary. In seconds, not hours, they get financials, risk indicators, policy checks, and approval authority, all in one view. When they ask why a loan is flagged, the agent provides a clear explanation, grounding every answer in the organization's internal credit policy documents, exactly the level of transparency regulators and auditors expect. Under the hood, AI data platform unifies structured data, unstructured documents, and AI models in a single governed environment. Raw data is ingested through governed pipelines and flows through a medallion architecture, ensuring data is clean, traceable, and audit-ready. Data scientists work in open-source notebooks to build and refine explainable machine learning models on trusted data. Job orchestration keeps everything running automatically, so when new data arrives, pipelines trigger end-to-end with no manual intervention. On the unstructured side, internal credit policy documents are indexed using retrieval augmented generation. When the agent responds, it pulls from the organization's most up-to-date policies, ensuring every answer is grounded, governed, and defensible. Access controls are defined directly within AI data platform for each team member, ensuring the right people have the right access to both data and AI resources at every step. The result? Faster decisions, full explainability, and enterprise-grade control. That's Oracle AI data platform.
