## 스크립트

You and your child both have an iPhone and you want to actively influence screen time and the content your child consumes In addition to clearly defined rules, you should also make sure that this works technically. To do this, you have to do two things. The first thing you need to do is create an Apple account for your child using your Apple account. Secondly, you can directly restrict screen time, apps, content and purchases afterwards. I'll show you step by step how to do it. To create a new account for your child, open the Settings app on your iPhone. Then tap "Family" under your name. Click the icon in the top right corner to add a member. Click Create Child Account. Now you can enter your child's personal details: surname, first name and date of birth. Press the blue button to confirm that you are of legal age. In addition, you have to enter the security code of the payment method and then click on "Done". Check the privacy policy for families and terms and conditions carefully now. If it's right for you, press "Accept" in the bottom right. Now you set the email address for your child. If it doesn't already have an address, you can create a new iCloud address. If there is already one, you can use it. Choose the address that works for you and your child, because you can't change it afterwards. Once you're sure, click Continue and Create Email. Now you need to choose the password for your child's Apple account. Make a strong password that you can both remember well. which you can both remember well. Store it in a password manager so it doesn't get lost. You can find out more about secure passwords under the link in the description. Click Continue. Now select the phone number that is needed for two-factor authentication when your child logs in with the Apple account on a new device. Either you take your mobile number or your child's mobile number. So, now the new Apple account is created for your child In a second step, I'll show you how you can shape settings based on content and screen time. I recommend that you do this with your child so that you have a common understanding of the attitudes. Click Continue to get started. Here you can restrict content that your child can see. Use the slider at the top to get recommendations from Apple about the settings. Nevertheless, go through the list point by point and make adjustments. Especially with web content, there is often a need for discussion. Click "Continue" to continue. There is also a new function called "Check for sensitive images". The iPhone recognizes sensitive content when receiving images, warns against it and offers helpful hints. Click "Continue" if you find it useful. Turn on Screen Removal Alert if you want your child to be alerted that they have the screen too close to their eyes. Determine afterwards when the mobile phone may not be used at all. I select the period over the night and then click on "Activate time-out". And now you have to create a code that can be used to remove parental controls. This means that if your child wants to be on YouTube a little longer, they can send you a request directly on their phone, which you can activate with this code. With this code, you can allow your child to use the app for either 15 minutes, an hour, or the whole day. However, you also have the option to decline the request. Pro tip: Choose a good password, for a few children it is an exciting challenge to find out the code to secretly get more screen time. Now choose whether you want to always confirm App Store purchases and whether you want to see your child's location. The feature can be useful, for example, to find your child's device in case it gets lost. In any case, we recommend informing your child if the tracking function is activated by the mobile phone. Nevertheless, children have the right to privacy. So your child's iPhone is ready. But remember: It is important that you accompany your child closely with their first mobile phone, especially at the beginning. You can find tips on how to do this on the page "The first mobile phone for my child" on Swisscom Campus. I'm Marcel and I wish you and your child a lot of fun with the new device.
