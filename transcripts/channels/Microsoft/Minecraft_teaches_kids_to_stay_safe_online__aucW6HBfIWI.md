## 스크립트

I've gotten text messages and they're like, “Your package is stuck somewhere. Enter your password and your username so we can fix it.” I've encountered malware on spam messaging. I went on this website, it said, “You’ve been hacked.” It was scary. Even kids now, like, as early as first grade, they are in a digital environment all the time. We need to put some tools in their hands that let them think outside the box and embrace that new technology. I like using Minecraft because it helps be creative and it teaches us stuff. So it's both parts. And I really like to be creative. Learning with Minecraft is definitely more visual. It helps picture things. The lessons and stuff on Minecraft help relate more to the real world, I feel like. I've had students that have actually encountered something outside of the school setting, and they said, “You know how we were talking about don't talk to strangers?” They're like, “I just had someone try to send me an email and I was able to just delete it.” I also want them to be inspired to be the next generation of people who are invested in the internet and keeping us all safe.
