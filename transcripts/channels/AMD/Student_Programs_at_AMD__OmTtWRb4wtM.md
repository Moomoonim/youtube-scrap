## 스크립트

So as an intern, I wasn't really doing “interny” stuff. I actually got to design components and pieces that were integrated into our platform systems. Intern projects are actually meaningful and impactful to the team. The team actually needs all of that and we can see the results, real time. That was amazing. I was back at school and Lisa held Turin up. I was like, “I-I held that! I touched that, I worked with that in the lab. Like, I have code that tested that.” Part of why I love being at AMD is leading this intern program, where interns are eager. Our community of leaders, as well, supports that. It's a priority for the company to hire top college talent. We're going to give you the resources you need because we try to give you that full time employee experience from day one. As somebody who's just starting their career, the culture here is perfect. Learning-oriented, growth-oriented, and no judgment. I can even reach out to anybody and set up a meeting and they'd be super happy to introduce me to what they're doing and what they are working on. I received the full time offer letter from my manager and I was stoked. I was so excited. I was ending up at a company that I loved and a team that I loved. For my experience, it was as smooth as it gets going from a college student to a full time employee. I think a lot of people kind of have that experience, that it's not just all of a sudden, you know, they're expecting so much more out of you. They expected a lot from day one and so by the time you're full time, you're ready for it.
