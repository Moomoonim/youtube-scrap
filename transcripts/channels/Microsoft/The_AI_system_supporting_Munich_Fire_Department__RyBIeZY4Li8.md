## 스크립트

The Munich fire department is helping citizens in the case of fire, injuries or illness. Our control center processes around 600,000 calls for emergencies and 120,000 calls for medical transport. Our emergency room treats up to 150 patients every day. Around 40 to 50 of these patients are admitted, while the rest are discharged as outpatients. In this case, we have to order a medical transport so that the patients can be picked up from our facility. If emergency calls come in alongside patient transport calls, the emergency calls are prioritized, and the patient transport calls have to wait. And that's the biggest hurdle, you have to sit in one place in the middle of our workday without actually having the time to do so. A lot of time is lost that we could be spending caring for patients, being there for them and doing our nursing work. AI was the right solution for us, medical transport calls can be handled better. It can answer the phone immediately. "What is the exact reason for the transport?" "The reason for the transfer is inpatient follow-up treatment after traumatic brain injury." "The medical transport is confirmed." We use Microsoft Azure products, primarily Microsoft Azure AI Foundry. We also use Microsoft Azure AI Speech with Voice HD, which allows us to conduct human-like dialogues. This means that we can register transports very quickly in an AI-supported setting, and the computer recognizes the gaps that still need to be filled and then asks the appropriate questions. I will have more time for the patients. It will reduce a lot of stress from being on hold and will make everyday work and workflows easier. We see great potential for scaling to be used throughout Germany, Europe, and maybe even worldwide.
