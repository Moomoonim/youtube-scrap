EV is opening a new world With EV, you are transporting not only people or goods, you are transporting energy And you can decide how you are going to use this energy You can use it just to drive your car but you can also give it back Of course, there is an interest for you as a customer You can reduce your bill of electricity, for example But there is also an interest for the world The world needs to better manage energy And EV is growing, and it is a tank of energy that you can use it any time It is also, through the value it brings for us a new type of business that we are not used to do with traditional cars
