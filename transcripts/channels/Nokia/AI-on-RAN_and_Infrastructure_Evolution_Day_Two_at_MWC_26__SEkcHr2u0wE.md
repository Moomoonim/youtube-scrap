## 스크립트

Day two has been quite busy here at the Nokia booth at Mobile World Congress. Today we've been focusing on automation and how we build smarter and more autonomous networks. We're showing level four autonomous operations across the network in the run and core to improve efficiency and customer experience. One of the highlights on our booth features a robotic dog demonstrating how advanced automation and AI can help with indoor radio network planning. Earlier we launched an Instagram poll asking you to help name him and we'll be revealing the winning selection later this week, so stay tuned. We were also joined by our futurist in residence Bernard Marr who visited the booth to explore our latest innovations and to share his top takeaways from both Nokia and Mobile World Congress so far. Throughout the day we welcomed more media and analysts onto the stand discussing this week's announcements as well as what's coming next for networks, AI, and digital infrastructure. A key announcement today was our network as code press release highlighting how Agnostic AI simplifies access to network capabilities for developers to create new services. We were joined by Subha, our Vice President of Sustainability. He told us about Nokia's ongoing initiatives to reduce environmental impact and gave a preview of our upcoming sustainability statement and more on that next week. The Nokia studio theater has been buzzing again with a full schedule of customer and partner speakers. If you're here at MWC, you should stop by to hear how we are shaping the future of connectivity together. And that's a wrap of day two here at the Nokia booth. Stay tuned for more updates. &gt;&gt; [music]
