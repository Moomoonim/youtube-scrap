## 스크립트

Good to see you all here today My name is Giovanny Arroba and I am the design director at Nissan's Global Design Center I can say with confidence that Max-Out embodies our next artistic stage for a closer look and to walk you through the design highlights I leave you in the good hands of design manager Lihao Yu my colleague who worked with the team to conceptualize this amazing vehicle Today, I'm so excited to bring Nissan’s Max-Out from virtual imagination to the physical reality and share our design vision To accelerate our momentum towards a more digitalized and electrified future We would like to introduce Shun the sense of speed into our design philosophy Japanese Futurism Shun means to run agile yet effortlessly Thanks to Nissan’s EV technology Vision that integrates with ASSB our designers are able to create this super sleek, low gravity futuristic profile One of the biggest challenge is to rethink what should future electric vehicle front end look like but still recognizable and iconic as a Nissan model The air tunnel created under the hood is super advanced lightweight, and efficient aerodynamic looking The digitalized V-motion creates an unmistakable front end as a future Nissan All the illumination and graphic motion is perfectly synchronized inside and outside and seamlessly connected with the mood of the user and the car The parabolic hologram wheels digitalized on the car is more vibrant and evocative Let's move to the rear end this wide display as a single surface is not only functional as the rear lamp but also as a portal for the future a new interface that shows the status of the car and the user communicating with the surroundings and expressing your personality The exterior color of Nissan Max-Out is called Metaverse Gray We wanted to make it look like an object that travels in a different space-time capturing the enormous momentum of an object that is moving in a digital landscape Nissan Max-Out is an open-top convertible and we want to create an experience that is borderless inside and out The two-seater Max-Out features the unique ability of morphing the seats flattening into the floor to provide more space when desired making it ideal for drivers with or without passengers You can even sit on the rear of the deck when the car is parked watching a movie or observing the stars with your partner The extremely simple interior construction and interface without any physical switches All the interaction is intuitive, the driver can purely focus on driving pleasure provided by our advanced e-4ORCE technology To provide users an experience by the seamless fusion between the virtual and real world The ultra-wide center display is the portal that merges everything together The digitalized landscape with three dimensional cinematic motion makes the user experience deeper more dynamic and more emotional This future comes from the past The future and the past are connected together in a time cycle and that's how trends repeat This fresh interior coordination is a futuristic rebirth of 90’s look and graphics There is a shift towards phygital on the Max-Out The half transparent steering wheel provides more openness and a seamless harmony boosting the connection between the screen and the user The key colors are nuance black and lime yellow and it evolves digital coordination to the next level Nissan Max-Out is our bold statement it shows the ambition of Nissan’s Design and how we are bravely moving towards the future
