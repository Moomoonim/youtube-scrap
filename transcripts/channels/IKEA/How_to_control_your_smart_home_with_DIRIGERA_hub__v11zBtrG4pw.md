## 스크립트

A smart home from IKEA: How to control your smart home with DIRIGERA hub The hub is the heart of a smart home as it enables you to connect and control your smart products in the IKEA Home smart app. All our smart products work independently, but adding them to the hub opens up new ways to control your smart home. The app offers full control of all smart products, but sometimes a remote control or a voice assistant is closer at hand. Products can also be managed by schedules or even motion. You control individual products, groups, rooms – or all at once. Follow us to learn how. Let’s start with remote controls, making it easy for anyone to turn on the lights, without the app on their phones, or change colour and dim lights. In the IKEA Home smart app, your own customised presets of products are saved as scenes and ready at your fingertips. Why not a scene to help you wind down before bedtime? Enjoy cosy lighting, relaxing tunes and no streetlight. With a motion sensor, activity in a room temporarily turns on light sources and wireless control outlets of your choice. If you want to use a voice assistant, you can connect to Amazon Alexa, Apple Home, or Google Home through integrations in the IKEA Home smart app. Make your life at home a little bit more colourful, effortless, and a little bit smarter.
