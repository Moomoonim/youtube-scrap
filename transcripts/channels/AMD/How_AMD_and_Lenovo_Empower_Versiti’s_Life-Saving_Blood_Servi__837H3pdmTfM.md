## 스크립트

Blood is life. It's a precious gift, and it literally saves lives. The demand for blood never ends, and that's what we at Versiti do. We are here serving our community with blood donation, science, research. We view Lenovo as a partner that can enable us to get to those solutions in a faster way. My name is Jack, and in 2014, at the age of 39, I was suddenly diagnosed with acute myeloid leukemia. I started to realize that there was a strong possibility that I was not going to live to see my kids grow up. From a treatment standpoint, Versiti has been involved from day one. Versiti is the fourth largest independent blood center in the United States and collects, processes, and distributes blood to hundreds of hospitals across the Midwest. We also do research that's groundbreaking and we hope decades from now will change patient care. Technology plays a critical role in that. It touches everything that we do and how fast we can do it, the quality with which we can do it. Lenovo provides all these great solutions that ensures that our donors and our patients have a great experience. 38% of the U.S. population is eligible to donate blood. Less than 5% does. One of our critical roles is to continue to find ways to inspire the U.S. population to donate at higher rates. As an organization, we do dozens of mobile collections throughout the Midwest every single day. Our IT team can't be everywhere all the time and so what we really look for are devices that we can rely upon. The Yoga 500Ws that we use out on mobile drives also are ruggedized so they stand up to the wear and tear during their life on the road. We've been looking at the Lenovo ThinkEdge servers as a possible option for our mobile collection efforts as well. They give us server-level performance while out on the road. Versiti is not only providing the acute treatments and therapy with their blood products, but also with all of our investment in research. A lot of what we do is obviously collect and then analyze data. So really at the heart of being able to do that well is being able to have optimized technology. And that information is going to contribute to the groundbreaking research that we do. We're using the Lenovo ThinkStation out at our Blood Research Institute. The ThinkStations are equipped with AMD Ryzen processors. Oftentimes they're working with instrumentation that require complex computational capability. And the processing power within the ThinkStations give our researchers the ability to perform those complex tasks. Well at the end of the day, Versiti is a very mission-driven organization. And it's not lost on me or my team that at the end of these computers is a patient or a patient's story. Without Versiti in their research, there was no chance to survive this. I did say, get me to 2020 so I can see my youngest graduate high school. You know, I just celebrated my 10th anniversary of a bone marrow transplant from a diagnosis of what would traditionally be considered an incurable cancer. They did the research. They found a way. I've gotten to see both boys graduate high school, both boys graduate college. When I think back to just the amount of support I needed from the hospitals and all their partners who helped them achieve what they do like Lenovo, it's just amazing to see how hard everybody works behind the scenes. Without everything that the staff at Versiti has done, those 10 years didn't exist for me. We like to think that we are a beacon of hope for the patients that are going to these hospitals. Lenovo is a great partner in our strategy to make sure that we're able to have that consistency where our communities can rely on us and depend on us when they need us the most.
