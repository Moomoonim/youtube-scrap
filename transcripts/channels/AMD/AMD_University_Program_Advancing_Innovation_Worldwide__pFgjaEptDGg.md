## 스크립트

As a leader in high performance and adaptive computing, AMD is committed to advancing innovation worldwide. As a vital part of our research and advanced development group, the AMD University Program streamlines collaboration between AMD and academic centers. AI is defining the next era of computing, and our collaboration with universities and research institutes is an important part of working together to solve the world's toughest problems. We are committed to creating an ecosystem that empowers these centers with AMD technology, enhances teaching and learning, while advancing the state-of-the-art research. With our AMD University partners, we're excited to relentlessly push forward innovative technologies.
