## 스크립트

Welcome to the hugging face task series. In this video, we'll take a look at the text to speech or DTS task. No discussion about audio tasks would be complete without talking about TDS. In text to speech, natural sounding speech is generated by a model giving a text input. Diaries such as ESPNet and TensorFlow DTS allow you to easily load any of the over 100 pre-trained models by the community. And this can even be expanded to multiple speaker and multiple languages. There are metrics such as log f-0 that allow us to evaluate the models by reflecting content similarities. But it's very challenging to determine the perceptual quality. So usually there are humans in the loop that perform qualitative evaluation. You can try it out right now in a space demo or by using the hosted widgets in each of the model repositories. For more information about DTS, check out the task pages.
