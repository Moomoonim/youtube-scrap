## 스크립트

A smart home from IKEA: How to expand your smart home over time with DIRIGERA hub The hub is the heart of a smart home as it enables you to connect and control your smart products in the IKEA Home smart app. With the hub, it’s easy for your smart home to grow over time. Maybe start with rooms where you spend most time, and explore smart lighting? Follow us as we show how to add an LED bulb to your smart home. When adding a new smart product like a speaker, air purifier, blind – or in this case, a smart LED bulb from IKEA, the IKEA Home smart app will guide you step-by-step. First, reset the bulb by toggling the main switch six times. The light will pulse then blink once it’s ready. The app continues to guide you as your DIRIGERA hub finds the bulb. Let the app know in which room the light is, and then you’re done! Connect the light to a remote control or use the app to change colour, dim …and turn off. Make your life at home a little bit more colourful, effortless, and a little bit smarter.
