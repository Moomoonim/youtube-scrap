## 스크립트

The idea behind the concept was really how do we change the experience of an electric sports car and create a unique experience for the user The car's got two modes GT mode when you're just cruising along and R mode when you're more engaged in the driving experience In addition to looking towards the future we also wanted to drop in some homages to to Nissan sports and racing heritage This project overall was such a dream for me I felt so many parallels with the driver I knew if I was excited then we were probably on the right track
