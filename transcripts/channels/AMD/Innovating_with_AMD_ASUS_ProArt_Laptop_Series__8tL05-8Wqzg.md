## 스크립트

For years, ASUS ProArt has excelled with top notch, high performance design, vibrant display, and exclusive features like ASUS Dial. This has made ProArt a popular choice for creative professionals. With our partnership with AMD and the power of AI, we've redesigned ProArt to empower not just creative professionals, but accessible to anyone with a story to tell. ASUS Dial Pad is one of the many things that makes these laptops stand out. To make content creation more accessible, we've included exclusive AI powered applications like MuseTree and StoryCube. The new AMD Ryzen AI processor with a dedicated NPU enhances the ProArts’ AI laptop experience. AMD has been a reliable partner for ASUS for years, powering some of our most powerful devices from gaming desktops to laptops and handhelds. So it only makes sense for us to replicate that same smooth and power efficient experience to our creator laptop series. This, for us, is where your story begins.
