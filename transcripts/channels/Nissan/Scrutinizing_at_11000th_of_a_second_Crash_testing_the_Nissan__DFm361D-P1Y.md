## 스크립트

At Nissan we are developing vehicles based on our Safety Shield concept: where the car protects the passengers so customers can drive with peace of mind Our tests have confirmed that the Ariya's body and cabin structure is able to absorb a variety of impacts offering protection in the event of a collision The crash tests we conduct include frontal side and rear impacts We also run tests that simulate when pedestrians are on the road We measure the effects of an impact on the vehicle body structure, seatbelts, airbags and on the occupants In total, more than 100 data points are evaluated These dummies are equipped with multiple sensors that record injury severity to the head and chest area that can be caused by the airbags and seatbelt As EV batteries are high-voltage we make sure that the battery pack is properly retained in the vehicle after a crash and that the electrolytes haven't leaked Since the Ariya will be sold in many markets with a variety of specifications we will conduct approximately 400 tests from the early stages of development to market launch During development of the latest Nissan LEAF we implemented stricter safety measures than required by regulations We are continuing this fundamental approach with the Ariya
