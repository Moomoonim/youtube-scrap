What are AI agent skills and&nbsp; why have they become an open&nbsp;&nbsp; standard adopted by practically&nbsp; every major AI coding platform? Well, because skills address a&nbsp; specific problem with agents. Now AI agents, they're pretty good reasoners and&nbsp;&nbsp; LLMs or large language models&nbsp; already know a lot of facts.
