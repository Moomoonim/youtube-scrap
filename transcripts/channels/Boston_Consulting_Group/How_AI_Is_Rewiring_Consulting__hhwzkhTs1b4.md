## 스크립트

My one piece of advice to all CEOs is to be courageous, to be bold, to be ambitious, to have a vision for what the end state could look like and all the possibilities that come with it. It will not be perfect, but the cost of doing nothing or doing little is much higher. I am Tawfik Hammoud, I'm BCG's chief client officer and chairman of our industry practices. This is the most fun I've had in 25 years. The amount of change, the velocity of that change, and the possibilities that that change brings to our clients and to society are just incredible. CEOs' expectations of BCG are clearly changing. What I'm hearing from CEOs is, how do I fundamentally rethink my company for an AI age? Every workflow, every customer interaction, every process, I need to think about how I make that AI first or AI native. In this AI environment, working shoulder to shoulder with our clients entails a much higher level of collaboration to generate impact. It's really being together, hands on keyboard, rethinking processes, rethinking workflows, imagining a different way of doing business, and that's what delivers our impact. BCG remains incredibly differentiated because it is building on its core competitive advantages, if you want, of strategic insights and clarity, a very collaborative, empathetic approach, I would say, to the client environment. But now, we've really added a very, very strong level of technical chops that allow us to see the impact all the way through. And so it's actually a lot of fun for our teams, and it's a lot of fun for our clients, because we get to invent a new way of doing business together.
