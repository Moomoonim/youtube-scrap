## 스크립트

What you're seeing today is our AMDers. The drive, the energy, the compassion, and the commitment to giving back to our communities. We really at AMD care deeply about the communities. Every chance I get, I want to volunteer. The food the volunteers are sorting today, we have to get it in a format that allows us to be able to distribute it to our neighbors. We rely on volunteers to make that happen. One million meals, that's something to go celebrate. Companies like AMD really allow us to get more work done across the city. We do give back events and programs all throughout the world. These solar lamps that we are building bring light to folks that do not have access to electricity. When our students get to come to a place like AMD to participate in activities like this that bring STEM subjects to life, it becomes real for them. The next generation of engineers are probably the most important thing we have for AMD. Ensuring that the next generation of STEM education and engineers come into the forefront of technology. We really believe that in order to be a good corporate citizen, we have to be a good local citizen as well. Our company mantra really is, together we advance.
