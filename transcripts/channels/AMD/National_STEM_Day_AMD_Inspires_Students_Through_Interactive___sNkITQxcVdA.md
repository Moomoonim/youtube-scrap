## 스크립트

The next generation of engineers are probably the most important thing we have for AMD. My vision is to stay in the STEM field because I really like working with computers, working with PCs. AMD has been helping me, like, stay on track with that. Coming in to, like, build the PCs with AMD, it was very impactful for us. We are a company that thrives in being part of the community. We do give back type of events and programs all throughout the world. This hands-on experience for our students teaches them that they can do hard things. It opens up the door to futures that they didn't even think were possible for them. It's really important for companies like AMD to be able to give back, to be able to provide that hands-on opportunity to make it seem real for the students so they know what their future will look like within this world. For them to see not just the processor, the graphics card, but all the components to make up the PC, you know as we enter into the age of AI, these are the products that enable that technology. So for them to get an experience of this, hands-on, hopefully that will give them some inspiration about what they like to see and do in the future.
