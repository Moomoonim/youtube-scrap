## 스크립트

Let's face it, change is hard, and sometimes it's easy to accept what works instead of what's right. Data Center Infrastructure Refresh can tempt IT operations teams into accepting the status quo, not realizing the hidden cost of inaction. The average age of a deployed server today is three to five years. With age comes increased maintenance costs, heightened security risks, and caps on performance. The server landscape five years ago was vastly different than today. There was minimal CPU competition, and technologies like AI, containers, and cloud-native existed only in a few data centers on the planet. AMD has driven transformative innovations that have changed the data center landscape with leadership performance solutions that are perfect for virtualized environments. With recent economic uncertainties, many businesses have postponed data center modernization initiatives, but that can actually be more costly than upgrading. For example, to deliver 380 VMs, one AMD EPYC 9654-based server can replace five Intel Xeon 8180 servers with an estimated 65% less power and 58% lower three-year TCO. So where's the real risk? New hardware initiatives or the status quo?
