## 스크립트

When I was a child in Igloolik and I would look up into the stars, I always had the feeling of anything is possible. Without the language, the culture does not exist. Culture and language work together. The language divide remains a challenge. AI brings an amazing opportunity to build a large language model for a low resource language. We want every community to leverage this technology, use it for their own case, and improve upon it. We didn't speak much English growing up. Syllabics was what we were taught. Now Inuktitut is in decline. The Microsoft tool will encourage more youth to learn Inuktitut. That relationship between youth and elders can really strengthen, preserving our language. Most of the education materials out there are in English. Now it can be translated into Inuktitut. It's not only education, it's health care, it's economic development. An overall quality of life improvement. It really opens up the world to to be able to communicate with others, globally. It’s a tool which gives me hope that Inuktitut surpasses me. And before I wasn't sure it would go to my grandchildren and great grandchildren. But now I know they'll have this. It will open a world of language that wasn't afforded to us before
