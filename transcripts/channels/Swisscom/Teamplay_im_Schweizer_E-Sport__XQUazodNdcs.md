The special thing about playing in the Swisscom Hero League is of course to compete with a great team over multiple weeks and hopefully to stand in the finals together. For me, playing together is - above all - thinking about your team and always having your mates in mind. Counter-Strike is a team game. It’s not for individuals. That’s how you win. Playing together is especially showing friendship on the battlefield, &lt;font color=#000000FF&gt;how you stick together, the team spirit&lt;/font&gt; But also the individual skill you can prove ingame. and SK Elite takes the Swisscom Hero League Final, they take the win! For me it's of course special to be part of Swisscom Hero League again because I was part of the first season and the goal to reach HeroFest is pretty cool as well. The opportunity to show what you can do and to show those who are interested in Counter-Strike how the competitive level can look like. alone against three and he does it!
