What was the vibe? So, like right before I met Dee? So in the year 2000, I've just finished my sophomore year. Sue Bird. We've just won a national championship. We're on top of the world. I'm not the vocal leader that you see, like pulling the team in for the huddle and yelling. At times, but that's not who I am naturally. But what I will do is put my arm around you and maybe guide you through some stuff. There was always this calmness about Sue.
