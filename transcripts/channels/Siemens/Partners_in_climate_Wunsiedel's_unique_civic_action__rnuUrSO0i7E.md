## 스크립트

Over the past 10 or 20 years, I believe that the town of Wunsiedel has had the very important task of paving the way for Marco Krasser with his and our Stadtwerke Wunsiedel, by which I mean that we had a lot of persuading to do. The people, citizens both regionally and nationally, in order to show that energy can be decentralized and that a municipal energy supply can also work on a decentralized basis. The citizens of Wunsiedel have taken all this on board, now living their Wunsiedel path. You can feel it everywhere in the town. I take particular pride in the citizens in Wunsiedel also when they talk about it, that Siemens is very committed here at the location. I have for example heard in a conversation with a Siemens employee that he also says, "We in Wunsiedel." - and that is something that also makes me proud as mayor. We notice one thing in Wunsiedel, of course: Due to this settlement of strong companies with regard to the energy future we create high quality jobs, jobs in all qualifications. And we also create a reversal of the rural exodus. So we perceive that through this development we are taking here, the interest of commercial companies becomes stronger to settle here again. And if we see now that the business park is basically at capacity and we see, that for another business park we are developing right now, companies are already there, who say, we want to settle here and we want to settle in the connection with the already existing energy companies in the energy park, then that shows quite clearly that we are on the right track here - and that Marco Krasser and also our predecessors in the City Council and in the administration together with Siemens, together with the companies that are involved, have set the right course. The citizens feel very clearly that in the region is on the upswing. People are coming, there is an influx again, we have quite a big demand for housing. The decentralized energy supply has a direct impact on citizens, on their own wallets, of course, a very direct effect. They have a reliability. Locally, the provision of general interest is simply secured - and it is secured at reliable prices. To shape energy locally as a citizen with my public utilities, with my own companies - that is, I think, already a direct and very positive effect directly on our citizens.
