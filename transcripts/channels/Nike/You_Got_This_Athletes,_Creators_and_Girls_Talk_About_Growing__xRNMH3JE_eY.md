## 스크립트

With a little belief, we can do anything. But growing up isn't easy. As we get bigger, we start to feel smaller. Our bodies change, our confidence shakes. We're thinking about stuff that makes our mind ache. Maybe we can't? Maybe we shouldn't? And then, we were made to believe we couldn't. But then when we move, we feel free. When we play sport, our confidence grows. When we skate, we fall and get back up. When we dance, we show ourselves who we are. And in those moments, our doubts fade away and we dare to believe we can do so much more. So, when that voice creeps in and says, "You can't," we need you in our corner saying, "You got this." You have the potential to do anything you put your mind to. Trust me.
