## 스크립트

When customers come to us, they say we want to reduce the total cost of ownership. It's our responsibility to reduce the power consumption as much as possible while delivering the performance that they're looking for. I think it's a win-win situation, both from a sustainability perspective, corporate responsibility perspective, but also from basically a corporation perspective. We're building things that people want and need. So energy efficiency really talks about how much power is consumed when you're doing electronics. Power is about really death by a thousand cuts. Every little piece of that chip consumes power. Good architects think about the entire system and how we make the entire system more useful. Agenic systems run on GPUs. These data centers are consuming massive amounts of power. These agents can also do things like improve the electricity distribution and be able to save electricity in your house. This is like an order of magnitude more impactful. It is going to fundamentally change how all of us live our everyday lives. The really cool thing about AMD, ideas come from the bottom and really smart people can come together and do something really cool. People that I work with here at AMD also share the same passion. When we combine that together, we can have a significant effect on the company and how we think about our global responsibility. No one individual can actually solve this. It isn't really about the ecosystem that you develop in the process.
