My grandfather is into politics and story to be told he has given away almost all the ancestral property that he had for the community. We've always made it a point in whatever level that we can uh to save and give back to the community. I think it became very subconscious for me to give back to the world. Either you touch lives or you transform lives and transforming lives resonated with all of us. We started in 2020 with just two villages in Telangana and now we are 17 villages that we've adopted as service now India. I love to take my kids along with me because seeing is believing. Every human being has core values and for me uh giving back to me is compassion and being able to do that it's just feels at a soul level purposeful. for the transformation of these lives over the 5 years have actually gotten the economic index um above 67 to 70% giving them boats for their livelihoods changed their life and what used to be their profit that they used to make is 5 rup piece per kg has now become tenfold with solar lamps. Kids in the evening are now able to meet and come out and play and eat together in the community hall because of the solar lights. Service Now is helping to fulfill a purpose together. We are able to come together as a team and give back to the society and transform these 17 villages that we've adopted over the past 5 years. Not only from a health and hygiene perspective, but also from transforming them um economically and doubling their incomes. It's very heartwarming to fulfill our purpose together.
