## 스크립트

So, here’s the pitch. The Siemens Xcelerator portfolio makes digitalizing your business easier. It includes a range of connected hardware, software, services and open APIs. You can do things like decarbonize your building or boost the productivity of your factory. It’s that broad. You can combine offerings from marketplace sellers and Siemens with your setup and share data between them. So you’re never tied to one vendor. Scaling up is also seamless. Say you’ve optimized your production and now want to automate more of your operation. Well, you can. You can even customize functionalities alone or with our ecosystem community. Plus, you can also use the portfolio as a service. So you get the latest tech when you need it, but only pay for what you use. Plus, Siemens can make it work that new products will work with your existing setup. Automatic updates are also included. In short, the Siemens Xcelerator portfolio offers a range of tools for an easier, faster, and scalable digital transformation.
