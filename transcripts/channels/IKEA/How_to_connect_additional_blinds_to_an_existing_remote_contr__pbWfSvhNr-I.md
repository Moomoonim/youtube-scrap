## 스크립트

KADRILJ FYRTUR TREDANSEN PRAKTLYSING How to connect additional blinds to an existing remote control Each blind kit includes a pre-paired signal repeater and remote control. This means they’re already connected and ready to use out of the box. You can choose to skip this and connect to an existing blinds remote and signal repeater. Step 1: Unscrew the back of the remote you want to use. Leave the back open for now; we’ll come back to this in steps 3 and 4. Step 2: Open the hatch on the blind you want to add and slide the battery pack into place. Step 3: Quickly press both buttons on the frame to awaken the blind. Hold the remote 2cm from the pairing icon on the blind you want it to connect to. Step 4: Press and hold the pairing button on the existing blinds remote for 20 seconds. The blind will roll slightly down and up again when pairing is complete. You can connect up to 10 blinds to one remote by repeating steps 2-4 for each new blind.
