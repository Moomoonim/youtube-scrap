## 스크립트

The geopolitical energy disruptions we're living through aren't isolated shocks. They're a pattern. Whether it's energy sources, supply chains, or technology investment, the strategy and decisions you make today will impact the coming decades and shape your dependencies and how you take action. Our four scenarios for 2050 are based on extensive data analysis and show how different the future could be. But across every scenario, energy and the energy transition set the parameters of our future. When you look to the long term, how should you make decisions today? The priority is about which low-carbon technologies to invest in and where you locate those technologies. How you secure your value chains as a company or as a nation. Which energy sources give you resilience, not just efficiency. And how you position for a world where climate risks could overload the financial systems that businesses rely on. These questions can't be tackled alone and will require the private sector, regulators, and governments to work together to develop answers. And it's important to get started. Our report shows the future is closer than we think. Explore each scenario in our full video on YouTube or read the report at BCG.com.
