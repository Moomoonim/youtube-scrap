## 스크립트

We know that one of the barriers for EV adoption globally is the anxiety over long distance travel. We looked at how combustion drivers use their cars globally, and the average time at a gas station is around 14 minutes maybe enough to fill up or grab a coffee, and we wanted to deliver a similar convenient, seamless, quick charging experience. This all-new LEAF can recharge 250 kilometers in just 14 minutes. Customers don’t want to be worried, so that’s why everything is seamlessly connected. That’s the key. Intelligent Route Planner integrates with Google built-in to identify charging locations along the road. So drivers can be sure they can cover even long journeys with confidence. We now have navigation-linked battery conditioning. Which considers the status of the road based on the navigation input and adjusts accordingly. This optimizes battery cooling based on the predicted load of the route conserving energy to increase driving range. We also included user-friendly features like plug and charge for customers in the U.S. Owners just need to connect and the billing is managed automatically ensuring it is seamless. Then of course, we have EV lifestyle benefits. V2X, which I think is really important for our future as it supports not only the energy balance between supply and demand, but also supports society to utilize renewable energy. Vehicle-to-Load, with up to two outlets inside the cabin and one accessible outside with an accessory connector. This EV is not just a car, it supports customers and communities with energy needs. We set out to deliver lifestyle-enhancing technology appealing to both existing EV owners and those seeking to move from a combustion engine vehicle to their first EV. Delivering both in-vehicle and broader lifestyle benefits.
