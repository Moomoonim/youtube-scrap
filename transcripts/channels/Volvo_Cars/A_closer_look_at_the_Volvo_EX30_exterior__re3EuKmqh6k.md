## 스크립트

This is the EX30. A small SUV doing big Volvo things. The fully electric EX30 is our smallest ever SUV. It's designed to have the smallest carbon footprint of any Volvo to date. The EX30 distills our design values into a smaller and concentrated package. At first glance, you're met with a small SUV that has the presence of a powerful, large SUV. Through the exterior we have a long wheelbase, large wheels and equal overhangs. This helps to bring balance and refinement to the exterior design. Throughout the EX30, we have about 25 per cent recycled aluminium as well as 17 per cent recycled steel and plastics, and those can be visually seen throughout the lower part of the vehicle. Aerodynamics is of course very important for the efficiency of an electric vehicle. But to draw attention to the wheel design in particular with that in mind, on the EX30 we have a range from 18-inch, 19-inch and 20-inch wheels, and they all come with aerodynamic inserts, which is best for aerodynamic flow and efficiency around the wheel. The EX30 is a small SUV with tons of character. To play upon the graphic design relationship on the architecture of the exterior, we've added an onyx black roof. Now this black roof interlocks the car with the pillar in the front and the pillar in the rear. The cantrail is painted, combined with the glass roof and it all ends in an architecture that supports the roof. So we have a strong C pillar and a great spot for the nameplate EX30. The EX30 celebrates our vertical tail lamp heritage. First introduced in EX90, we have split the lamps upper and lower with body colour in between. The EX30 also carries its own rendition of Thor's hammer. Smaller size, efficient and symmetrical. This is called an air curtain. It allows and enables the air to flow efficiently round such a short front end and open up into the wheel arch area. As you approach the EX30, you are met with a welcome sequence. The car wakes up and there is an animation that plays. This helps to let you know that it's ready to go when you step inside. The EX30 from an exterior standpoint has five different colours. And a new colour for us: Moss Yellow. The colour was inspired from lichen that we see growing on the rocks on our coast here in west Sweden. Through use of colour, textile and pattern we are able to add that visual punch to the EX30. As designers, we take inspiration from the world around us. This can be anything from nature to product design – even architecture and graphic design. For the EX30, we've taken specific helmet inspiration from sci-fi design. More specifically Star Wars, and the Boba Fett helmet used in these movies. This has inspired the front-end design with its graphical impression and proud confidence.
