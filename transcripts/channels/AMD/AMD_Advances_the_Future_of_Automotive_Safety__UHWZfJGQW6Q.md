## 스크립트

In today's AI enhanced vehicles, ADAS systems are enhancing safety and driver experience. They use data from various sensors like cameras, radar and LiDAR. and process and analyze them to enable features such as collision avoidance, automated driving parking assist, driver and passenger monitoring, object and sign detection and identification, and so on. Now, looking in the future. Real time sensing and communication will be vital for orchestrating ADAS seamlessly across interconnected vehicles and roadway infrastructure. Automakers are challenged to harness increased processing performance from AI compute while conserving vehicle battery power. Additionally, automotive safety standards require high reliability in harsh driving conditions, affecting thermal and packaging requirements. Flexible processing solutions are essential to accommodating ADAS platforms as they evolve. AMD offers scalable solutions to balance performance, power, and cost with a broad range of integration options. AMD technologies have been deployed in the automotive industry for more than 15 years, and our ADAS innovation continues to flourish.
