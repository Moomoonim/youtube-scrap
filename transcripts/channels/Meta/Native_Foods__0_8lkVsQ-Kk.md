## 스크립트

- Food is the greatest connector in the world. We all eat. Most of us love to eat. Some crazy people like me love to cook, too. [Speaking native language] Hello, welcome. My name is Mindy, and I am the chef and owner of Karkalla Byron Bay. I’m also a proud Widjabul Wia-bul woman from the Bundjalung Nation. My desire is to connect people to our First Nations’ culture through food. Instagram gives us an opportunity to connect people onto a huge, global audience with these unique and rare foods. —Amazingly nutritious, super high in antioxidants. And if you look really closely, it's got all these fine hairs, Also acts as a natural insecticide— They’re visually beautiful, you can see them growing in nature. I really want people to understand the connection around these ingredients, the connection to our environment, and realize that nothing operates in isolation. I'm really inspired by nature. When I'm out on country and I discover some beautiful native food, because it’s growing all around us, my desire is just to connect people with it, and to spread that joy and that love. —It literally grows in the sand dunes. —The ocean's version of asparagus, and you cook it in very much the same way— Karkalla is a real expression of our culture. You're going to see ancient ingredients created into modern dining. It's an immersive experience. It will be the smells, the sights, the flavors and the experience of country. I always say, native food is an important part of our past, it’s going to be vital for our future. I hope that all Australians and everyone abroad can fall in love with these native ingredients, because for me, to experience a culture, you need to experience its food. And my hope is that if people experience our food, they'll experience our culture, they’ll fall in love with it a little bit more, appreciate it, and then join us as allies to protect our country, our jagun, for future generations.
