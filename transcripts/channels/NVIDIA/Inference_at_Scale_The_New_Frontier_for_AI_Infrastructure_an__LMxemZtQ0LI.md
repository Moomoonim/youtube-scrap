## 스크립트

From chat bots to streaming service recommendations, nearly every AI interaction depends on inference the instant a model makes a real-time prediction. AI has evolved far beyond simple tasks like object detection or content generation. Today, it understands context, strategizes, and continuously refineses its responses. This dynamic process known as AI reasoning makes models smarter, more adaptable, and invaluable across industries. Traditional AI models react fast but often miss the bigger picture. They recognize patterns but struggle with complex reasoning. Large language models with AI reasoning capabilities, however, can break down intricate tasks and deliver smarter, more contextaware solutions. Take event planning for example. A standard AI might generate a wedding seating chart based on available seats but overlooks family dynamics or guest preferences. In contrast, an LLM with advanced reasoning can analyze thousands more tokens during inference, crafting a seating arrangement that keeps the peace, avoiding awkward encounters and ensuring a smooth event. This shift from basic pattern recognition to structured reasoning unlocks AI's ability to solve real world challenges. Achieving this level of intelligence requires massive computational power. AI reasoning scales dynamically, applying more compute to complex tasks through iterative inference steps for higher accuracy. This process called test time scaling can demand over a 100 times the compute of standard inference. This demand during inference makes optimization more critical than ever. And it's not a simple task. Effective inference optimization balances speed, accuracy, cost, and user experience. High throughput can reduce responsiveness while prioritizing interactivity over throughput compromises revenue. Balancing these axes of inference optimization demands a fungeable AI infrastructure to scale and deploy AI without trade-offs during inference. Meeting this challenge demands a new kind of AI infrastructure, the AI factory. Designed to power the entire AI life cycle, it seamlessly handles everything from data ingestion to large-scale inference. By combining high performance computing, high-speed networking, and optimized software, AI factories manufacture intelligence at scale, transforming raw data into powerful AIdriven insights. NVIDIA's full stack AI inference platform and Blackwell architecture are at the core of this AI factory evolution. Scaling beyond Moore's law requires new energyefficient designs such as silicon photonix. Millions of GPUs drive AI reasoning where even small efficiency gains save megawatts of power. With Blackwell, a 1 megawatt AI factory delivers up to 50 times more output than earlier architectures. Managing these workloads at scale is complex. That's why Nvidia developed Dynamo, the AI facto's operating system. Open source and compatible with leading community frameworks including NVIDIA Tensor RT LLM, PyTorch, SG Lang, and VLM. It optimizes everything from reasoning models to large-scale inference. With full support for open- source models, this framework empowers enterprises, startups, and researchers to refine AI in a fastchanging ecosystem. AI factories purpose-built for AI reasoning models and inference will drive breakthroughs across finance, healthcare, and automation, powering the next generation of intelligent applications.
