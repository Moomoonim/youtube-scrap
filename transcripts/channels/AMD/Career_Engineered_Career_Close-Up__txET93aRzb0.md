## 스크립트

I like to take macro photography. Taking photos where things that close up. I do see some similarity between photography and daily work. I'm an architect in AMD and I'm working in the multimedia hub. It's fun when you only see that tiny portion of the picture and you can able to see all the details. Just like at work, sometimes when we solve a problem, we have to look at very microarchitecture of things. Working in AMD is a very good experience for me. I was mentored by a lot of great, talented engineers. And having different perspective, it’s very important. I've been working in AMD over 20 years and within that 20 years I've been in multiple roles. So whenever I move from one role to another role, sometimes it's scary, but it's also excited because you're challenging yourself to work on those new problems. My advice to a younger engineer would be: stay focused and also keep open mind. Looking at things at different angle will often give you different ideas and innovations. It's actually wonderful when you view the world through the lens and through different angles.
