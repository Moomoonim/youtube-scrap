## 스크립트

Imagine you're getting a new car, you can get gas, electric, hybrid, but what about e-POWER? With e-POWER, you get the excitement of driving an EV and the convenience of a gas car. Here's how it works: An electric motor moves the wheels directly, so it's as responsive as an EV, but there's a compact gas engine that powers that electric motor. Okay, but what does that actually change? More efficiency versus a gas car, long and quiet drives, and instant acceleration Oh, you thought that was it? You also get e-Pedal. Step to accelerate, release to slow down, It's normally just for EVs, but you also get it with e-POWER. e-POWER gives you the best of everything.
