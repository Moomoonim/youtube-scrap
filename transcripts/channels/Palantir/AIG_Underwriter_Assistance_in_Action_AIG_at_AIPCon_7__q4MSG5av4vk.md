Good morning. My name is Jim Young and I'm the&nbsp; global chief data officer at AIG and I'm delighted&nbsp;&nbsp; to be with you today. My role puts me at the&nbsp; intersection of data technology and transformation&nbsp;&nbsp; and I'm excited to present today what our team&nbsp; have built with the assistance of partners&nbsp;&nbsp; to transform a core function of our business,&nbsp; commercial underwriting. I'm sure many of you have&nbsp;&nbsp; heard of AIG. We're a leading global insurance&nbsp; company. We help businesses and individuals&nbsp;&nbsp; in over 200 countries and jurisdictions protect&nbsp; what matters most by managing risk and providing&nbsp;&nbsp; insurance solutions. At the core of this&nbsp; is underwriting. As you can see, 70% of our&nbsp;&nbsp; business is commercial underwriting. Underwriting&nbsp; is the process where we assess risk and determine&nbsp;&nbsp; coverage. It's where we decide, do we insure&nbsp; this business and what's the right coverage?&nbsp;&nbsp; It's one of the most critical and complex&nbsp; decisions we make and it was stuck in a process&nbsp;&nbsp; that hadn't really changed for 50 years. AIG has&nbsp; leaned head first into AI in close collaboration&nbsp;&nbsp; with partners like Palunteer and in meeting that&nbsp; moment we are rebuilding our underwriting process&nbsp;&nbsp; on AIG's ontology with generative AI logic&nbsp; agents and automation reducing the length of&nbsp;&nbsp; aspects of our underwriting process from one&nbsp; month down to one day. The insurance industry&nbsp;&nbsp; in the United States is primarily regulated at&nbsp; the state level rather than the federal level.&nbsp;&nbsp; This has contributed to a lack of uniform data&nbsp; standards which can lead to data discrepancies&nbsp;&nbsp; across brokers, malformed PDF documents and&nbsp; Excel files and legacy technologies which require&nbsp;&nbsp; complex data extraction. So let me introduce you&nbsp; to the world of the underwriter. Due to disparit&nbsp;&nbsp; often unstructured nature of the data that the&nbsp; underwriters work with, inefficiencies have&nbsp;&nbsp; existed in this process for decades and these have&nbsp; been obstacles to scaling growth. Underwriters&nbsp;&nbsp; work through large amounts of data across&nbsp; multiple sources to help them assess risk and&nbsp;&nbsp; determine coverage. This data comes from brokers,&nbsp; from clients, and from industry data sources,&nbsp;&nbsp; all in different formats, usually requiring manual&nbsp; review. So typically an underwriter would have&nbsp;&nbsp; to review five to 10 different complex documents&nbsp; comprising up to 100 pages. Retrieving, reviewing,&nbsp;&nbsp; and evaluating this data amounts to weeks of&nbsp; work per submission. So the result of this highly&nbsp;&nbsp; manual errorprone process is that it takes up to&nbsp; three to four weeks for an underwriter to complete&nbsp;&nbsp; a submission. This cycle time makes it nearly&nbsp; impossible for us to get to every submission&nbsp;&nbsp; we receive. And most importantly that speed is&nbsp; critical to our business. Our competitors are also&nbsp;&nbsp; quoting for the same opportunities. So winning&nbsp; the race means winning the client. So we have&nbsp;&nbsp; to move faster without sacrificing the quality of&nbsp; our execution. And this is the mission our team&nbsp;&nbsp; has undertaken with partners like Palunteer. Our&nbsp; objective is simple. Accelerate growth at scale&nbsp;&nbsp; with rapid review at maximum quality for every&nbsp; submission we receive. This wasn't a question&nbsp;&nbsp; of replacing the operator. Our underwriters have&nbsp; valuable expertise and years worth of experience&nbsp;&nbsp; that give a gives AIG its edge. The issue was&nbsp; that they were spending 90% of their valuable&nbsp;&nbsp; time on retrieval, extraction, and manual entry&nbsp; of data, distracting them from what matters most.&nbsp;&nbsp; Powered by AIG's ontology, underwriter&nbsp; assistance mitigates these repetitive processes,&nbsp;&nbsp; deploying automations to refocus the underwriter&nbsp; on the actions and decisions that uniquely require&nbsp;&nbsp; their expertise. Core to this mission, we've&nbsp; created a framework of five key principles to&nbsp;&nbsp; maintain a disciplined approach to applying Genai&nbsp; to our underwriting process. First, we targeted a&nbsp;&nbsp; single Gen AI use case. We are focused on solving&nbsp; a real core immediate business challenge versus&nbsp;&nbsp; experimenting with Genai in non-core areas of&nbsp; the company. Second, we co-created the solution&nbsp;&nbsp; with our business teams. We wanted to ensure&nbsp; the solution we built aligned with realworld&nbsp;&nbsp; workflows. So, we collaborated with various&nbsp; business units in the company to create a tailored&nbsp;&nbsp; solution. Third, we adopted a human in the loop&nbsp; philosophy. The underwriters at the heart of our&nbsp;&nbsp; business and genai strategy. They are the key&nbsp; decision maker and we created a tool to enhance&nbsp;&nbsp; their human expertise not replace them. Fourth,&nbsp; we adopted an agentic modular architecture. This&nbsp;&nbsp; allows us to both easily integrate and adopt new&nbsp; technology solutions because as we all know this&nbsp;&nbsp; technology and the models are rapidly developing&nbsp; and we wanted to create a foundation that would&nbsp;&nbsp; allow us to adapt and change as the regulatory&nbsp; and technology landscapes continue to evolve. And&nbsp;&nbsp; finally, we measure what matters. Our investments&nbsp; in these new tools are measured against predefined&nbsp;&nbsp; metrics. Underwriter assistance delivers three&nbsp; main capabilities. First is ingestion. So we&nbsp;&nbsp; automate the extraction of roughly 100 attributes&nbsp; that drive the underwriter's decision time&nbsp;&nbsp; from hundreds of data sources and files, PDFs,&nbsp; databases, Excel files, and more. And this turns&nbsp;&nbsp; days and weeks of underwriter work to minutes,&nbsp; allowing us to feasibly process thousands of&nbsp;&nbsp; submissions in parallel. Second is augmentation.&nbsp; Tools in our ontology enable us to automatically&nbsp;&nbsp; search across internal and external data sources&nbsp; for relevant context and enrich and validate the&nbsp;&nbsp; content of the submission information. And&nbsp; then finally, prioritization. We provide the&nbsp;&nbsp; underwriter additional information to assist them&nbsp; in prioritizing their valuable time on incoming submissions. So, let me bring to life what&nbsp; I've been talking about through a real example.&nbsp;&nbsp; Let's say underwriter assistance is looking for a&nbsp; client's total 2024 revenue, which is one of the&nbsp;&nbsp; roughly 100 data fields we need for underwriting.&nbsp; In our RAG framework, we would generate a prompt&nbsp;&nbsp; in a query for total revenue. The query then&nbsp; returns relevant context, in this case, the 2024&nbsp;&nbsp; income statement, thereby limiting the vectors&nbsp; the large language model can actually search&nbsp;&nbsp; for the answer. Next, we send both the prompt and&nbsp; the relevant context to the large language model.&nbsp;&nbsp; The large language model then generates a response&nbsp; including the source document chunk from which the&nbsp;&nbsp; 2024 revenue was found which is then reviewed,&nbsp; confirmed or corrected by our underwriter or as&nbsp;&nbsp; we call them the human in the loop. Therefore,&nbsp; back in the seat of the underwriter, this&nbsp;&nbsp; approach substantially minimizes hallucinations by&nbsp; limiting the source context for the large language&nbsp;&nbsp; model. It also drives better efficiency&nbsp; in terms of response time and token cost.&nbsp;&nbsp; So with the assistance of Palunteer and others,&nbsp; we built an AI enabled system for the next era of AIG. I'm incredibly proud of what our team has&nbsp; accomplished in deploying underwriter assistance.&nbsp;&nbsp; To recap, we were successful and we are expanding&nbsp; the implementation to other areas of our North&nbsp;&nbsp; America business. We demonstrated a three to&nbsp; four-week underwriting timeline review for each&nbsp;&nbsp; submission could be reduced down to less than&nbsp; one day. With additional underwriter capacity,&nbsp;&nbsp; this unlock can enable AIG to review 100%&nbsp; of the relevant submissions we received. We&nbsp;&nbsp; consistently achieved over a 90% data&nbsp; quality accuracy rate compared to our&nbsp;&nbsp; manual input process. And critically, our bind&nbsp; to submit ratio in our pilot actually increased,&nbsp;&nbsp; indicating that organic growth is possible&nbsp; without any increase whatsoever in submission&nbsp;&nbsp; flow.
