## 스크립트

As cloud applications continue to ramp up&nbsp; their requests-per-second, and high-performance&nbsp;&nbsp; computing applications increase their working-set&nbsp; sizes, the demand for scalable solutions is more&nbsp;&nbsp; pressing than ever. Nsight Systems is NVIDIA's&nbsp; comprehensive performance analysis tool. Today,&nbsp;&nbsp; Nsight Systems is evolving to meet the challenges&nbsp; of AI applications scaling out across data&nbsp;&nbsp; centers and clouds. We're excited to introduce new&nbsp; features aimed at assisting application developers&nbsp;&nbsp; with microservices. Nsight Systems' multi-node&nbsp; performance analysis is enabled by scripts,&nbsp;&nbsp; called recipes, that triage data to help you see&nbsp; and understand application performance. A recipe's&nbsp;&nbsp; output is visualized with a Jupyter notebook&nbsp; integration. This guides you to where optimization&nbsp;&nbsp; is needed, and what the likely culprit is. Recipes&nbsp; help you inspect things like GPU utilization, CUDA&nbsp;&nbsp; kernel time, and now networking! Communications&nbsp; are a critical part of scaling out applications,&nbsp;&nbsp; so we're introducing new analysis recipes designed&nbsp; to help users understand how compute cold-spots&nbsp;&nbsp; relate to communication. Nsight Systems can now&nbsp; generate multi-node heatmaps to show InfiniBand&nbsp;&nbsp; congestion, InfiniBand and ethernet throughput,&nbsp; NVLink throughputs, and overlapped compute and&nbsp;&nbsp; networking. We've also enhanced profiling support&nbsp; for popular container systems like Kubernetes and&nbsp;&nbsp; Docker. This includes comprehensive support for&nbsp; CSP Kubernetes services -- including from Amazon,&nbsp;&nbsp; Azure, Oracle, and Google. And to meet you where&nbsp; you are writing code, server development is&nbsp;&nbsp; enabled by a remote GUI streaming container.&nbsp; This allows you to view reports remotely,&nbsp;&nbsp; rather than needing to copy them back to your PC&nbsp; or Laptop. And integration with Jupyter software&nbsp;&nbsp; is expanding even further. Nsight Systems now&nbsp; integrates with JupyterLab, allowing you to&nbsp;&nbsp; profile code within individual cells. You can also&nbsp; view textual results, such as statistics, directly&nbsp;&nbsp; in JupyterLab. Timeline reports can launch the&nbsp; remote GUI streaming container with a single&nbsp;&nbsp; click, seamlessly opening in a new tab. Modern&nbsp; day computing is defined by GPU-acceleration and&nbsp;&nbsp; AI. Scaling applications across multiple nodes and&nbsp; on cloud infrastructure is crucial to meet these&nbsp;&nbsp; new demands. Nsight Systems has evolved to help&nbsp; you build applications in the new landscape of&nbsp;&nbsp; computing -- whether on-premises, or in the cloud.&nbsp; Learn more and get started at developer.nvidia.com
