After scoping and building a prototype, the next step in the AI application development workflow is iterate. Repeated rigorous evaluation and optimization. A robust evaluations regimen is the cornerstone of a productionready AI application. Because LLMs, the engine behind AI applications, are non-deterministic and therefore unpredictable, building an application capable of generating consistent output, is a critical prerequisite to confident deployment into production. This challenge is one reason why AI applications are easy to demo but hard to productionize. A thorough AI application evaluation process plays effectively the same role as unit testing applied to more traditional nonLMbased applications. But unlike unit tests, you don't have the luxury of definitive right or wrong results. Weave offers straightforward, easy to implement AI application performance evaluations by tracking every single data point and effectively organizing and visualizing all relevant inputs, outputs, code, and metadata. Whether optimizing your application via fine-tuning, experimenting with different rag content or prompts, or changing LLMs and API call configuration settings, weave allows you to measure the impact of every update across multiple dimensions, including accuracy, latency, cost, safety, and user experience. Improved performance leads to more consistent and desirable results which in turn lead to greater confidence in your AI application.
