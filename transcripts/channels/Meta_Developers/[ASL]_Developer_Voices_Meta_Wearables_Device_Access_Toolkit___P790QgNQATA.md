[Music] Hi, my name is Connor Griffith and I'm a member of the wearables content team. As we have heard today, the Metawareables device access toolkit will enable developers to unlock novel use cases for AI glasses. This toolkit opens the door to new valuable ways for consumers to engage with their favorite services, redefining how we interact with technology in our daily lives. In my role, I'm fortunate to work with visionary partners who are pushing the boundaries of wearable computing and AI. It's this spirit of innovation and exploration that fuels my passion and excitement for the work we do. Recently, we have partnered with a small select group of partners such as Disney Imagineering and 18 Birdies to provide their teams with early access to the Metawareables device access toolkit. Together, we have prototyped cutting edge integrations for AI glasses that showcase the incredible potential of this platform. These prototypes are just the beginning. They demonstrate the diverse and exciting use cases that the toolkit enables from enhancing everyday experiences to creating entirely new ways to interact with the world around us. The Disney Imagineering team has focused on exploring how AI glasses can transform the in-person experience at Disneyland parks. Their prototype offers a glimpse into a future where visitors can seamlessly access important park information on the go, all while remaining present and enjoying time with family and friends.
