Making cinematic travel content can be pretty hard, as we trying to make the most beautiful cinematic travel content. I often take this huge camera with me whenever I travel, but I often find myself needing this time to just enjoy the moment rather than filming constantly. Because at the end of the day, we make cinematic travel content because we like to travel, and it's super important to capture memories for, of course, your camera, but also for your heart as well. This problem, the balance between life and work can be solved by using stock footage. Today in this tutorial video, I'm partnering up with Adobe Stock to create the most cinematic travel content that you can film while balancing life and work. So for this travel video, I live in Japan and I wanted to create this summer short form reel that is shot horizontally and I often travel around Japan and try to get the most beautiful footage that I can take. I still often, very often forget to shoot certain locations the way I want to film, or missing golden hours. So first of all, these are footage that I wanted to use for my cinematic video, but it is missing a lot of variety. To solve this, I went to Adobe Stock and found this footage of timelapse in Itsukushima (Miyajima) during daytime. One of the amazing things about Adobe Stock is that you can download 4K ProRes footage straight from the website, and after you license the footage, you will have this amazing high quality footage ready to go. And timelapse is very time consuming and often when we are traveling we only have limited time, so this is super helpful. The second shot is this drone shot going through the forest, and as a person who wants to pack as light as possible and I also do not own a drone, this is very helpful. And the third footage is this firework. In Japan, especially in summer, there are a lot of firework festivals and I think this footage captured the firework so so well. And last but certainly not the least is this footage of Kiyomizu Temple in Kyoto during golden hour. Golden hour is pretty hard to film because the weather and the timing of the day has to be perfect, and this is especially for Kyoto. But Kiyomizu Temple is one of the most popular destinations, so it's very crowded. But thanks Adobe Stock, we can download this video in 4K ProRes at the highest quality. In terms of the footage, there is one thing that I wanted to add to spice the video up just a little bit more, and that is a Light Leak Transition not only stock footage that is shot on a camera, but in Adobe Stock. You can also find different types of transition, templates and bunch of other effects that can be useful for editing videos. I found this film Burn Light leak footage from Adobe Stock. I download this in 1080 and all I did was change the blend mode to light, adjust the size of the footage, and I was good to go. Adobe Stock is not only amazing for stock footage, but also music and audio track as well. I wanted to have this solo piano cinematic song that will go well with the theme that I wanted to go, and all I did was search in Adobe Premiere Pro using Adobe Stock License the music track, and simply drag the music track onto my timeline. And I was good to go. And that goes. same with sound tracks too. But yeah, I hope this video helped you guys. Using Adobe Stock can not only enhance your creativity, but also save your time while traveling and don't have to think too much about the shot that you want, and simply enjoy the moment and enjoying the art of cinematography.
