## 스크립트

Siemens Xcelerator can help address expert staff shortages. Here’s the pitch: Say you want to make your business more sustainable, but lack the expertise. With Siemens Xcelerator, you can do this, even without having enough in-house expertise. Instead, you can use products that are easy to integrate, like Building X. This helps you make use of your building’s data to forecast your energy needs. Or use advanced analytics to reduce costs and emissions. And because this data is accessed through one platform, you can use it whenever to track and report on your sustainability efforts. Still need help? Then connect with our community of ecosystem members. Best of all, these offers can be used as a service, so businesses of any size can use them without hefty upfront costs. So, there you go. Siemens Xcelerator helps address expert staff shortages by helping you use data to make informed decisions, even without having enough in-house expertise.
