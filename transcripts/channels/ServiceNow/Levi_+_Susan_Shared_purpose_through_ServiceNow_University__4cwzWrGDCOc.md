## 스크립트

One day I was under a house, cramped, dirty, and I realized that I couldn't do this forever. One day I'm at work and I get a call from Levi. I said, "Hey, mom, I want to do what you do and get into Service Now." I laughed at him and I had to tell her like, "No, I'm serious cuz I need something better." My mom pointed me towards Service Now University. Trying to balance construction with family and learning at the same time was tough work, but I was really motivated to build a better life for my family. When I started looking for jobs in the Service Now ecosystem, I ended up getting a job at the company my mom works for. I had nothing to do with it. I said, "We're on a meeting. Don't say I love you." At the end of a call, I may have slipped up a couple of times, but we got it under control pretty quick. If you're thinking of making a career change, it is hard work. I love it. And being able to provide for my family while still being right here with them. [Music]
