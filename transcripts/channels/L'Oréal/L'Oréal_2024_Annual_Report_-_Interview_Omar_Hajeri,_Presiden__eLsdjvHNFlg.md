## 스크립트

For 115 years, L'Oréal Professionnel Products Division has been leading the professional beauty industry, serving all stylists worldwide. 2024 was marked by solid growth, strong innovation pipeline, and ongoing commitment to sustainability. The division continues to gain market share. In 2024, we achieved a record market share of 26%. We continue to lead the professional beauty industry. The strong momentum of premium hair care. We continue to see increasing demand for premium hair care experiences. Consumers are seeking professional-grade formulas, sophisticated treatments, and personalized services. Thanks to our powerful brand portfolio and our anchorage in more than 400,000 salons worldwide, we are ideally positioned to capture this holistic trend of premium hair care. We innovate in beauty tech and sustainability with Airlight Pro by L'Oréal Professionnel. This is our first professional hair dryer powered by infrared light technology for faster drying, lower energy consumption, and no damage to the hair. Having won multiple awards, Airlight Pro opens a new era for the division in the professional tools segment. In 2024, we have accelerated our refillable products agenda. We've launched for the first time a refillable oil with our iconic product Elixir Ultime by Kérastase. With its luxurious and refillable packaging, we have successfully combined sustainability, high-end design, and sensoriality. Furthermore, we have continued to roll out our Hair Stylists for the Future program, now encompassing more than 6,500 salons. This program supports salons in their sustainable transition by focusing on reducing water and energy consumption and improving waste management. The division is driving the transformation of a professional beauty industry through a winning strategy based on three growth drivers. First, with our powerful brand portfolio and our breakthrough innovations, we are answering to all hair needs at all price points. Second, with our winning omnichannel strategy, reaching consumers across salons, e-commerce, and selective retail, we are recruiting new consumers across all channels. Third, thanks to our expansion in new growth markets such as Brazil, Mexico, and the Gulf countries, we are conquering new salons and recruiting new consumers in all regions. Together, with 3 million stylists and our global L'Oreal Professional Products Division team, we are shaping the future of professional beauty. The causes, led by our two billionaire brands, Kerastase and L'Oreal Professionnel, exemplify our commitment to positive social impact. The Haeder program, led by L'Oreal Professionnel, is dedicated to the hair stylists' mental health. This initiative provides solutions to help hair stylists to manage their mental health and well-being. We have already reached 130,000 professionals. Kérastase continues to support women in their careers and reduce the gender confidence gap through the Power Talks program. The most exciting challenge of 2024 was entering the new territory of premium devices with our beauty tech gem Air L'Oréal Pro by L'Oréal Professionnel.
