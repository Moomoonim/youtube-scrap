## 스크립트

- When I was young, I had to wash the dishes, take out the trash, and I had to mow lawns. It was just hot and I just didn't like mowing lawns. I would rather wash the dishes than go outside in the hot sun and mow lawns. That's how bad I disliked mowing lawns. But now I love to do it and I do it every single day for free. It's kind of amazing how life works sometimes. Growing up, my parents always told me to help people when you see fit. I came across this elderly man outside mowing his lawn. It looked like he was struggling, so I pulled over and helped him out. That night, I just decided I'll start mowing free lawns for the elderly, disabled, single parents, and veterans right here in Huntsville, Alabama. So I went on Facebook and I made a post asking anyone if they knew anyone that needed their lawn mowed and I'd come by and mow it for free. And that one small act of kindness kind of changed my life forever. We saw a lot of parents reach out and say, "Look, this looks like a good thing. Can my kids get involved?" And we said, "Sure, come on." And that's when I came up with the idea of Raising Men Lawn Care Service. Just wanted to give kids an outlet, put down the video games, and get out there and make a difference in their community. A lot of these kids are building character as well. They'll make new friends and they will see the smiles on the faces of the people who they'll mow for and find out how much it meant to them. They see you doing good. They wanna help out. It's amazing to see them wanting to get out there in their community and help out.
