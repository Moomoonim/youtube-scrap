## 스크립트

Cardiac interpretation provides an automated ejection fraction estimate. If you capture a paristernal long axis apical 4 or apical 2 image of sufficient quality, the autoef algorithm automatically calculates an ejection fraction. If you capture one of those views, you will see an auto EF report created along with the confidence interval. You will also see which views need to be acquired to increase its accuracy. In this example, adding an AP4 or AP2 will increase its accuracy. At the top of the screen, you see how many views you have acquired that are contributing to the autof calculation. Here, one of three views have been captured that can contribute to the autof result. To see which views in your protocol can contribute to calculating autof, select the views tab. As you acquire more views of sufficient quality, the autof percentage will update along with the confidence interval. The smaller the range, the more accurate the output. An autof will not be produced on a specific view if it is not of sufficient quality. If this is the case, go to your views list and try to record a better quality view. During the exam, you can preview the autof report by tapping here to see if the autof falls within the normal range. Once you have completed your scanning protocol, an auto EF report will be available in the image gallery. If you have multiple autof reports, choose the last one in the gallery. This will be the most recent. When you select the report and view your final auto EF, you will see which views contributed to the calculation and whether the auto EF falls within the normal range.
