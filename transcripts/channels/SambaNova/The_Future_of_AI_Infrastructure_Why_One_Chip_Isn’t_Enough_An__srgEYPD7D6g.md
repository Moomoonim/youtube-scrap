## 스크립트

AI inference is evolving. AI workloads are becoming more interactive, more dynamic, and more agent-driven. AI is moving beyond single responses into multi-step execution. That shift introduces new compute demands across training, inference, and agent orchestration. Different processors now handle different parts of the workload. CPUs orchestrate agents and manage tool calls. GPUs power model training and other compute-heavy workloads. RDUs optimize inference for real-time AI responses. Together, these components work across modern AI infrastructure. AI workflows are becoming more distributed. With different systems handling different stages of execution. Infrastructure is becoming more specialized for modern AI workloads. In practice, AI workflows now span multiple compute systems. This is where SambaNova fits into heterogeneous AI infrastructure. In heterogeneous inference, different stages run on different hardware. The GPU handles prefill, where the workload is compute-bound. The RDU handles decode, where the workload becomes memory-bound. RDUs are designed for modern AI inference with continuous data flow execution, models staying resident in memory, and less data movement during execution. That makes them well-suited for efficient agentic AI workloads. The result is faster inference, lower latency, and improved efficiency. All of it optimized for modern AI workloads. AI infrastructure is evolving. Heterogeneous systems are where that future is heading. SambaNova is built for that future. Delivering faster inference, dramatically lower energy usage, deployment in existing data centers, and higher utilization at scale.
