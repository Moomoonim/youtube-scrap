## 스크립트

The big challenge that we face is how to actually get high performance, low compute closer to the user. Akamai technologies is a cybersecurity company and cloud computing company whose mission is to power and protect the businesses online. In many ways, you can think about Akamai as cloud computing business, similar to that of what you'd see from the hyperscalers and Amazon, a Google or Microsoft. We're focusing on use cases. They don't. Customers are constantly looking to push the envelope in terms of what they can do from a processing perspective, whether it's running agents closer to the user trying to figure out how to create new immersive experiences in video or gaming. One of the biggest challenges with cloud computing is how do you get dense compute capabilities close to the user, often in difficult to reach regions where you have space constraints, power constraints? What we have found is that the AMD epic processors allow us to run cloud native applications and services with performance, scale, and cost that our customers are looking for in these modern architectures. AMD has been a fantastic partner, and they've really leaned in to helping Akamai with our challenge of trying to get more compute closer to the user.
