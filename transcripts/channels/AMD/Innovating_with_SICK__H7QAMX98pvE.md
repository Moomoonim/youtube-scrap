## 스크립트

SICK is quite unique as a company. We develop a wide range of sensors for industrial markets, specifically for production and logistics. We chose AMD as a partner because they provide high-performance, low-power solutions. You find SICK products in warehouses, fulfillment centers, in mobile robots, a wide range of different places. Our cooperation with AMD is fundamental in providing AI-based solutions for our customers. SICK's approach to AI is to use it for making efficient decisions and classification tasks, but also to solve front-end-related problems and making better data for the customers. AMD helps us to solve those problems by providing us competitive, embedded solutions like ZYNQ, Kintex, Spartan. The world without SICK would be a less safe place, a less productive place, a less efficient place than with SICK.
