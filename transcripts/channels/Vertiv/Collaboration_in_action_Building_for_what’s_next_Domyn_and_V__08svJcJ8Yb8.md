## 스크립트

At Vertive, we build future ready digital infrastructure through technology advancement with partners and deep customer collaboration. Vertive end-to-end power and cooling reference designs developed to support the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 platform are part of our strategy to anticipate increased rack power and help customers develop deployment ready infrastructure and support AI factories at scale. Realtime simulation through the digital twin built on NVIDIA Omniverse enables engineers to dramatically reduce planning times and reduce risk during the buildout process. Another partnership with engineering software specialist ANIs allows product performance to be simulated and improved before a single component is built, cutting waste, costs and timelines. We also bring innovation to life through deep customer collaboration. Compass data centers partnered with Verdive to develop the Vertive Coolph face flex. This innovative solution combines liquid and refrigerant-based air cooling technologies into a single unit. This ability to deploy either air or liquid cooling provides businesses with a confidence to meet current needs while providing the flexibility to anticipate future requirements. In Italy, the domain coliseum project developed in collaboration with NVIDIA and Verdive sets a new standard for sovereign AI data centers. Verdive is delivering pre-fabricated infrastructure, highdensity power, and hybrid liquid air cooling. Verdive manufacturing and factory integration reduces deployment time by 50% compared to traditional data center builds. The site will also leverage Verdive Unifi management software for real-time orchestration of power, cooling, and compute. In Norway, data center operator Polar is deploying 12 megawatts of AI ready capacity, easily scalable to 52 megawatt. Powered entirely by hydro energy, the facility leverages Verdives's integrated solutions and latest power and cooling technologies to support AI and high performance computing workloads with maximum efficiency. Together with our partners and customers, Vertive is redefining what's possible, enabling the next generation of digital infrastructure to be more powerful, more efficient, and ready for what's next.
