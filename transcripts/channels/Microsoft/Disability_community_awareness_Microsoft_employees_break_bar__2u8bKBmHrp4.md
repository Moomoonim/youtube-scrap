## 스크립트

We are more than a single story. We are individuals with talents, passions, and perspectives that shape the world. The challenges we navigate don't define us, they deepen our understanding. They give us insight into what others may overlook, and fuel our drive to create solutions that work for everyone. From visible to invisible, from seen to unseen, our lived experiences expand what's possible. We design for inclusion. We innovate with empathy. We build systems that connect and empower. At Microsoft, accessibility is our core, defining every move we make to shape tomorrow. This is The Code of Us.
