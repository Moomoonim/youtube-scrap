## 스크립트

Formula One has a soul. That soul is very edgy. To capture that feeling of Formula One and the grid, the absolute action is a hard thing to do. This is basically the first time ever that I said, let's render it. We're working with a combination of Maya, Houdini and Blender. AMD and the technology behind it touches so many points of the pipeline. Having AMD chips within our compositing workstations and also our editing and color grading suites speeds up the whole process. It didn't take long to figure out that AMD is the best choice we can make. Lewis Hamilton drives the car. AMD drives the data. Together, we are driven to advance.
