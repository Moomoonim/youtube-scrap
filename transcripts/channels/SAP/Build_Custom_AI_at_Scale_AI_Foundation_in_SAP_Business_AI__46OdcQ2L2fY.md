## 스크립트

How do I get the most out of AI? That's just the thing. Prepackaged AI solutions are great, but they don't always meet your individual needs. If you want your business to stand out, you need more. You need a platform that lets you build, run, and integrate custom solutions at scale. AI Foundation is the operating system at the heart of SAP Business AI. It's what empowers you to make AI your own. To match your unique requirements you can extend Joule with custom skills and create your own Joule Agents with Joule Studio. On top of that, you can orchestrate workflows and make custom AI solutions with the generative AI hub. All within a unified system. Manage all of it with built-in automation and governance, making your AI operations secure, compliant, and powerful. SAP Document AI handles your business content automatically, while SAP HANA Cloud ensures everything runs at top speed. Context matters. That's why AI Foundation lets you embed your custom solutions into your business applications. It also connects them with both your SAP and non-SAP data sources, so they produce reliable results that actually hit the mark. With AI Foundation, you create value through every stage of the AI lifecycle. Build, run and integrate with AI you can trust.
