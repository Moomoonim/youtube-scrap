## 스크립트

Today, I believe Luma will change how work is done. First, let's talk about the foundations. Two years ago, the AI industry made a bet. The bet was that we should build separate model for every capability. A language model, a vision model, a world model, a physics model, even an emotion model. And let's chain them together. And somehow you will get intelligence. I think it was a reasonable bet. But now we know it was a wrong bet. This is not really intelligence. This is plumbing. And we are wasting the most important technology that humans have ever produced on plumbing. There is a better way, and it has been worked on for millions of years. The human brain. The human brain has different regions, areas for language, for vision, for spatial reasoning. They were never built separately. You know, they evolved together. And at birth, they are wired together through billions of complex neural connections. You cannot call them separate. They share context. They fire simultaneously. They think together as one. For example, when an architect sketches on a napkin, her hand is drawing a line, but her mind is simulating the load on the beam, the light through the window, what it feels like to stand in the room. She's seeing the building that doesn't exist yet. Left and right brain working together. This is really not a drawing task. This is general intelligence. So then the question becomes, what if we stopped building artificial intelligence in pieces and built it as one thing? This is what we call unified intelligence. Unified intelligence is logic and reasoning paired with physical accuracy and creativity. Unified models understand text, code, long-form video, speech, audio. They can reason and produce outputs in a combination of formats best suited to solve the problem. Quite like the human brain. We have been working towards this moment since Luma was founded. And today, we are introducing the very first model in this line of research. We are calling it Unified One or Uni One. Uni One is built on Unified Intelligence architecture that we have been working on for a while now. It's a decoder-only autoregressive transformer that operates over a unified token space of text, images, and in the future other modalities all in the same thing. Text and images are created as first-class citizens in a single interleave sequence both as input and output. This architecture unlocks a very powerful capability. The model can think in language and imagine and render in pixels within the same forward pass much like a human brain. So, what does it actually feel like to use a model built on Unified Intelligence? Three things. Intelligent, directable, and cultured.
