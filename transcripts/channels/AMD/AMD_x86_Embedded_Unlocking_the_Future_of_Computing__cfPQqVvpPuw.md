## 스크립트

When it comes to meeting the strict requirements of embedded applications, failure is not an option. AMD x86 embedded processors offer integrated connectivity, built-in redundancy, and security features that deliver results you can depend on. AMD offers three main families. EPYC embedded, Ryzen embedded, and Ryzen AI embedded. EPYC embedded processors are built for networking, storage, and industrial edge servers, where data must be processed reliably and securely, close to the source. They feature network-specific connectivity, like Ethernet, with time-sensitive networking, high core counts for parallel processing, integrated connectivity, long life cycles, and advanced security. If you need real-time data processing, EPYC embedded can deliver up to 128 PCIe lanes for acceleration and storage, handling dozens of streams at once. For security, you get encryption, secure virtualization, and hardware root of trust. Beyond performance per watt, EPYC embedded adds reliability, compact size, and seven years of product lifecycle. If your application needs graphics and compute power, choose AMD Ryzen embedded or Ryzen AI embedded processors. They unlock possibilities for intelligent systems with some devices featuring NPUs for AI acceleration. Ryzen embedded processors are built upon leadership performance and add 10-year life cycles, broad connectivity, robust security, and open-source drivers, purposely designed for embedded environments. A single Ryzen embedded chip can power real-time medical imaging, enable immersive retail experiences, or manage your car's digital dashboard. Our new Ryzen AI embedded processors are built for AI applications at the edge. These include everything from automotive digital cockpits, to smart healthcare, to physical AI for autonomous systems, including humanoid robots. Ryzen AI embedded devices provide high performance, efficient AI compute in a compact BGA package, even for the most constrained embedded systems. If you're evaluating x86 embedded platforms for your next project, AMD is committed and ready to partner with you.
