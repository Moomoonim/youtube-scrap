## 스크립트

Regardless of whatever comes to your way, or whatever happens to you, do not give up on yourself. On World Refugee Day 2020, we asked former refugees about finding strength in uncertain times. After the time leaving my country, I was too much afraid. What do I miss the most? I would say my friends over there, and I would say I miss the music. What really makes me happy? Really, it's my family. When I see my family is happy I am happy. I am always smiling. You can even ask my co-workers. I think we can fight anything with a smile. I am grateful, that I actually I had people to talk to. I am grateful, I actually had my faith. Now we're fighting COVID-19 all together, we can make it. Be strong, don't be afraid! I would say - don't give up, that's it! By 2022 we will have 2500 refugees involved in Skills for Employment programmes in 30 countries across the world We're stronger together.
