## 스크립트

Today, AI is part of our everyday life, and the Dell 14 plus brings on device AI, making it accessible to the consumer on the everyday. The Dell 14 plus 2 in 1 comes in a very small footprint, comes equipped with on device AI, multitask more. So the AMD Ryzen AI processors are a three layer architecture. We have the CPU, GPU, and the NPU. The NPU was the neural processing unit of this device, and basically where all of your AI magic happens. As you are multitasking and doing different things simultaneously, your AI specific tasks get offloaded to the neural processing unit, which free up the CPU and GPU to do the other background stuff at a faster pace. I think it's not just about being able to do things more seamlessly, but also being able to function more seamlessly. And that's what AI brings.
