## 스크립트

My favorite part of UFC is stepping someone into our space on a big fight night. The look on their face when they watch all the lights go to black. And when those fighters make the walk there's that feeling of getting ready to really watch something special. UFC live event humbly, is as great a spectacle as anything in live sports. One of the most critical things about my job is to make people care at home. But how can I as a play by play guy within the confines of a very fast moving sport tell those stories? We try to orchestrate and tell the stories as best as possible in real time or beforehand and add a different level of depth. So for us, data is story. Statistics are not valuable Statistics are only valuble in what they teach you about what is happeing in front of you in a visceral, practical way. We saw an opportunity in MMA to set the standard for what great data ought to be. We track over 65 data points per fighter per round. They had plenty of data, but what was very difficult for them was to really mine that data properly and to find interesting and entertaining insights for their audience. It would take somebody who knew the data in and out and had the technical capability to search the data to create an insight. So the problem UFC came to us with was how do we use technology, AI, ML to speed up this process? We partnered with IBM and very quickly we understood that IBM had people who understood sports, and data analytics in sports, and more specifically, our sport. Once we really had an understanding of that data, we then needed to map specific insight pipelines to that data. So it could be an insight around a win streak, it could be a record, it could be milestone. So that's really the heart and soul of the entire UFC Insights Engine. The UFC Insights Engine is a data exploration tool. It's meant to provide UFC broadcast team members an easy way to access all UFC data at their fingertips. We get a listing of all of the insights the system created. We get those insights sorted by how relevant the Insights Engine thinks that they are. The UFC Insights Engine is crucial to our broadcast. It's something that adds a different layer, a different level to the statistics and storytelling. UFC Insights Engine built with IBM watsonx. The Volk Man is 7-0 in the UFC when he lands at least 15 leg kicks. 4/4 on submission attempts She is 1 of only 2 fighters in UFC history with a 100% submission accuracy. As a producer, as a fan, this is what you want. This is what I want. I'm so excited about the future with IBM, with watsonx, with the Insights Engine. I think this is something tangible that we can really do to take it to the next level.
