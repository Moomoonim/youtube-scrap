## 스크립트

When I was a teenager, I think I was 14 or 15, I decided to start doing martial arts. Martial arts or self-defense will affect the way you present yourself to the world. It helped me to feel more confident in my own body and in myself. I decided that I was not just going to be a martial arts practitioner, but I was going to help spread the word about the benefit of physical empowerment. Fight Back is a virtual reality experience that we designed to run on the Meta Quest. It takes you on a journey of self-discovery. You get connected to your inner strength and feeling strong in your own body. - Welcome to empowerment self-defense class utilizing virtual reality that will teach you some of the basics of self-defense. You will see circles of light. All you have to do is go punch through the circle of lights. - In the experience we use hand tracking. So, that means you're using your hands to defeat your enemies, not game controllers. Those motion that you have been practicing over the course of the experience that you build muscle memory for are actual gesture of self-defense. Fight Back is your self connecting with your own body. It all comes from within you. - Wow. That was very cool. - We've had people experiencing Fight Back having a giant smile on their face and sparkle in their eyes. With the Quest 3, I have even more chances to bring my stories to my audience to convince more women and other vulnerable people to join self-defense training. - Good job, everyone. - When I play Fight Back, I feel strong, I feel empowered, and I feel surrounded by love from my sisterhood.
