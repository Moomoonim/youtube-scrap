## 스크립트

[Music] Hello everyone, welcome to Computex 2025. I'm your host Ying Yin from Nvidia product management. It's a true pleasure to be here in Taiwan, a home for Nvidia for over three decades and the heart of so many of our invaluable partnerships. Our CEO Jensen Wong talked about many exciting technologies and let's get to it. Jensen began by redefining the modern computer not as a standalone PC or server but as a fullscale data center operating as a unified AIdriven computing system. These new data centers or AI factories transform energy into tokens of intelligence marking a fundamental shift in how computation powers innovation. At the heart of this transformation is Nvidia's Grace Blackwell platform. Now in full production, the new Nvidia Grace Flagwell Ultra delivers 1.5x the inference performance with groundbreaking engineering that includes dual chip connectivity, TSMC's advanced POWSL packaging and the NV link spine which enables 130 terabytes per second of all to all GPU bandwidth across 72 Nvidia flagwell ultra GPUs. effectively creating a massive single virtual GPU. Nvidia also introduced Envink Fusion, an open and flexible approach to Nilink that allows partners to tightly integrate their own CPUs, A6 and accelerators with Nvidia Whiteall and the upcoming Ruben architecture. This marks a leap forward for semi-custom AI infrastructure solutions tailored to diverse workloads and industries to further accelerate the enterprise shift to AI powered infrastructure. Nvidia's ecosystem partners spanning major players from ASUS as rockrecom gigabyte invents egatron QCT super micro we strong and wewing will offer RTX pro servers built to handle the demands of enterprise AI factory environments. One of the most exciting announcements was the Nvidia Enterprise AI platform accelerates trillion dollar global IT investment. NVIDIA AI data platform, a purpose-built storage system that can semantically query unstructured data with unprecedented speed and accuracy. Paired with Nvidia AIQ, this platform tackles one of AI's biggest bottlenecks, extracting meaning from massive amount of raw unstructured data. Finally, the keynote showcased the Nvidia MGX ecosystem component availability featuring data center infrastructure innovations from over 60 global suppliers. This includes MGX components support Nvidia GB300, MBL72, liquid cool systems, and high power architectures. All highlighting substantial mechanical, thermal, and electrical solutions, laying the groundwork of Nvidia's next generation product launches and widespread adoption. From silicon to systems to AI factory, we cannot make it without you. Thank you, Taiwan.
