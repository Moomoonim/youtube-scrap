## 스크립트

- When I clip in to my pedals, and when I start my ride, everything else disappears. - Because then you got that bike. That was your Christmas present that year. - My grandma was diagnosed with mantle cell lymphoma in 2003, and being nine years old, she was someone I have looked up to my entire life. I would always hear stories about people getting cancer and everything, but when it hit so close to home, it changed my life. I was just scrolling through Instagram and it said, "Do you want to bike across the country and make a difference?" And I was like, that's literally everything I've wanted to do. (people cheering) All right, we're rolling. It's day 19. 70 miles to Arapahoe, Nebraska today. Today we are going to Colorado. (people cheering) Thank you guys again for all these donations. We're almost at $700,000 as a team, for all the 4K teams this year. I'm biking across the country for cancer research and everyday that I ride I'm thinking about everyone that is battling for their lives or have lost the battle. And how what I'm doing is gonna change their life for the better. What's up guys? Day 70 here. We are going into Baker Beach in San Francisco. It's been one crazy ride. (clapping) (cheering) The sense of community that has been brought to myself and to the other members of my team. It's just extremely powerful to have that loss turn into such a positive gain. (laughter) She would be smiling.
