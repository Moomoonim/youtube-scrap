## 스크립트

In Sao Paolo a lady lived in an apartment without any windows for almost ten years. That story triggered Fernando. He decided to devote himself to improving the housing situation for the families living in Sao Paolos Favelas. Fernando became a social entrepreneur and his mission and desire to scale up his work is now supported by us at IKEA Social Entrepreneurship. We want to make a better everyday life for as many people as possible. And since exclusion and inequality are global challenges we have partnerships in all parts of the world. We partner with social entrepreneurs who use their business as a way to make everyday life better for those who need it the most. With some of our partners we collaborate and do business with them to produce unique hand-made collections for IKEA customers, globally. And we both benefit. Our partners gain insights about global product development. and we get to learn about new handicraft techniques. But we also support smaller local initiatives where a single IKEA store or IKEA country partner up with social entrepreneurs to make a difference. Together, these global and local partnerships support the creation of jobs and incomes for tens of thousands of vulnerable people. Seeing the impact that this has made us want to do more. So today we back and boost social entrepreneurs whos work is not related to producing IKEA products or services. We partner up IKEA co-workers with social entrepreneurs and share our business knowledge and networks with them. This way we support them to expand and have a bigger impact. It also gives us the opportunity to learn from them, like how to become a more circular, entrepreneurial and sustainable business. And we will share our learnings to inspire others, increasing each others potential to create lasting social change. All this aims to improve vulnerable and marginalised peoples capabilities and their means of living. It's about making a positive impact on people and on society.
