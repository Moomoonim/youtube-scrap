## 스크립트

My biggest concern of not having my hands was how am I going to be able to do fencing and draw? And my mom told me, "don't worry because both fencing and art are not made with your hands, but they lie within your mind." You never know how and when you will fall in love with the sport you do. I was very lucky. At five years old I happened to find myself in a gym. I went there to try volleyball, but I walked into the wrong entrance and found fencing. I really wanted to create something where everyone had the chance to try more sports and not just one. Bebe Vio Academy! The beauty of sports is that it manages to break down any kind of prejudice whether it is color, whether it is religion whether it is male or female, gender, sexuality, anything… And that is precisely what we are trying to create a parallel world in which they can play together and create normality among children. For me, the Bebe Academy was a dream for many years so to see that it's now working that children are happy and are doing lot of sports is really pleasing. Go behind the blue line! Don't you dare... But really, you need to let them figure it out, you need to let them fall over you need to let them get hurt and learn how to deal with it on their own. Let them make mistakes, let them mess it up 500 times, I’ll still be there until they make that shot right. The goal is to show the prosthetics as a game not as an obstacle. Can I make a coffee? I started my first experience on the national team making coffee for everyone. They called me up just to make coffee. How old were you? I was young...16. See how good I am. I am hating you for this. In these past years I realized how many things I thought were difficult are actually easy. Perhaps because we're born with competition in our blood.
