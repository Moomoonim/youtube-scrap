## 스크립트

What’s real and what isn’t? It’s getting more and more difficult to tell the difference. What AI is already capable of nowadays is really impressive. Today’s episode is about how AI videos are taking the internet by storm, how you can distinguish real content from fake content and how you can create AI videos yourself. I find it pretty impressive how far AI has come within the past year. The technology is still in its infancy and yet the videos are already so good and often seem very real. AI videos are getting increasingly realistic, but how are these videos created? Right, let’s do a test. Most people have a smartphone, tablet or laptop with which they can create AI-generated videos. You also need tools, e.g. Synthesia, WO2, Runway or Sora OpenAI. Now it’s time for you to get creative. What’s your story and how do you want the video to look? We then use this information to create a prompt, that is, a precise instruction to the AI. In our case, we want to create a video of me eating muesli. You see, creating videos with artificial intelligence is not that difficult. What is a creative playground for many can be overwhelming for others. There are always advantages and disadvantages with new technologies. The advantages are, say, that creative processes can be implemented quickly. That new lines of work are emerging, such as AI editors or prompt engineers, and that AI can provide support for learning processes. Disadvantages are: it consumes a lot of energy; AI videos have less creative depth than real ones; the result is still not 100% error-free; and manipulation by the media is becoming ever easier with AI videos. Especially with respect to security and seeking information, it’s advantageous to be able to recognise whether a video is manipulatively AI-generated or not. Let’s look at how you can find that out. Watch and listen carefully and check out who the video is from. Hands and fingers often look strange or unnatural and move illogically. Light and shadow are also not always very realistic and voices sometimes sound artificial and unemotional. AI videos aren’t yet perfect, but they’re developing rapidly. They are already being used in commercials, education and social media. Anyone that’s creative and learns how to work with AI stands a real chance in the future.
