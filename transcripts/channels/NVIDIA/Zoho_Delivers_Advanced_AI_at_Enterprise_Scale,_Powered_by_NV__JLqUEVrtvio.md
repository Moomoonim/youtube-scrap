We have tools to run your business. We call it the operating system of business. Every department, from marketing to finance to recruitment to HR, we have tools across the stack. As market leaders, NVIDIA has given us a lot to take back. We started off with just GPU procurement and in the last couple of years, given that large language models have become a thing, we are on top of NVIDIA's H100 GPUs. The Hopper architecture has really helped us build things faster, especially with FP8 technology. Training our models on FP8, and doing the training using a framework like Nemo has really helped us accelerate our large language model development process. We are in the process of building our own large language models to support Indic models, and to support contextual intelligence across businesses with privacy at its core. We're also using NVIDIA's inference framework, TensorRT LLM. We work with everything from narrow to small, medium and large language models. And especially on the larger end of the spectrum. We've been heavy on NVIDIA software and hardware stack. So use cases like grammar correction, use cases like, voice to text transcribing, where we use it in our meeting tool to get the transcript. And these things have to be real time, we believe in something called contextual intelligence, where we rightsize models across use cases. We build this with privacy at core; one customer's data is not used to train other customer's models, so we're very particular about it. All of this means a lot of model training and inference has to happen at scale. And that is why I'm looking forward to the partnership as well. with all the support that we get from NVIDIA, it really helps us go to market faster and ensure the businesses who trust Zoho to the get maximum value out of their ecosystem.
