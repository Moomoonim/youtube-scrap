## 스크립트

Traditionally, adding advanced video effects has been a compute heavy process. Requiring time consuming renders, preview lags and significant battery drain, especially on mobile workstations. That changes with CyberLink's PowerDirector. Optimized to leverage AMD Ryzen™ AI Pro processors. Together, they enable a faster, more efficient post-production workflow using dedicated AI acceleration. as the user scrolls through previews and adds effects to the timeline, PowerDirector taps into the AMD Ryzen™ AI Pro processors’ integrated NPU to perform real-time scene detection, isolating subjects and backgrounds with precision. The NPU handles AI driven motion tracking, object segmentation, and dynamic effect placement, freeing up CPU and GPU resources for other editing tasks. The result? Immediate scene analysis and near-instantaneous effect application. Thanks to hardware driven AI acceleration, playback remains fluid and responsive even with multiple layers of visual effects and animations. Harness the power of AMD Ryzen™ AI Pro processors and CyberLink PowerDirector and experience high performance AI enhanced editing that keeps up with your creative demands.
