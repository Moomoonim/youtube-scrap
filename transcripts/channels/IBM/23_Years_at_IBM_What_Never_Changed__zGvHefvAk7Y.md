## 스크립트

Being in IBM for 23 years, people readily assume I've been only doing one role for 23 years. So this is, in fact, by my best recollection, my 13th role in 23 years. My name is Pia Azarcon. I'm the Managing Partner of IBM Consulting here in the Philippines. And I've been with IBM for 23 years. I started in IBM at the time when it was largely infrastructure. Technology that was at the forefront of business requirements, and IBM has evolved through the years. We are at the center of the generative AI revolution and quantum technology. So, you can say that because IBM reinvents itself every so often, I've been fortunate enough to find myself in that reinvention time and time again. The technology is going to continue to change. If you and I spoke, maybe, a year or two years from now, I'd probably be talking about something different. AI has definitely changed the landscape and made it so much more of a level playing field for many people. And the platforms we use today will change. With that, what does not change is people's ability to understand business problems and communicate them effectively with our clients. My advice is very, very simple to young people. You know, those that are starting out in the workforce. Those that have been in the workforce and are looking for something new. Stay curious. And I say that because it is very necessary to continue to want to be a lifelong learner. And I've seen, right, inside IBM and out that those people that can connect the technology to actual business problems and explain the complex ideas in a much more grounded, practical and pragmatic way are often the ones that come out the winners.
