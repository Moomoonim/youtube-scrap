## 스크립트

Founderz is an online and AI business school where we help professionals and organizations learn the skills they need to thrive in the AI era. We started building massive online courses around how to adopt AI and how to start using AI with Microsoft and suddenly we moved from having like 10,000 learners to having 100,000 learners. So it's about AI literacy, but most importantly, we teach learners to think for themselves, to have critical thinking, and to question things and everything that they can get from AI. Our clients are both professionals and small and big organizations. We actually are helping more than 1400 companies around the world. We started building our AI features and technologies and that's, I think, the beautiful part of, you know, building AI in Azure Microsoft Foundry because basically we can forget about the compute power. We're going to need for specific day. But even if we didn't know that's coming, suddenly a lot of people will start joining one of our free courses we'll be ready for that. So in order to create the best learning experience we had to develop our own technology. And this is why we recently launched the AI fellows that are, AI mentors that are available 24/7. And in any language. Microsoft Foundry allows us to build and innovate while being fully compliant with European regulations. So we started developing our own control tower that was using AI, so we could basically, react to every interaction happening in the platform and, making sure we could detect the most important ones where our human team should, go into. And so with the AI that we have embedded in the platform, we can learn from the progress and the way that each one of our students are learning. We can give this hyper personalized feedback. The fellows help us actually handle more than 15,000 interactions a day. And then when there's something that it's too complex for them to answer, they escalate that to our human team. We believe the revolution isn't just AI, but education. So we really need to change the way that we teach and the way that we learn. The AI can be a very powerful growth tool if used responsibly. Technology is a lever, a helper, but the people are put at the center.
