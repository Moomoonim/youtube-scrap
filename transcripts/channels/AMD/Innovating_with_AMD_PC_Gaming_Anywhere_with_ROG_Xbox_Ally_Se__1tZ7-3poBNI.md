## 스크립트

The Xbox Ally, as a device, it's meant to bridge gaming and mobility for when you need to play on the go. Gaming handheld itself is a different category in terms of performance and power to any other device in the market. With the overall ROG Ally series, we focus on the moniker “Play All Your Games” because we truly believe that a consumer has a wide array of games that they'd be interested in playing. And so with Xbox Ally, we use the power of Windows to help establish a foundation that lets you play everything that you have. The AMD Z series processors are designed for handheld, where there's a great understanding of power and efficiency, and to be able to play with just enough performance but all the battery life that you need. For gamers, it means having hardware selection that supports their various needs. If you're an Xbox player, the Xbox Ally is going to be a new experience for you. So the Xbox Ally is a PC handheld where you have the depth and the ability to play your PC gaming library, but it's going to feel familiar because it is a controller based mindset. Having partners like AMD that fully understand the support that's necessary for gamers on day one is critical to making sure that we build the best device possible.
