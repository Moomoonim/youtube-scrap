## 스크립트

Most of you are familiar with AI tools like MidJourney or ChatGPT. But they all need an internet connection. Now there’s on-device AI. It is directly integrated on our devices and so doesn’t need an internet connection. Apple, Google, Samsung and Android are working flat out on new projects. The race for the best features is on. With on-device AI, the data is processed right on your phone. So requests don’t first have to be sent to a server and back, because it all happens in here, in your smartphone or tablet. This can have certain advantages. First: your data stays on your device. That means you don’t have to worry that your personal information might be floating around the internet somewhere. Second: you don’t need an internet connection. That can be an advantage when it has to be quick, such as with real-time translations, which can be very useful abroad. Let’s test that right away with the Samsung Galaxy S24. My colleague Ambra, who speaks Italian, is going to call me. Ah, that’ll be her. So I answer and click on the call assistant. “Live translation.” Then I put it on loudspeaker, so you can listen too. There. Hello, Ambra? Hello, Ambra. Hello, how are you? I hope you enjoyed your holiday? I hope you enjoyed your holidays. Okay, well, you can definitely use it, it takes some getting used to, but it works. Ambra, I’ll say goodbye. Goodbye. All providers are researching possibilities as to how AI can take your image and video editing to a new level. For anyone who likes taking photos AI smartphones offer entirely new possibilities for editing your photos. Hey team, smile. This case here at the side bothers me a bit, Now I can just use AI image editing to remove it. And then the missing pixels are calculated. That’s great, I’ll take that. I wonder how the whole thing will develop from here. One thing is certain, that on-device AI is more and more present on our smartphones, and so in our everyday lives too.
