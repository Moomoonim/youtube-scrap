## 스크립트

For me personally, I'd absolutely hate sitting at my computer for so long every day. The stereotype I had is that they just dance around on the ground. This Swisscom project is super exciting for us. Also bringing two different worlds together to collaborate. I expect us to put on a really brilliant show at Energy Air. We really want everyone to get their phones out and go wild. I'm Nino Accardi, Creative Director and 3D Artist at Next Hype. My routine: I get up, go to work, come back home, sleep, get back up, go back to work. When I'm not in front of my screen, I go let some energy out, a little parcours, jumping around. Honestly, I've got no clue who I'm paired up with. We just know they're B-Boys. So I expect some wicked dance moves. I'm Michaela Kvet, aka Checz_o_flex. I'm a dancer, a choreographer, I perform, I battle, and I've danced my whole life. I got into it the very first time at a dance camp. It grabbed me. It also sort of saved me when I was a teenager. It may also be a reason why I'm here at all. And today it's simply my job. So, I don't know anything else or do anything else. Just dancing. I'll be working with other dancers and artists in this project. We're all taking part in this because we totally agree with Swisscom: We can only do this together. When I see them dancing, it just makes me want to get going. That's crazy. Brilliant! Of course, they sit doing this for hours. Swisscom brought two worlds together. It's definitely a challenge. I can really imagine that the hard part about working with the tech team will be that we'll have our own perceptions, and they'll have theirs. For both sides to know what's possible. But we're going to rock it. It'll be so cool.
