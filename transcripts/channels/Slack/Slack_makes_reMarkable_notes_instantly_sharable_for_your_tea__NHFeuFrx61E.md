## 스크립트

The reMarkable paper tablet is where people start the journey of thinking. You get this sort of amazing unstructured free form experience that you need to be creative that then allows you to do your work. But we realized very quickly as that if there are barriers in between where you do your work and where you do your thinking, that's where you lose time but also you lose your ideas, you lose your momentum. The idea of bridging Slack and reMarkable came as a no-brainer. What if we were able to take the notes that you have put together on your reMarkable with this beautiful pen and paper experience? With the Slack integrations you're able to just send it to Slack in one tap. It opens a canvas that has been AI generated based on their notes and then from there they can decide what to do with it. It could be an email, it could be a slide or it could be a brand new user interface. Knowing that we are able to morph an idea into something that is actionable and tangible is very important for us. It's that great idea the momentum that you had you are able to continue with it without stopping. I really look forward to the merging of the creativity space and the productivity space where collaboration is in the middle. That's where I get really excited.
