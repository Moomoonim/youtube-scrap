## 스크립트

AI is transforming the way that your business operates. As companies invest billions in AI, the focus is shifting from designing and training models to running them through AI inference. AI inference turns data into action. Inference happens at a larger scale. It demands efficiency. It requires power. AMD EPYC CPUs and Instinct GPUs are built for this. For smaller, mixed-use deployments, AMD EPYC CPUs deliver up to 3.8 times the performance of the competition. Performance that can help reduce the total number of servers needed. For large-scale inference, AMD Instinct GPUs provide up to 1.3 times the performance of the competition. With industry-leading memory capacity and bandwidth, helping customers optimize costs. More AI inference, fewer servers. Optimized costs. Inference is the next stage of AI, and it's advanced by AMD.
