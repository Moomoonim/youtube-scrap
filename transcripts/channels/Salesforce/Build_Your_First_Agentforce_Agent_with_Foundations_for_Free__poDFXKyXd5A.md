## 스크립트

Salesforce Foundations is a free add-on for Sales or Service Enterprise Edition and Up customers, giving you access to key features across Sales, Service, Marketing, Commerce, Data Cloud, and Agentforce for free. Learn how your employees and customers can leverage Agentforce to enhance their productivity. Agentforce via Salesforce Foundations features a new, simplified UI that provides access to other Salesforce clouds on the left-hand navigation bar. Now let's build our first agent. Navigate to the top right of the screen and click the Quick Settings icon and open Advanced Setup. On the Setup page, navigate to Quick Find in the top left of the screen and type “agents”. A list will appear and you can click Agentforce Agents. In Agentforce Studio, you'll see the previous agents you created at the bottom of the page, and other resources to learn more about using and creating agents. In this demo, we'll go to the top right and click New Agent. The first step in Agent Builder is where you have the ability to create from an existing template or create with Gen AI, a new feature we're deploying. You can create a Sales Development Rep agent to perform lead nurturing 24/7, or a Sales Coach agent who can provide feedback to your sales rep at different stages of the opportunity, or a Setup agent to help with administrative tasks. Lastly, you can create a Service agent to answer customer questions. For our demo, we'll build a Service agent. These are the agents we have live now, but there will be more to come. Click the Next button at the top right of the page to get started building our Service agent. Second, you'll be taken to the Review Topics page. Here you can select what topics you want your Service agent to handle, ranging from Case Management to General FAQ. In this demo, your customer needs to get answers to FAQs based on your business knowledge articles. If an agent does not know the answer to the question, it will hand off to a human rep. To serve this need, our agent will have two topics, General FAQ, and Escalation to a human rep. Let's click Next in the top right, to customize our agent further. Now let's define our agent further. Remember, we are creating this agent to answer customer questions and escalate when necessary. Let's name it, “My First Agent”. Then, enter a description. For role, let's say, “Answer questions for the customer.” Company? Just insert your company name. Finally, select New Agent User from the dropdown, and click the box to enrich your conversation data. Now let's click Next at the top right. For our last step, you will access Data Cloud to provide the data that your Service agent will use to handle customer questions. Now let's click Create in the top right of the screen. You've built your agent and you'll see a conversation bar to the right similar to the default agents we discussed earlier. Now you can ask service questions related to your business. You can also test the agent on an experienced cloud site. Please visit Trailhead to review the modules on how to build your first agent, and learn more about capabilities, and be able to test your agent in an experienced cloud site. Access Salesforce Foundations via our website to learn more, and start building with Agentforce. To get started, visit salesforce.com/crm/foundations
