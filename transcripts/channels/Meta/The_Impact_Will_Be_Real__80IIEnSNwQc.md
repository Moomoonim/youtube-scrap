Some people say the metaverse will only be virtual. One day, this lecture hall will be made of code. And though they're virtual students, what they learn together is real. A surgeon will be able to practice as many times as needed in the metaverse before laying her hands on a real patient. These students aren't really back in 32 BCE, but they can still watch Mark Antony debate in ancient Rome. The metaverse may be virtual, but the impact will be real.
