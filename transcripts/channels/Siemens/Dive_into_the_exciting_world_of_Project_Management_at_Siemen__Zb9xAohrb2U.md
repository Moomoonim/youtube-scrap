## 스크립트

I mean, Siemens project management is really the best job I could ever take. Very dynamic, it's quite an adventure. You meet different people, different lines, different products, different solutions, different projects, that's fun. To deliver state-of-the-art technology and to work all over the world. To be your own CEO of your own project. You can challenge yourself, you can grow, you can gain new capabilities. For me, projects are like an idea, a need of the customer that we need to develop. The path along these 20 years was exciting. I see the solutions that are still working, that are still in place. And this makes me happy, really happy, and makes me continue with this career. A project manager career is really diverse. You will find a big learning path. We have people working all around the world and supporting the project team. You have the pleasure of behind-the-scenes views on the mobility topics, the industry topics, the infrastructure topics. You have really the freedom to build it in a way that it fits best to your strengths. So, this flexibility, when I want to work, can be governed by me and this is super beneficial for me as an employee.
