## 스크립트

The Global Entertainment Awards is a really exciting initiative. It's in its third year now and we're here to shine a spotlight on the creatives who produce content and marketing assets that drive audiences to consume or engage with entertainment. And it's really important that we recognise this whole other section of the entertainment industry. There's a lot of buzz and excitement when the awards go into the final judging stages. All the judging comes together in just a few days before the awards. We have a lot of fun over online interviews. It's one of the best parts of the whole show. Then it's into production where the whole show must be shot, edited and delivered in under a day. The first step is to create all these assets, these graphics that are used on screen and central to the technology to achieve this turnaround is a really robust high-speed solution provided to us by AMD. This starts with the server. The server built by Boston is able to take all the content in very quickly over 10 gig ethernet. We have three amazing machines from AMD, two of which have their W7900 graphics card along with the latest Ryzen Threadripper PRO CPU. Once the first part of the show is done we go straight into ingest. Having such a fast IO with the ability to plug in multiple camera cards and start sending that data down to the server at very high speed means that we can start editing the show before we finish filming it. This year we've opted to use Red Digital Cinema as our key camera technology. R3D gives us amazing dynamic range and the options around high frame rates, the Komodo-X and the V-Raptor are brilliant. By the end of filming the show our editor is already well into the first edit and then our camera team move on to capturing some really fun moments on the media wall. At this point the whole show is stored on the server centrally and anybody can work on any part of the show at the same time. Color, edit, it can all happen simultaneously. Any grading, any noise reduction, anything where you treat the video image you can still play back full 8K raw files absolutely no problem but for the rest of the edit team the job doesn't stop there. It goes straight into behind the scenes social media clips so the rest of the team is still drawing off the server and this is where we can send renders over to the server to be rendered which is also kitted out in AMD's EPYC CPUs. AMD powers our full post-production workflow and incredibly placed it here in a hotel suite in Portugal for two days. The ability to turn around the edit of that show in such a short time frame comes from the team and the technology, the combination of the two, the ability to work quickly and work reliably and AMD provides a system that is fast, robust and delivers everything that you need when you need it.
