## 스크립트

My childhood was very different to my friends at school. I was helping my parents out in the restaurant. They were working long hours to give me and my sister a good life. I remember my dad saying to me, "Got two options. You work in the kitchen for the rest of your life or you go to university, get a job." I knew I want to go into education. I want to study it. I was asked to go to a service now administrator course. I had to work very hard. But when I actually then passed, I was relieved with joy. All that hard work, it's actually paid off. I'm really, really thankful for my parents because they really taught me what hard work and determination is. The advice I would give someone that's considering a career change, if you're passionate, you can get there with hard work. [Music]
