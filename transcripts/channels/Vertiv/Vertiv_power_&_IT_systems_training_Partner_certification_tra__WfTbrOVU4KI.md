## 스크립트

In the power track, partners build the expertise they need to design and deliver solutions across Vertiv portfolio. Through workshops, hands-on practice, and access to configuration tools, they gain in-depth technical skills that make a real difference in customer projects. Always supported by Vertiv experts who are there to guide them step by step. The AE system track offers partners comprehensive learning experience across Vertiv and IT infrastructure portfolio from UPS systems and racks to software and integrated smart solution as well as tools like a Vertiv as a solutions designer. What sets this path apart is the privileged access to advanced tools, engagements in analytical workshops, immersive technology demonstrations at our cutting-edge facility, and high-density showroom. Partners also benefit benefit from a direct collaboration with our expert team of application engineers and product managers who share invaluable field-driven insights to elevate their technical expertise and solution delivery.
