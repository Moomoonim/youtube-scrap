## 스크립트

SPEAKER 1: We're living in a time when AI can act as your assistant, helping with tasks like answering questions and summarizing your emails. But let's be honest, these powerful AI tools can be imperfect. Sometimes they can experience prompt attacks, or attempts to trick them into ignoring system rules and producing unwanted results. That's where Microsoft steps in. We're on a mission to help mitigate these types of manipulations, also known as jailbreaking. You might have heard about people jailbreaking smartphones, unlocking devices to bypass manufacturers' restrictions. This is the same idea. For instance, someone might try to trick AI into revealing private information, or performing unauthorized actions. Indirect prompt attacks are complex and happen when someone hides malicious instructions in emails, documents, or websites, prompting the recipient's AI tools to expose confidential data or to bypass security measures. We take these threats very seriously and are working hard to combat both direct and indirect attacks. We use tools like prompt shields to detect and block malicious prompts in real time. We also rigorously test our AI systems, checking for vulnerabilities to get ahead of and prevent potential threats. Generative AI is reshaping how people live and work. At Microsoft, we're helping developers build AI more responsibly, and working toward a safer digital future for everyone. [ Music ]
