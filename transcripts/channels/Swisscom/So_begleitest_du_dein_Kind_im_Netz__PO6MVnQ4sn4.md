What types of inappropriate content are there? There is various content in the media world that is not suitable for children's eyes. These are the most important ones: Violence and pornography are the classics among unsuitable content. They are also the only ones that are regulated by law. According to the Criminal Code, under sixteen-year-olds are not allowed to see any of this content. Adults are also not allowed to make them accessible. Social media content offers a wide range of entertainment and information. However, this also includes content that is not suitable for children. Cyberbullying attacks and hate speech in the comment section can affect children directly and also put a lot of emotional strain on them. Fear of Missing out (FOMO) is another phenomenon that makes it difficult for children to put the mobile phone away.
