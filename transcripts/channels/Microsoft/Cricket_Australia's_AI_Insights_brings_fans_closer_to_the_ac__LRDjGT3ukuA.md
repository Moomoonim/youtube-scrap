## 스크립트

Cricket is our national sport. It's an Australian pastime. It brings communities together. So fan engagement is at the very core of everything we think about here at Cricket Australia. We're engaging with more than a million fans on the Cricket Australia Live app. The challenge for us is to personalize their experience. When I'm watching a cricket match, I'm paying attention to the battle between bat and ball. It's fascinating, it keeps me involved. It's a pretty nuanced sport. It can speed up or surprise you at any point in the game. And I love to follow along and learn more about the players. AI Insights provides a level of opportunity for all fans, whether it's engaging with the rich statistics and history of the sport or it's just learning the simple rules and regulations. Ease of use and accuracy to check the player stats, to check the match stats, was non-negotiable for us, and Azure OpenAI just hit the mark. Millions of data points that sits in our Azure Cosmos DB are being served to our fans in subseconds. "That's a good strike. He's a strong boy, he's Ryan Harris." I like that I can switch between profiles. So most of the time I'm a Newbie, sometimes I'll switch to Stats Guru. I like to deep dive, especially on great players, what their stats are, what they're known for. I could really see myself finding out information about Test history. I noticed that the AI Insights go as far back as the 1800s. With our Azure infrastructure set up in a way that is scalable, it gives us a whole new avenue to interact with our fans like never before. The frequency on which they use the app, that has increased by 30% from the past season to the current season. Cricket is a game that's in your blood. We're all from different paths of life but we're connected to cricket and the game that we love. AI Insights gives us the ability to supercharge someone's knowledge of the game, make them feel part of a community, and part of the sport.
