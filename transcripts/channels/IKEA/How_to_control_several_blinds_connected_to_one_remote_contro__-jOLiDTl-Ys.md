## 스크립트

KADRILJ FYRTUR TREDANSEN PRAKTLYSING How to control several blinds connected to one remote using the IKEA home smart app Open the IKEA Home smart app and go to Settings. Select Add new device, then choose the option for Blind. Select ‘Blind’ Step 1: Open the hatch on the frame of the blind and slide the battery pack into place. Time to connect the blind. Step 2: Press and hold both buttons on the frame for at least 5 seconds. A white light will blink 4 times and then pulsate. The app will confirm when pairing is complete. All done! You can now control your smart blind via the app.
