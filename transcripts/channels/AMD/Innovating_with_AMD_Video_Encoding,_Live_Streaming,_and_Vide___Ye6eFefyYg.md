## 스크립트

Osprey video is innovative because we design products to solve tomorrow's problems. Chances are if you watch a rocket launch or outer space video, there’s an Osprey product there. Osprey video is the hardware manufacturer for video capture cards and for video live streaming solutions. Our products are widely used in the Department of Defense, from aerial surveillance all the way to secure communications for government and military. So when it comes to innovation, the AMD Zynq UltraScale is the heartbeat of every single one of our new encoding and decoding products. So using AMD's SoC enables us to control everything from A to Z, which makes the system more stable and we also get products to market faster. Our new openGear platform, which is based on the same basic design as our desktop encoders, are quick to deploy to our customers because a lot of them already have openGear frame platform in their workflow. I would absolutely recommend AMD to anybody out there. AMD has been outstanding supporting us from day zero prototyping all the way to full scale production.
