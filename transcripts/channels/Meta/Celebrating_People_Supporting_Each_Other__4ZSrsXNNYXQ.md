## 스크립트

In 2017, Florida was preparing for Hurricane Irma. I decided to take action and respond to my community myself. I was watching the news and I learned about a huge flood that was happening in my hometown back in India. We were struggling with trying to get enough people to give blood. I used Facebook to organize community to be prepared to respond after the storm to each other. Quickly, what came to my mind was to do an online fundraiser. That's why we wanted to use Facebook and the blood donation tool and all the other features to help drive our efforts to get people to give. I probably invited 20 people. Those 20 people invited their friends and it created a whole ripple effect. We all want to help others. Being able to use the platform to help inspire that energy and to see that there are these problems out there that can be solved is very motivating. Everybody should be involved in social impact. We were able to feed over 23,000 people in 7 days. That's the rent we pay to live on this earth. The things you can do to impact others is amazing. We were able to raise a total of 1.642 million dollars. And they used that fund to build bridges, restore houses, and help people buy medicines and food. I have a choice to stand up and walk to another person and offer him help. This is just an incredible way to make a difference. That's why I think it's a really important piece of how we reach out to people to inspire them to take action to save lives.
