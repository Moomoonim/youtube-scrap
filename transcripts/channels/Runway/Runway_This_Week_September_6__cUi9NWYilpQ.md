Next, we're excited to announce that we have shipped a brand new code of paint for the web version of Runway. Light mode is now available to all users and can be toggled via the dashboard or session user menu. For this week's tip and trick, we wanted to show a creative way of adding more micro moments into a single scene. We start by shooting our frame, ideally at a 4K or higher resolution. Because of the resolution, we can then zoom in and crop the subject that we would want to modify. From here, we simply use ALF to generate different scenes across the various crops. Finally, we bring all of the scenes back into our editor. And because ALF keeps all of the camera motion the same, we can piece together our final scene. So, there you have it. A new way to make use of those megapixels in your camera to achieve more creative control right inside of Runway. Next up is our community showcase. We wanted to highlight a piece by Lola Palooa Argentina and DF Entertainment. Using Runway and ALF, they created this incredible marketing video for the upcoming Lola Palooa Festival. It's a great showcase of how creative vision and generative tools come together to make a bold and emotional short that hooks social audiences. Next up is our daily Discord challenge.
