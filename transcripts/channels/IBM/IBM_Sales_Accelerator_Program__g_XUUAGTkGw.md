## 스크립트

The Sales Acceleration Program is where early career professionals could start their career and learn by doing. Real clients, real deals, real impact and real IBM. This year's program is even more immersive. We have tied the connection to our clients. Even better, coaching and mentoring support and practical skills are going to help to grow your career in the future. I finished my degree last year and I started a position in IBM as an intern. I wanted to do something else instead. So I looked for opportunities inside IBM. And I found out about this program. I did my internship in IBM as a consulting designer. And now I changed to become a seller. It's really easy to change for one thing to another. And you really gain a lot of experience, a lot of competence, a lot of things that I've never thought I would be doing. So yeah, I think it's like really a global experience where you can do what you want to do. When I came here for the first time in this building, with all these all new people in this international environment, and saw the place, Valencia, the sea, that was really amazing. Truly amazing. I have heard that sales can be quite competitive, but I think it's always what you make out of it. And I would say here in Valencia, it's a very supportive culture. We really help each other. We support each other, we grow together and I love that. Since joining the program, I have seen some changes. I've built a lot of confidence. The one skill that I learned is being organized. I think it's one of the most underrated skills when it comes to sales, as we are doing a lot of activities and we have to stay on top of what we're doing. Otherwise, things can get messy very, very quickly. I think we all know a lot, but if we share all the things we know, we are unstoppable. So yeah, I love that about Valencia. If you're early in your career. Curious about the intersection between sales and technology. And you want to grow alongside innovators that are shaping our future. This place is for you.
