## 스크립트

Today's AI opportunities are extending beyond the cloud to the edge. AI engines have two primary use cases. First for digital signal processing and secondly for machine learning. They accelerate advanced applications for high performance signal processing and for machine learning inference. They also help remove bottlenecks and optimize performance so that applications can run much more efficiently. AMD AI engines can be found in AMD Versal Adaptive SoCs. These are the same engines also inside AMD's Ryzen AI, which is our leading AI PC processor and has shipped into millions of AI PCs worldwide. AI acceleration can be particularly useful for industrial and automotive applications. In the industrial space, AI acceleration can improve productivity and safety. In factories, it can drive real-time decision making so operations can run more efficiently, safely, and reliably. In the automotive space, AI acceleration can improve real-time performance to enhance safety in cars. All of our AI engines, whether used in PCs or embedded applications, are based on our proven AMD XDNA architecture that's deployed across our broad product portfolio.
