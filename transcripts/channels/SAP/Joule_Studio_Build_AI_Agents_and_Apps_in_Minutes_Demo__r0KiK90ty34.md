## 스크립트

What if the hardest part of building enterprise software isn't the code? What if it's the chaos before the code? You've got AI agents and tools that don't talk to each other. You keep starting over because no one agreed on what you were building. The new Joule Studio is the solution built for this moment. Think of it as an agent harness. The model brings the intelligence and Joule Studio brings everything else, your business processes, your enterprise architecture, SAP's domain expertise and best practices. Joule Studio delivers the speed and simplicity of vibe coding for the enterprise, producing output that is reliable, secure, and SAP-compliant out of the box. We call this intent-based development. First, the developer describes the business challenge in plain language to Joule. In this demo, we're building a complete tariff disruption response solution. starting from a single prompt. A retail manufacturer needs to detect when sudden tariff changes hit their open purchase orders, calculate the margin and customer commitment exposure across SAP and non-SAP systems, and coordinate a cross-team response before the financial impact compounds. Joule immediately activates the intent analysis skill, not just interpreting the prompt, but doing live analysis in parallel across core systems to see if it needs to build an agent, workflow, or application to solve this business problem. Agents in Joule activate MCP servers to access the AI agent hub in LeanIX and start building a solution that inherits the full intelligence of your enterprise landscape. The agent also runs the prompt through the SAP domain models and SAP Knowledge Graph to validate the recommendations against SAP best practices. The analysis confirms the landscape. SAP ERP is the primary data source. Joule generates the idea board by reading your actual process documentation and your real system landscape before producing a single requirement. Now you see that Joule recommends a three-layer solution with an AI agent, an N8N agentic workflow, and extensions. It also leveraged the Signavio integration to understand relevant business processes end-to-end before a single requirement is written. With the solution confirmed, Joule generates a full Product Requirements Document grounded in your processes and your people. It's a shared source of truth that can be used for stakeholder approval and it even estimates the impact on KPIs. The technical specification translates these business requirements into an implementation blueprint complete with mapping of all artifacts that need to be built and assembled. Now we can build. From the specification, Joule starts by generating the trade impact analyst AI agent, which is coded in Python and natively supports the agent-to-agent protocol. When a tariff shock hits, the agent processes the event inputs, queries SAP ERP for PO, supplier, and product data, calculates margin impact, identifies customer commitment exposure, generates a prioritized action list, and identifies duty mitigation options. The LLM equips agents with planning and reasoning capabilities while document grounding anchors the agent in supplier contracts, tariff schedules, and vendor performance history for informed decision-making. Next, MCP servers are added as tools to connect the agent with third-party capabilities, in this case to read delayed orders and if needed to explore other vendors for transport. The agent pulls live external data alongside SAP data, letting it work across your full system landscape, not just SAP. That's an impact assessment that used to take a procurement team three to five days now delivered in minutes with full traceability back to every affected order. Developers can switch to pro-code view and use their AI development tools of choice, for example, VS Code, Claude, or Cursor, and work with any agent framework, such as Langchain, Llama Index, or Pydantic AI. Within minutes, you've gone from an idea to a working agent with intent-based development in Joule Studio, low code to pro code, single agent to multi-agent, SAP to non-SAP, all in one environment using your tools and frameworks of choice and grounded in your business context. Next, Joule builds four agentic workflows covering every phase of the disruption response cycle. The disruption event response coordinator receives the agent's assessment and fans out simultaneously to four teams: procurement, trade compliance, supply chain, and finance, each with structured action items, context, deadlines, and escalation paths. This is one key pattern in Joule Studio. The agent reasons and hands its findings to the workflow layer. In other solutions generated based on customer need, N8N workflows can also embed AI agents that reason within each automated step. Both patterns are available in Joule Studio. The agent handles the reasoning, the workflows handle the response and everything they produce flows into one place: the dashboard your teams will actually use. With Joule Studio, you get one workflow, end-to-end, across every system boundary, including AI agents, deterministic steps, approvals, integrations, and events built for real execution with retries, exceptions, and scale. Now our agent needs a new app to maintain custom data from disruption events, affected purchase orders, action items, and mitigation options. Joule brings in the custom data model integrated with SAP ERP, as well as data products from Business Data cloud. Then it generates the data models and the business services with API calls supporting the AI agent, all following SAP best practices. The UI for the app can be previewed within Joule Studio. With this trade disruption command center, leaders get real-time visibility across everything the agent and workflows are producing: active disruptions, margin at risk, affected purchase orders, response status, all live.
