## 스크립트

[Music] Over 96% of all manufactured goods from everyday items like food packaging to advanced electronics like semiconductors have no viable substitute materials. This can lead to sustainability challenges and supply chain vulnerabilities. There are 10 to the power of 60 possible materials in the universe. But we've explored less than 10 to the^ of 8, revealing an urgent need for innovation. Historically, new materials were discovered through trial and error. Thomas Edison famously tested 6,000 substances for his light bulb filament. Today, researchers and scientists are turning to AI to accelerate materials discovery from years to months, enabling the creation of efficient batteries, biodegradable polymers, and more. But there's a lack of highquality chemical data to train effective AI models. NVIDIA Alchemy is tackling this critical bottleneck. NVIDIA Alchemy's two-step workflow delivers highquality simulation data 1,600 times faster, cutting decades of work to days. First, Alchemy's NIM micros service for batch geometry relaxation determines the lowest energy state of a material or molecule by running hundreds of energy minimizations in parallel. This ensures downstream simulations are performed on chemically stable structures with the lowest energy state at hand. Alchemy's batched density functional theory or DFT micros service can efficiently calculate properties for a given application. The generated data sets can be used to virtually screen and recommend optimal candidates before costly realworld testing. With these capabilities at their fingertips, scientists can explore new chemical spaces effortlessly. Combining AI with simulations gives researchers an efficient, accurate way to discover new materials, empowering them to accelerate innovation across science and industry and build a more sustainable future for all.
