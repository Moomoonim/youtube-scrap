Do you want to be able to send company updates when HubSpot deals are created? Let me show you how to do it automatically using Zapier. To do this task, I'll want to create a Zap. In Zapier, a Zap is a simple way to connect different apps and make them work together automatically. With Zapier's no code app builder, I can create my Zap from scratch. Or with Zapier's new AI co-pilot, I can just describe what I want. I could say something like, "When a deal is closed in HubSpot, post a celebration message on my LinkedIn company page." and it'll build the workflow for me. My zap will then be created. On the lefth hand side, the co-pilot will tell me the steps that I need to take to complete my zap. And if I need to tweak my prompt or troubleshoot anything, I can just type right into the co-pilot. It walks me through the steps. To start, select your trigger. Our trigger is an event that starts any zap. In this case, our trigger is HubSpot. I'll need to log into my account before configuring my steps. To do this, click on the account tab. The first time we set up HubSpot, we'll need to connect it with Zapier. If it's your first time logging in, a link will pop up for you to log into your HubSpot account. And don't worry, Zapier will only perform the actions you tell it to in a given Zap. We'll then need to fill out our trigger apps data. Select the pipeline and stage you'd like to use for your Zap and then click continue. In order to finish setting up the trigger, we'll need to pull in some example data. To do that, click test trigger. Testing our steps ensures that the data we entered is working. You're all set with the first step. Click continue with selected record and we can start creating our action in Zapier. An action is the task that happens automatically after our trigger is set off. Your action event should already be set. So, log into your LinkedIn account just like you did with your HubSpot account. From there, we can start to assign our trigger apps data into the action apps fields. Copilot will automatically fill out our configuration steps with our HubSpot data. And if my data isn't there, I can ask the Copilot to fill it out for me. With that, I can select from the drop-own menu or add my own text to customize my Zap even further. Once that's complete, I'll test the step. Great. This is ready to turn on. Now, whenever a deal is closed in HubSpot, a company celebration message will automatically be sent out to our company, LinkedIn.
