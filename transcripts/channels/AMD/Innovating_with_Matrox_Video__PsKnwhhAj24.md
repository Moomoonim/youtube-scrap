## 스크립트

Matrox has actually been at the forefront of leading customers into an IP environment. The AV industry is actually undergoing a major transformation towards IP-based infrastructure. But many users feel stuck in a closed ecosystem. At Matrox Video, we wanted to change that and give users the freedom to scale and connect without vendor lock-in. And that's why we built our IPMX-enabled products. Our Avio 2, KVM extenders, Vion IP video gateways, and Convert IP series are all part of this new generation of IPMX-enabled tools. We're helping users break free of the constraints of those proprietary solutions. Now they can deploy and manage high-performance AV systems over their existing IP networks. It's a huge shift and one that we're proud to be leading alongside partners like AMD. We use AMD's adaptive SOCs and FPGAs across our entire product line. AMD gives us the performance and flexibility we need to deliver reliable, low-latency AV over IP. The best part of working with AMD is seeing how this collaboration translates into real success for our customers. Together, we're building products that last and that our customers love using.
