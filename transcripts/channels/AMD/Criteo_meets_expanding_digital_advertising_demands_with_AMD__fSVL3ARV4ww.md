## 스크립트

We're always looking for ways to improve the economics of what we do. So in that area, AMD has been a great partner. Digital advertising has always been very compute intensive, so Criteo has been there for 20 years or so. If you're browsing your media websites, the marketplace, or whatever internet service you're using, you've probably seeing one of our ads. We serve around 12 million requests per second. The traffic keeps increasing and the amount of data with it, and we have scaled from 150 to 30,000 servers and it's still hugely compute intensive. So displaying ads for users means that we need to be on the open markets and to do that, we need to be very efficient. We need to be responding to that in under 100 milliseconds. So speed and latency are very, very important in our work. We're a numbers driven, so we test everything we actually write some formula with all the dimensions that we want to have, such as power, performance, cost. In the end, the obvious choice was clearly EPYC servers. We have 130PB on our Hadoop distributed storage system. We have roughly six times the number of products done on Amazon's marketplace. Billions of products that we must ingest from our clients. We leverage machine learning, which runs seamlessly on AMD EPYC. The innovation and the power that AMD provides is really key for us.
