## 스크립트

I was born in Cork. I went to school here. I then went on to college in UCC before joining Lilly as a graduate in 2017. So, one of the unique things about this site in Kinsale is each day at work is different. With the diversity of business, there's a lot of different opportunities that you can expose yourself to and that you can make what you want of your career across a broad range of the manufacturing platforms. When I started as a graduate engineer, I was working in downstream manufacturing. After being an engineer, I got interested in working in management. So, I took on a role as a process team mentor supporting one of the process teams in one of our biotech facilities. And after that, then I progressed into a process team leader role. By joining Lilly, I was able to kind of grow my own career while also continuing to enjoy things outside work. I've a good passion for golf and play a lot of golf staying in Cork in Ireland and being able to continue that while continuing my career was something that I really valued. Lilly are obviously a massive pharmaceutical company worldwide now and having such a big base at Kinsale really affords a lot of opportunities to people. And I now have the opportunity to travel with Lilly to work in the corporate center in Indianapolis. And being able to kind of get that opportunity with such a big company to travel and see the kind of scope of the company even further is something that's really unique. So, I have always wanted to work abroad. I just didn't want to leave Lilly to do that and getting to do that with the company is a great opportunity.
