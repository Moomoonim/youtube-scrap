## 스크립트

Across the NHS, what we're always trying to do is work out how we can deliver the best possible care we can to a population of a million people across Manchester and Trafford, that we serve day-to-day. Clinical medicine is all about communication. All you've got is what you can glean by talking to the patient. You're putting in negatives and positives, trying to drive you down a diagnostic path. It's important to be present with people in the consult. It's sometimes challenging to do that if you're also trying to record things on a computer at the same time. At the end of a consultation, you'll spend ten minutes generating letters and creating notes from a consultation, which is quite time-consuming. With eight patients in a clinic, that easily adds up. The waiting list — it's a real problem at the moment. People often wait maybe up to 12 months to see me in clinic. Any tool that helps with this is a win. Dragon Copilot is a tool that enriches the patient record by making the lives of the clinicians easier. It will listen to the conversation between the clinician and the patient, and will automatically populate the electronic patient record with the relevant information and the next steps as part of that dialogue. On examination today, pulse is 86. Blood pressure 128 over 77. Weight 55 kilograms. We are trying to reduce the number of clicks for a clinician logging on to multiple systems, having to copy and paste information from one system to another. And this is exactly what we are achieving with Dragon Copilot. We use an electronic patient record system called Epic, and Dragon Copilot integrates really well for that. So it means that with just a couple of clicks, it'll automatically pull all the information through into Epic and generate a note for you. It gets you 80% of the way there, reducing the amount of time that you need to put the finishing touches on it. The early indications are, with the adoption of this technology here, we will be able to see and treat up to a quarter-of-a-million new patients every single year, which is significant. And it's not just the time-saving. That interaction with the patient is changed. Often you have technologies taking away that human side of things, but this is actually helping us and increasing that human interaction.
