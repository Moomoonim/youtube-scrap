## 스크립트

Sparkles was a young, adventurous unicorn who lived in the mystical forest. These are the stories I like to read. I didn't always like to read. I would add words and it felt like I wasn’t the same level as my peers. My name is T-Rex and I'm an eighth grader. My dad would write stories to me and they were okay. It wasn't enjoyable until I found Reading Coach! It's fun to pick my own levels and choose my own characters. Even unicorns, especially unicorns. Reading Coach is a really cool to and it makes stories just for me about things I like. I am Ricardo. I am a teacher. I am T-Rex's dad. I see reading coach as a tool that just brings people together in my opinion. At least it did for me and my wife and my daughter. Reading isn't just about words. It's about confidence and belonging.
