## 스크립트

Balancing athleticism and artistry is, for me, about the confidence of not being perfect. INSTINCTS I've been working for where I'm at right now for about 16 years now. My approach to practice, to take training more serious as an athlete. I'm at the right maturity to approach all these competitions. I trust the fact that I know if I go to the right that my hand is gonna catch me. My elbow is gonna catch me. My head is gonna catch me. I know that my body is trained to go into any direction that I want to go. The instinct of reacting in the moment is a trained instinct at the end. It doesn't really matter at the end if you win that battle, but if you really touched your audience. That really feels good and that's a better feeling than having medals. How much time do I actually want to use for my artistic development compared to my physical development? That time management is something I'm still working on. I got this coin from my grandma when breaking was announced to become Olympic. So yeah, the coin means a lot to me. And, it stands for to keep dreaming. When I have the freedom and no judgment, that's when I turned out the best as a dancer. As long as we're working towards something, we have a motivation. That motivation keeps us, keeps us alive, keeps us going for it. There's no finish line. It's part of life, not to have all the answers.
