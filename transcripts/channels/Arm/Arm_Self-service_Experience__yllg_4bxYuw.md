## 스크립트

Welcome to Arm's self-service experience, designed to empower you with seamless access to all the resources, tools, and services you need. Here's how you can easily navigate our services to progress your SOC design smoothly. Once the agreement with Arm is signed, your nominated project lead receives a welcome email explaining how to securely register for an Arm ID. Registration is quick and easy. Within seconds, they can access Arm's products and services their company is entitled to. They use their Arm ID to log in to their Arm account, where they can manage their profile, update their details, manage subscriptions, and see all they have access to in one place. They follow a guided journey, starting with an overview guide and onboarding videos. While watching the onboarding video, the project lead learns how to assign access to different roles using the access management portal. They log in to the Arm management portal and assign download rights to the relevant users. If they already know which IP they will use in their design, they can log in to the product download hub to get the products and resources needed. If they're still evaluating different options, the design process begins with the SOC architect using Arm IP Explorer to search and compare different IPs and make an initial selection. The IP selection is further refined by running simulations to test different high-level configurations using their own custom software workloads. They create a high-level design concept to visualize the IP blocks, check compatibility and connectivity, and get an initial estimate on power and area. They complete the configuration and export the configured RTL, SOC diagram, interface specification files, and design checklists. Those files are shared with the hardware team to start the design work. The team uses the design checklists for additional guidance throughout the design process. When they need more detail about a specific check, they visit the Arm documentation hub to get manuals and guides, helping them choose the right configuration. While reading the manual, they identify gaps in their knowledge, so they access Arm's on-demand training platform, where they can watch training videos to learn more about the products they are using in their design. After the training, they have a question about their specific use case, so they access the Arm support hub to raise a support case and quickly get personalized advice from an Arm application engineer. They can also use the support hub to access a knowledge base of frequently asked questions, browse resolved cases, and receive timely updates on their support requests. At Arm, we're committed to making your experience as smooth and efficient as possible. Log in today and unlock the full potential of our self-service customer support experience.
