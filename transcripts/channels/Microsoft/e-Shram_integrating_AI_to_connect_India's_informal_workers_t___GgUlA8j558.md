## 스크립트

India has more than 400 million unorganized workers. They are earning only day-to-day wages and not getting any benefits. We need to shift the people from the informal to the formal sector. That would help us to develop our economy. Through the initiative of e-Shram, the National Unorganized Workers Database was developed, and we are able to provide those workers with social security benefits, also giving them access to the National Career Service portal. NCS is a one-stop solution for job seekers, employers, training providers, and counselors. We have AI-based matching between the job seeker's skills and the employers, so any informal worker can go to the NCS portal and match with the right employer. NCS has an AI resume builder, and if a person is lacking certain skills, the AI tool helps them to develop those skills by giving them recommendations. We were expecting millions of unorganized workers to register in the system. So we wanted to have scalability, reliability, and security. Microsoft Azure met all these qualifications. In addition to that, Azure OpenAI has become a game changer for unorganized workers. Earlier I worked for a company through a contractor. So I went to the NCS portal and entered my job preferences. I got a driver's job and I'm earning 50% more now. It's a transportation company based in Delhi. I'm quite young, I am capable of a lot of things, I am getting to learn a lot from this platform. And I'm confident that it can help me build a stable life. Around 150 million workers are now on the NCS portal. We are also now trying to bring in more and more schemes to benefit these workers. AI is supporting us to provide opportunities for the invididuals and youth of the country.
