## 스크립트

Unified National Networks or UNN was established with the mission of modernizing and consolidating Brunai telecommunications infrastructure. One of our key achievements in this journey has been the successful implementation of this new data center which is a major step forward in improving service delivery and preparing for the future demands in digital connectivity. Some of the key challenges include the need for enhanced redundancy and greater energy efficiency. Additionally, ensuring business continuity and resilience against potential disruptions was a top priority. Investing in this new data center was a strategic decision to address these challenges and future proof our operations. Selecting the right partner for this project was a critical decision. We focus on several key considerations, technological excellence, reliability, scalability, and a strong track record in delivering data center solutions. Vertive stood out due to its proven expertise in critical infrastructure, innovative solution tailored to UN needs and a strong commitment to sustainability. UNN was embarking on an ambitious national digitalization journey. That mean not only upgrading the telecom infrastructure but also building a resilient foundation to support 5G, cloud services and smart government initiative. So one of the key challenges was time. They needed a scalable reliable facilities up and running fast. We recommended what integrated modular solution because it offer a fasttrack approach to building high performance data centers without compromising on quality or uptime. It was a right fit for Bunai forward-looking goals. With a vertive prefab data center solution, UNN was able to accelerate their deployment timeline significantly compared to traditional constructions. The modular approach meant that much of the system could be pre-fabricated and tested offsite then quickly assembled on site reducing construction risk and downtime. UNN pre-fabricated data center boasts stateofart high availability data center facility in line with Brunai's digitization road map with capacity and services to provide geor redundancy. It is also the first ecoefficient data center in Brunai designed to increase efficiency and reduce emissions by using energy efficient technologies, renewable energy sources, optimized cooling systems and practices that reduce waste. Today the prefab DC houses 28 racks in total with 200 of them allocated for commercial or business use. In total, UNN is capable to accommodate total of 1.5 megawatt of IT load. To cool down the critical IT system, YN has dimensioned the cooling system at 2 megawatt where each data halls have one cooling systems on standby to ensure high availability and resiliency of the data center. The design gives unn the flexibilities to scale in the future while maintaining high efficiencies and reliabilities from day one. Unlike traditional builds which can take years and face a lot of on-site complexity, our modular solutions was mostly factory built. This minimizes construction time and ensure consistent quality across every component. What set this apart is how quickly it can be deployed, the scalabilities and how tightly integrated all the subsystems are from power to cooling and to monitoring in Brunai and across Southeast Asia. We are enabling critical infrastructures through end-to-end solution that deliver reliability, scalability and speed. I want to extend a big congratulation to UNN and the entire project team. This new facility is more than just a data center. It is a symbol of Brunai digital futures. We are proud to be part of this journey and looking forward for continued collaboration. This new data center marks a significant milestone for UNM and for Brunai's digital transformation efforts, enabling innovation and investment opportunities and economic growth. As his majesty the Sultan of Brunai has highlighted, digital transformation is integral to Brunai's progress emphasizing the critical need for Brunai to embrace digital technology as a key driver for economic growth, social development, and achieving the smart nation vision. UNN is proud to be contributing to the nation's vision of becoming a digitally advanced economy. [Music]
