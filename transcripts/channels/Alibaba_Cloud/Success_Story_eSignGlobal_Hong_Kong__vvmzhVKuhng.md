## 스크립트

When crossber contracting meets AI, how to achieve both efficiency and security? Lawyers from Dubai use Global's AI template to draft contracts. A crossber contract only need 3 minutes. Just one click to send. Customers in Hong Kong can view the contract on their phones. AI intelligent translation. It supports more than 40 languages. Users can view the original text and translation results at the same page. AI risk identification. It can quickly identify unfair terms, legal risks, liability for breach of contract and other key issues. Esign global support over 70 countries and regions. Multiple identity authentication methods. AI summary and AI comparison from generating translations to risk control to make global signing zero distance. ESG Global redefineses the boundaries of trust with technology. Sign smarter, trust easier.
