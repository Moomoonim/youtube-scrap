## 스크립트

Being the only black person, and black woman, in most of the spaces I've navigated, it was very isolating. So, I think I probably would always eventually create something that would bring more people that look like me together. Black Girl Festival is a festival and platform dedicated to black women, girls and non-binary people. We have created a space where so many black women, customers, consumers and people who are just interested in supporting black businesses were kind of congregating together. Diversity isn't just a buzzword. We need to be actively doing things, not just because we're being always urged to, but because it's right. I think the thing that people probably don't know about LinkedIn is that it is a space for creative professionals. I’m sharing articles and writing pieces. I'm letting my creative expression shine through. The change that I want to see in the workplace is for more access, more opportunities, and more money being given to black business owners.
