## 스크립트

Our experience shows that manufacturers can leverage AI to create what we call the factory of the future and unlock up to 60% productivity improvements. [music] We're talking about an agile and automated factory that leverages in combination new technologies and also conventional ways of optimization such as lean processes to reach out superior productivity at a much lower level of investment. In fact, we have seen the return on investment payback period for cuttingedge factory automation drop from a typical 5 to seven years in the past to just 1 to 3 years. Productivity improvements are vital as manufacturers face several challenges today. We expect a global labor shortage of 250 million by the middle of the century. Labor and energy costs are rising and competitive pressures are growing. Factory of the Future responds to these challenges by combining agentic physical and virtual AI with conventional process and layout optimization all in one overarching factory design. It's not about implementing individual use cases. It's about combining use cases to change end toend workflows. That's where the full potential of such a transformation sits. The factory of the future needs more than just tech foundations. Of course, it needs the right people foundations, including the capabilities and governance to support these hyperproductive autonomous workflows and most important to scale them across the factory and the entire business.
