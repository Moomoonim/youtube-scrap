## 스크립트

Ask a designer: Hans Blomquist on living and working at home Working from home is the new normal for many of us, and of course that’s a challenge when it comes to your home, and the space you live in and this home we’re going to talk about today is an open plan home We had to come up with ideas and solutions for how to have two working spaces for the people who live here, but also working spaces for friends and family who join them in the house. One lesson I want you to take with you from this home is to divide an open space into different zones, so you can have different activities in the same room. We have two set work stations: one for the man living in the house. He works as an accountant, so he only needed a set desk with a comfortable chair. The women who lives here is an illustrator, so she has a sit and stand desk with a stool that you can raise and lower. More areas in this home that we wanted to include were, of course, a comfortable living area both for themselves to hang out, but also for easy coffee breaks when a lot of people are working together. We included a dining table that has both work chairs and normal dining chairs, so you can sit comfortably when coming to work in this space. we have added in trolleys so they can easily can pack away their things when they leave for the night. We also have a library area which is used for both working and hanging out, where they have storage for all their books, memories from trips and decorative items. We added glass doors so you have a dust-free space. If it’s possible, I do recommend to have a space in the home (especially the bedroom) to be completely computer and telephone-free, to be able to relax, hang out and sleep really well.
