## 스크립트

For the 2025 Global Entertainment Awards, AMD sits at the centre of the fastest production workflow we've ever used. Fantastic quality video files land directly into our edit, meaning our post-production starts almost at exactly the same time as the event itself. The Global Entertainment Awards is a real celebration of creativity, centred around the people who create all the marketing assets for film, games and television. We film the awards on a Saturday, and we have just under 24 hours to deliver the finished package to our media partner deadline. It all starts with these latest cameras from Blackmagic Design. The Ursa 12K seniors we have on our set provide excellent dynamic range and incredible resolution. Alongside these, we have the latest Pixis cameras, which are connected to high-speed Wi-Fi, enabling proxy files to be sent to the server via the Blackmagic cloud. So the heart of the entire setup here is the Boston server, which runs on AMD EPYC CPUs. All of the Ursa cameras are connected via native 10 gig ethernet, which means it's handling a central file store for all of the editors and all the cameras to interface with at the same time. The cameras are all connected to Blackmagic cloud, so the proxies are being uploaded real-time so the editor can actually start editing the files as they're being shot. So as soon as filming stops, we can transfer the files from the camera directly to the server. And then the exciting part, our workstations, powered by AMD Ryzen Threadripper processors, are immediately able to access files from within our DaVinci Resolve shared project. And in just a short time, our colorist is taking over. Having our production powered by AMD and in collaboration with Blackmagic Design really supercharges the whole process. Data wrangling, dealing with camera memory cards, suddenly all these things are a thing of the past. AMD advances the Global Entertainment Awards through a reliable, fast and innovative solution where hardware and software come together to deliver the show in under 24 hours.
