## 스크립트

I don’t know how you feel, but routines are a fundamental part of my daily life. That is exactly why I love the fact that the new E-Class allows me to create my very own personalized routines. So, all of you tech enthusiasts out there just think: Smart home is smart car. Let me give you an example. We will use this template as an example. If you choose "Warm and cozy", once I activate this, it turns on the seat heating at a certain outside temperature to make sure that in the car I am all warm and cozy when it is cold outside. But this is the thing: I can also personalize routines to my own liking. I can set temperatures and moods in accordance to certain times of day, days of the week, outside or inside temperatures. If you are wondering how the vehicle knows which profile to activate and which routines to set, the answer lies right here. This is my infrared camera with face recognition, so it will know who is in the driver’s seat and which personal profile to connect to. But that was only the very first fact. I have a few left to show you why the E-Class is the perfect business companion. Speaking of cameras: for the first time ever, a selfie and video camera in a Mercedes-Benz can be used for business conferences. As soon as you start driving, the video is switched off but the call will still remain on. But enough fooling around! Back to business, we have yet another novelty. We no longer need to depend on Apple CarPlay or Android Auto. We now have our own app store integrated into the Mercedes me Store. Once again, you can personalize and adapt everything to your very own liking. You can add Webex or Zoom or entertaining apps like YouTube or TikTok. Of course, the new E-Class is also equipped with the latest assistance systems to make sure that I always get from A to B safely. The one feature I value most is the optional DIGITAL LIGHT. It projects auxiliary markings and warning symbols onto the road directly in front of me while I am driving. That means I can keep both eyes focused on the road while I am always up to date with the most essential information about my journey. The new E-Class also has a lot to offer to keep me relaxed and safe on the road. I have linked my smartwatch to the E-Class and now the two of them can monitor my vital signs. When I become tired on a long, strenuous or monotonous journey, they can adapt the temperature or the ambient light to keep me fresh and energized. That way, again, I will stay safe on the road. Did you also know that your smartwatch as well as smartphone can be used as a key to the new E-Class? Pretty neat! With that fact, we can close by saying that it has become clear that this perfect business companion also brings personalization to perfection. It is very unlikely that you will ever come across two same E-Classes because they learn and grow along with you so they are all super individual. Plus, it is really good looking, isn’t it? Take your time, have a look around. I need to get back to work. I will see you soon.
