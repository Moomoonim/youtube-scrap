## 스크립트

Our work to become people and planet positive continues In FY22 we decreased our climate footprint by 12% compared to the baseline year FY16 including a significant increase in the share of renewable electricity for retail and production And we’re enabling people to save electricity and money at home SOLHETTA, for example, is more affordable and 35% more energy efficient than previous IKEA LED bulbs and comes in even more functions, shapes and sizes We’re enabling people to reduce water consumption as well ÅBÄCKEN water nozzle, for example, can save up to 95% of tap water used We’re designing and redesigning products to last longer Like BILLY, a best-seller since 1979, which has been redesigned to be easier to assemble and reassemble And we’re developing a global offer of circular services to make it easier for customers to prolong the lifespan of IKEA products When it comes to wood, we’re continuing to use only FSC-certified or recycled wood And we’ve reached a share of 15% for recycled wood We published one of the first-ever outdoor air pollution footprints across a business’ value chain And we’ve strengthened our commitments to tackle inequality and make respect for human rights a foundation for our business contributing to more resilient societies and continuing our focus on children as a particularly vulnerable group Read more about our progress in the latest IKEA Sustainability Report and Climate Report FY22
