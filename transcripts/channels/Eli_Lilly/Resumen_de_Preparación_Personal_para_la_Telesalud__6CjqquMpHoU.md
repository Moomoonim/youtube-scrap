## 스크립트

Below is a three-step preparation guide to help you get ready for your virtual appointment. To be prepared, be punctual, know the day and time of your appointment, and use your calendar to set personal reminders. Have your medications on hand during your virtual appointment and have your numbers ready before your scheduled appointment. You may be asked about your A1C results, blood glucose levels, or weight. By following these three steps, it will be easier for you and your healthcare professional to get the most out of your virtual appointment.
