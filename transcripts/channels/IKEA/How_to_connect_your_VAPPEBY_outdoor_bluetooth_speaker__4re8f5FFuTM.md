## 스크립트

How to connect to your VAPPEBY outdoor speaker lamp Press and hold power button until light starts flashing Select VAPPEBY Outdoor on your bluetooth enabled device To switch to a different device, press and hold the Bluetooth button on the speaker Select VAPPEBY Outdoor on the new device Rotate for volume control Press light button once for 100% light Press once more for dimmed light Press once more to turn the light off Grab the handle and bring the speaker on the go
