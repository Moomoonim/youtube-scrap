## 스크립트

The fourth edition of Aruhan Social Innovation Awards 2025 is looking for social innovations that leverage technology to create lasting change. We are seeking innovations that leverage technology to help communities access better healthcare, improve quality of life, and prevent diseases. Access includes enabling remote care, making treatment more affordable, innovations that improve quality of life through effective diagnosis and treatment, and empathetic mental health care solutions, innovations that harness technology for prevention to promote wellness, early detection, and eradication of diseases. Winning entries will receive prize money of up to rupees 50 lakh plus mentorship and incubation. Visit infosys.com/rohan to know more and submit your application before 15th June 2025. [Music]
