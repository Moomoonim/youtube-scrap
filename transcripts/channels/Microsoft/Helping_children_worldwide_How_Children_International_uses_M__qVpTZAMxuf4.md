## 스크립트

(gentle music) - I first started in Children International as a sponsored participant when I was seven years old, and I worked here for 15 years now. I translate letters from the sponsors to the participants and everything related to special donations that they send to the children. - Working with over 200,000 kids through empowerment programs, education, health programs, with the ultimate goal of getting these kids out of poverty. We operate in 10 countries globally, and so you're looking at a wide variety of atmospheres or environments we operate in. So the challenges are how do you bring technology to those places. Over the past few years, we've had a drastic change in how we leverage technology. We've simplified our systems by partnering with Microsoft. - We made a move from an on-prem data center to Azure Cloud. - [Tim] Also, Dynamics CRM provides us the ability to look at the youth and families we work with from a customer perspective, allowing us to capture the information we need that helps map to the different programs we have. - Copilot Studio agents have been a strategic enabler because we're able to scale without adding head count. Our field offices are creating these micro solutions and automations within sometimes days or even hours. - I had the chance to create an agent that is dedicated to translation. I also created an agent for content verification. In the past, we used to translate letters one by one. Now, with Copilot, we have managed a way to translate them in bulk. If we used to spend maybe eight hours doing one activity, and now with Copilot, we spend one hour, then the other seven hours can be used more into the community and give them a more personalized treatment. - They're being the change agents, they're the ones that are making the difference, creating that environment to be the multipliers in their communities. - The opportunity that they have given me to grow personally, professionally, that's something that I'm very thankful for. - [Audience Members] Yay! (audience applaud)
