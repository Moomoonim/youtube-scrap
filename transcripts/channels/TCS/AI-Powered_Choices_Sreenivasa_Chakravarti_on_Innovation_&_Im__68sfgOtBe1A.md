They allow the differentiation to be brought in. Layering it with AI of different types, whether it's industrial AI or it is cognitive along with generative AI creates that significant edge. Pharma products, drugs which used to take several years to launch into the market can be done much faster. A car that has to be designed what used to take definitely 4 years today has been achieved in a year.
