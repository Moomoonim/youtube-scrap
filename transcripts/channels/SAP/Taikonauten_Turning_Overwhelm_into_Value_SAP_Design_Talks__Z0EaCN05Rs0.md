## 스크립트

I'm Jonas Lempa. I'm one of the two owners of Taikonauten, a Berlin-based digital agency creating incredible user-centered digital products and services and my role is there being the Managing Director of Future and Innovation. Taikonauten is a digital agency and innovation consultancy focusing on user-centered digital products and services and helps every organization to innovate in their digital ecosystem. So design from my perspective or from the perspective of how it comes from a certain area is problem solving. It's just about the medium you're taking to solve these problems. My inspiration comes from every day. How do I cross the street? Is this sound loud enough for me? Why is car design so freaking old when you sit in it and you buy a new car? Why is this product cycle so different? And I think this inspiration is everywhere because all the things that I see I would like to solve is inspiration enough for Taikonauten and for me around to look at that from different perspectives. For me, I need to touch things, I need to feel things, I need to try out things. And experiments is something we really focus on as Taikonauten where we research and where we develop, where we create something and try something out because this try and error topic is super important and super helpful because everything else is theoretically possible but you need to look at if it's practically possible to identify what kind of things are influencing businesses, influencing tech, influencing users. And every temporary trend can become a mega trend but not every mega trend can become a thing that influences your world. We are especially part of the moment when it's not necessary anymore to work with us because when we achieve this point we made it possible that the teams we are collaborating with and the digital products we are building or co-creating does not need our input anymore.
