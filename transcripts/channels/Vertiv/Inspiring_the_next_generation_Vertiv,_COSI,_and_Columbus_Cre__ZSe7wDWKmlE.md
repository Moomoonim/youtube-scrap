## 스크립트

[Music] STEM day is a program where Verdivive, the Columbus crew, and COSI partner together to try and connect the dots between science, technology, engineering, and math and the real world, real life applications. During this program, Veriv and the crew will be leading about 80 students through an interactive behind-the-scenes tour of lower.com field where these students will have the opportunity to not only witness, they'll be able to actively engage with some of the critical data center technologies that Verda provides the crew with to enable them to be successful both on and off the field. STEM day gives us the opportunity to expose our young members to different job avenues in a fun way to make them want to experience STEM and what it has to offer. &gt;&gt; At COSI, we look to engage, inspire, and transform our community. Steam day is a great opportunity for us to engage in the community, to engage with the next leaders of our future and our next steam leaders of tomorrow.
