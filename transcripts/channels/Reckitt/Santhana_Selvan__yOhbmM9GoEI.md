my name is santana and i am a volunteer for hyderabad relief riders we are a group of cyclists who have been delivering essential supplies to those affected by kovid i usually start by answering calls asking what people need then i will arrange a relief rider to get it to them if i am delivering myself i will get ready and set off to pick up items around the city we often collect medicines but also free food parcels and even oxygen basically if it can be delivered by bike we will deliver it we are helping people at a time when they need it most a lot are elderly or vulnerable and they are really grateful for what we do for them if i can use the freedom i enjoy in a positive way that can only be a good thing [Music]
