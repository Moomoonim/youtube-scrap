## 스크립트

Hello? Anybody there? Today we’re talking about the metaverse. In 2021, the world went haywire and people were talking about a digital revolution. Since then, the boom has slowed considerably. But what is the metaverse? The term metaverse has been around for ages. It first appeared in 1992 in the dark science fiction novel “Snow Crash” by Neil Stephenson. The term is defined in very different ways. We define the metaverse as a three-dimensional digital space where people from all over the world meet up. It is a social place, where you use an avatar to move around, interact with others or do your own thing. The best-known platforms are Fortnite, Decentraland, Minecraft and Sandbox. You can do a lot more than just play there. Some online portals reckon that for certain plots of virtual land, up to 2 million dollars have changed hands. There are more stories like this and a few pretty successful ones. For example, rapper Travis Scott played a concert in front of 45 million people in the shooter metaverse Fortnite. Swisscom created its own Fortnite map in 2023, where users defend Swisscom Hero City, overcome challenges and were able to apply for an apprenticeship at the end. There are a lot of different platforms. That could be why the Metaverse has not yet gained widespread acceptance. Platforms have no interest in merging and it is difficult to keep track of them all. Another hurdle are the gadgets you need for certain metaverses. Meta, which is Facebook founder Mark Zuckerberg’s company, runs a metaverse that merges with reality and can only be entered with VR glasses. They currently cost around 500 francs. What does this mean for the future of metaverses? The digital spaces are relevant for many people. We just have to find the right form for it. In any case, it’s all very exciting.
