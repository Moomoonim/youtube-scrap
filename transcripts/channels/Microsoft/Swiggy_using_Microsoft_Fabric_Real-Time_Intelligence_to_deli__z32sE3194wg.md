## 스크립트

Swiggy is one of India's leading food-delivery and quick-commerce companies. We are present in over 700 cities, have hundreds of thousands of drivers, and serve over 100 million customers. Our customers really care about speed, selection, and price. It's very difficult for me to shop for groceries because of the traffic and I have very busy schedules to work in. I really can't be waiting for an app to deliver after 30 minutes. Instamart is our quick-commerce platform where customers can order items across grocery and home essentials from over a thousand dark stores. And all of this needs to be delivered in ten minutes. Delivering in ten minutes is challenging. To do this, there's a lot of data behind every decision we make. We need to understand traffic signals, where our riders are, and gather all of this data and analyze them in very quick time. In the past, we have to take data from different sources, transform it, work on it to make it meaningful for the business. And it used to take more than a few hours to get there. Microsoft Fabric with Real-Time Intelligence has helped us reduce data latency from minutes to seconds. It has also helped our analytics team to get their query running from hours to within minutes. Fabric RTI has helped us track millions of orders per day across their lifecycle to provide our customers with accurate order status. It has improved demand prediction and inventory management. Now we have better visibility at our dark stores, which helps us serve our customers satisfactorily. We can reach the customer faster. Day by day we can do more orders. So this technology captures everything; it's a very good thing. If they are showing eight minutes or ten minutes, they're going to keep that and that's important for me because I plan my day around it. I would rather spend some time with my people rather than running around doing errands. I love that experience.
