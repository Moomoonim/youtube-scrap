## 스크립트

Do you want to have X-ray vision? Well, with a Nissan car, you can get pretty close. Check out this Invisible Hood View. It allows you to see what's directly in front of the car's hood and what's around the front tires. No more hitting the curbs! Unless you're into that. But the superpowers don't stop there. There's also the front and rear-view, You’ve always got visibility on the road ahead. And when in reverse, your car’s watching your back. But in the front, there’s also the Front Wide View, which gives you up to 180 degrees of vision. And of course, there’s an Around View, where you see 360 degrees around your car.
