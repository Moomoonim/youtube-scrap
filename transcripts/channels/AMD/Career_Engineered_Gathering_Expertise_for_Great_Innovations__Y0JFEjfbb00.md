## 스크립트

The point I realized I can do the engineering work is the thesis project I have done in Leuven University for undergraduate. It’s a mixing panel to tune the different frequencies of the different sounds. That’s the point I realized my expertise, my interest would be really on the engineering part, on the coding part. I joined AMD in 2011. I've been always with the SOC team. I started with the consoles and worked on the dGPU products, And then when MI Instinct production started, I had the privilege to contribute to that. And also AMD promote for diversity, including the women engineers, women leadership. What I would tell my younger me is: keep doing what you were doing. No matter what career path you are in, it’s a matter of accumulating the experience. All those experiences and expertise, knowledges I’ve gained, right now, is putting me in the right position where I can contribute with great innovations, which makes me excited every day to go to work.
