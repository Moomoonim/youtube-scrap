## 스크립트

Albert Heijn is 138 years old, and we are the number one food retailer in the Netherlands. We have over 1,200 stores and we serve nearly five million customers every single week. Our customers have less time, want to eat more healthily, and have very specific needs. Steijn is a digital assistant in our app that is answering that key question, "What's for dinner tonight? How can I help you?" Steijn is powered by AI and we leveraged several tools from Microsoft to really empower all the technology behind it. We used several tools within Azure AI Foundry, to leverage the 20,000 recipes that we have in our database, and we vectorized them, so we are able to present them on the front end. That really helped us because there was a lot of testing just before we went to production and Azure AI Foundry was perfect for that. Because of all these tools, Steijn was built in a couple of months and not years. I would rate myself as a cook, I think probably an eight, but I only follow recipes. In the past, I normally took 30 minutes to find a recipe, but now with Steijn, I just add three of my favorite ingredients to the app and it gives the best suggestion for me and my family. And saves me up to two hours a week. The most challenging part about planning and making meals is time. It's a stressy moment. I have to pick up my kids at five, and at four o'clock I still have to think about what to eat. I use the Scan &amp; Kook feature within the Albert Heijn application. I take a photo of my fridge and then it makes some suggestions of recipes I could make. Happy family. Our dream for Steijn is that people at some point come up and say, "I didn't know how to cook a healthy meal anymore, but now, due to Steijn, I'm helped every single day." So I think Steijn can really make a difference in our customers' lives.
