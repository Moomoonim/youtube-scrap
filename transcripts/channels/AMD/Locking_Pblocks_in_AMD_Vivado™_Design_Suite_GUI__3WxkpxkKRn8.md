## 스크립트

Hello and welcome. In this short video we will take a look at how to&nbsp;lock the Pblocks in place in the graphical user interface of AMD Vivado Design Suite. In Vivado Design Suite 2025.1, we introduced the ability to lock Pblocks in place. When the Lock Pblocks icon is enabled, the Pblocks cannot be moved in the GUI. This feature prevents accidental Pblock movement while navigating or working in the&nbsp;device window. If you use Pblocks in your device implementations, you may have encountered the following issue: While navigating in the device view window, you attempt to zoom into a specific area within a selected Pblock. However, rather than zooming as expected, your mouse click causes the Pblock to move. As a result, you have to exit the Pblock resource selection pop-up deselect the Pblock, and then repeat your zoom action. In Vivado Design Suite 2025.1, we’ve introduced a new feature that allows you to lock Pblocks in place. With this function enabled, when you navigate in the device view window and initiate a zoom action within a selected Pblock, the tool will perform the zoom as expected without moving the selected Pblock. This enhancement makes navigation smoother and helps prevent accidental modifications to your Pblocks. If you later need to move Pblocks around, you can simply unlock the Pblocks by deselecting the Lock Pblocks icon. Once unlocked, selected items will once again be moved with the mouse click-drag-release action. Thank you for watching!
