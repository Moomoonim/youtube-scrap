## 스크립트

When I speak with leaders around the world about the future, they ask me about dramatic events. How can I prepare for a breakthrough in nuclear fusion or a war in low orbit? But these aren't the scenarios that leaders should be focusing on. Black Swan events are by their nature low probability and cannot anchor your organization's strategy. Instead, leaders should prepare for futures that are actually on the table, grounded in data, with strategic choices that need to be explored today. For our latest report, Beyond Tomorrow, we developed four scenarios for 2050 that are contrasting but plausible. Imagine a world where global trade falls back to Cold War levels, defense spending nearly triples from today, and the World Trade Organization no longer exists. That's our Battling Blocs scenario, and it's built from trends already in motion today. Contrast that one with AI Abundance, where multilateral cooperation on technology regulation drives deep global integration. Both are potential futures. Each requires a very different strategy. The decisions leaders make in the next five years must be informed by rigorous foresight as they will shape the next 25.
