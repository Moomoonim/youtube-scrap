## 스크립트

(bright music) - [Presenter] Goodwill of New York and New Jersey is on a mission to secure employment opportunities for people with disabilities and/or other barriers to employment. GoodTemps is Goodwill's staffing arm. The team is responsible for working with potential employers and finding good candidates as roles open up. A crucial part of this process is reviewing and sharing resumes for the job seekers that GoodTemps has identified as a good fit. But job seekers send their resumes in a wide variety of styles and file formats, slowing down decision-time for the hiring team. Meet Lilia, a placement coordinator for GoodTemps. A job has opened up for an administrative assistant, and Lilia thinks Carter, John, and Louisa could be good fits, but each of their resumes presents information in a different order, doesn't emphasize the right skills, and contains their own contact information instead of GoodTemps. By the time Lilia could reach out to each of these candidates for an updated resume, the job may be filled by someone else. In the past, Lilia would spend time manually processing the resumes, but with Resume Builder, developed in collaboration with Microsoft, Lilia can process hundreds of resumes in a matter of minutes. GoodTemps staff can import an existing resume and trigger an AI-enabled process that makes formatting changes, converts the resume into a PDF, and emails a clean copy for human review before submission. Resume Builder helps staff by automatically putting a professional polish on what is submitted to employers, helping hiring managers quickly review resumes so they can make faster decisions, and for those seeking employment, improving their chances of landing the job. (gentle music)
