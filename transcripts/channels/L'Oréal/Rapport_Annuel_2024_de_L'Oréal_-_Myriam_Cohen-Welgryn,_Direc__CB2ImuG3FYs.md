At the forefront of skincare innovation, L'Oréal's dermatological beauty division collaborates with 310,000 healthcare professionals to offer dermatological products and services that change people's lives. Innovation, our cutting-edge research has once again enabled us to create innovations that truly make a difference and change people's lives. This year, I would like to mention Mélab 3, the biggest innovation in the history of the brand of the posed rock. Its patented molecule, measile, is designed to achieve the efficacy of dermatological reference standards against hyperpigmentation, while ensuring high tolerance. Next, I would say gratitude. I would like to thank all the healthcare professionals with whom we are working hand in hand to create innovations that improve people's lives on a daily basis. Of course, I also thank the fantastic teams at L'Oréal Beauté dermatologique. Despite the turbulence that marked the year 2024 around the world, their resilience and agility made the difference. With comparable growth of +9.8%, L'Oréal dermatological beauty has crossed the 7 billion euro sales mark and reached a historic market share of 25.5%. [Music] Beautytech is revolutionizing access to skin health by allowing more people to access expert diagnoses. Our Vichi brand is at the heart of this transformation with Scalp Console Pro, an innovative device enabling personalized scalp diagnosis already available in 11 markets. I will also mention Skin Cuticle, which, with its individualized professional service, offers virtual consultations with aesthetic professionals. To meet the challenges of the future, we do more than just sell products. We offer solutions that allow consumers to become active participants in their own skin health. I am particularly proud of our actions to make beauty increasingly inclusive. Only 15% of the cases presented in medical textbooks involve skin rich in melanin. This lack of representation in teaching and research can have an impact on diagnoses and treatments. We have therefore decided to launch the Universal Dermatology of All Colors initiative in order to better understand the specific needs of all phototypes and to ensure access to appropriate care. This initiative is structured around three pillars: improving research, strengthening teaching and ensuring that our innovations meet its needs. To cite just a few examples, the placed rock puts the B3. Thanks to its revolutionary molecule, melasile offers superior anti-pigmentation efficacy on all phototypes. As for Vichi's Ercos shampoo, its effectiveness has been proven on all hair types and all scalp tones. [Music] Approximately 2 billion people worldwide suffer from skin problems. Each year, this situation intensifies due to stress, the aging population, and the impact of climate change. And skin problems have a significant impact on patients' daily lives. It is not just about treating a pathology, it is about fighting against stigmatization. And to complicate matters further, there is a shortage of dermatologists, which makes access to care difficult. That is why we are launching the L’Oréal Act for Dermatology, a 20 million euro commitment over 5 years to fund access to skin health worldwide through four pillars: research, education, awareness and sharing of solutions to improve skin health . In terms of research, we launched the global skin health access observatory with ILDS in 2024. This is the first benchmark study in 194 countries aimed at understanding the distribution of dermatologists, the difficulties in accessing care for patients and the presence of alternative health professionals in dermatological deserts.
