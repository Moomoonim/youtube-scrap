## 스크립트

I think that the average person doesn't usually think about “Where does my food come from?” There's a lot going on behind the scenes. You have Atlantic salmon being farmed in floating net pens in Norway, shipped to Poland or China to be processed, and then being shipped around the world for consumption. That's not just inefficient from an economic perspective, that's not sensible from an environmental perspective as well. Land-based recirculating aquaculture is an obvious alternative. We farm the fish inside a building in a completely controlled environment. Everything in these fish farms was automated: chemical parameters, oxygenation, feeding schedules, lighting schedules. The only thing that was not automated was the management of the fish themselves. What, really, everybody in the industry understands is that fish don't like to be touched. They're covered by this mucus barrier. Once we touch a fish, we injure that, we make them very susceptible to get all kinds of diseases. They get really stressed about it. I figured there's got to be a better way of doing it. That's really what Radmantis is focusing on. We can identify fish of a particular size and harvest them by routing them out towards one exit and all the fish that don't happen to fit the criteria that we want go out the other exit. We train our AI system to recognize physical injury, stress indicators, inflammation issues, so you can remedy and mitigate those problems early on to decrease damage that is being done to the fish population and increase the sustainability of our food production network. The hardware that we're using for all of our work is the Kria SOM. The Kria is one of those fabled combinations of small form factor drawing very little current, with absolutely insane computational power. What we are doing here is trying to understand if the basic principles of animal behavior can be integrated with leading edge technology, which AMD is providing. The combination of the two can generate a new approach to handling live fish. What's meaningful to me is that the fish are treated well, that they have a high quality of life, with conditions that are natural to them. We've been incredibly excited to partner with AMD on this. It has really been a fabulous opportunity for us that we intend to do a lot of good things with.
