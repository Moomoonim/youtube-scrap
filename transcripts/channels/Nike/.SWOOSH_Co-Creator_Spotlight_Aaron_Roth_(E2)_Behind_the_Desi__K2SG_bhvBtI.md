## 스크립트

How's it going? I'm Aaron Roth. I'm a high school teacher in Roseville, California. I'm Bryce Wong. .SWOOSH Virtual Footwear and Collections Designer. My shoe design was “A Pixel Rope” in reference to the old duotone 8-bit games on our phones from back in the day. It was like one part nostalgia, like three parts, like this message that I wanted to share of creativity and like the simplicity of our phones from the past. One of the coolest things about seeing your moodboard turn to virtual shoe was just how succinct your story was. It wasn't till we first started talking about it that we kind of found that there was layers in the story. All of the numbers have a meaning, but it's not the numbers themselves that have the meaning right? And it was actually your idea to bring in, you know, with those old phones we had to use the T9, right? But we ended up taking some of the standard Nike Air branding, but we just did the, you know, 64— is it 6453? We, you know, we ended up taking that and bringing that in and then making it look pixelated like we would have had on all those other phones. And we did the same with the “Air”. We were able to adjust tooling and do things that like were kind of outside the norm. You know, the stuff that only Drake can do on his Air Force 1. But we did that with yours, virtually. That's the kind of stuff that I felt was really cool working with you. And because you had that idea of like what footwear was already, you're able like just dive right in. Even cooler was your knowledge of Nike history and like the fonts, the typefaces we use. In the world of graphic design, for me a really important thing is like typography. My wife and I like, she also does graphic design and like you don't want to go on a road trip with us If you don't want to talk about the fonts being used. You know, Nike and Futura are like, peanut butter and jelly at this point. So I wanted to make sure that, yeah, we could go, I could go find some pixelated font and get permission to use it or whatever. But I want to actually go in and make it. So that was my homework in between some of our meetings was to go in because I was like, I'm making the graphics. Like that's something that I wanted to do. And one of the first concepts that I had, like when we go in the first meeting, was like basically just to like straight up, take the shoe one for one. We talked about having the screen be the tongue itself and we hit some limitations there. But then we were also talking about how like this is really paying homage not just to older phones, but just older games and simpler games, right? But there's definitely a time where we were struggling to figure out how do we translate this? How one-to-one do we want? What's the... kind of like the messaging? So I really like where we landed because it feels like an immediate, like sense of nostalgia. But then you're like, how does that kind of connect to this story? I would love it if you could talk to us about like what the red color symbolizes and then even like what the messaging is kind of on the on the back heel tab. In the first meeting, we kind of almost walked away with just like that phone concept, right? And it was actually Logan called this out, “What about—Where's your story fitting within this?” And that's when I realized, like bringing the red in. So like when we look at a lot of the older phones, like red wasn't a thing that popped up on our screens. Now on our phones, that red dot—and there's like lots of articles and stuff about how it just starts to own our lives, right? And so, I thought like, how can I bring that into the shoe? But it was also this, this idea of like that disruption from from that, that flow maybe that you could say like the creative flow that we can have like in life. As a dad a newer dad, I can spend the day with my daughter and then realize that half that day was spent on my phone. Because these notifications keep popping up. And so I'm not really spending the day with her. At the end of the day, you know, we're snuggling. And right now I'm like, I wish I had more of this. And the reality is I could have had a ton more of it. We put red on the outsole and that was more of a direct call to like those notifications. And we kind of talked about the idea of like stomping them out, but also like stepping by them and like working past them. And then we had the red on the battery and just like that idea of, like the drainage. And so then that ties into the last little detail you mentioned, just barely, which is on the back tab. We put a phone number, right? Because the message that I really wanted to try and encourage as a teacher, as a creator, is like really just having other people create more. I figured out that “create more” is ten digits. And so the if you look at T-9 again, you'll see that that back tab says, “create more”. I think, as somebody who found art and design through my primary school, through high school education, those types of messages and the teachers, like yourself, is what got me, kindn of like, to be where I am today. So it was just such a cool story to be able to like, bring it to life and actually have your voice, through this shoe, kind of telling people to create more. So being part of this one is really cool.
