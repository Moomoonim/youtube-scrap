## 스크립트

I'm from the Bay Area. My parents actually met on Caltrain when they were commuting into the city. My family has a very rich history in San Francisco. My great-grandfather owned a grocery store on Market Street. I love SF because of all the incredible green space and parks like the one we're in right now. I love San Francisco because of its sports teams, its culture, its food, its history. I love SF because it's just a really stunningly beautiful city. So at Waymo, we have our team testing program. The program's goal is for members of the company to ride in our cars and experience it first hand. I was really eager to become a volunteer employee tester because I work on the product. I work on Waymo, and I thought it was extremely important for me to experience it. Being an employee tester at Waymo has been an invaluable experience. Assumptions that we make in the rider app can only be validated by experiencing them first hand. What I think the Waymo Driver can bring to San Francisco is another opportunity to connect the city through transportation. If employees are willing to take our vehicles around the city, I think that shows that we really believe in the safety and quality of our product. We're building this with and for the city. It's gonna be an incredible journey that we're embarking on. Thank you, San Francisco, for your part in this. Stay tuned. There's a lot more to come.
