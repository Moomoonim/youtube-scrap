## 스크립트

So, here’s the pitch. On the Siemens Xcelerator Marketplace you’ll find everything you need to digitally transform your business. It’s an open platform with products from Siemens and Marketplace sellers, offering everything from connected hardware and services to software and add-ons. You can access it at any time. Just pick what you need from the drop-down menu, like a service to make your business more sustainable. Then explore your options. You can also connect with Marketplace sellers, like someone offering a service tailored to your needs. And they’ll share expertise, offer products or help you develop something new. If you need help beyond what’s available, just connect with Siemens and we’ll figure it out together. In a nutshell, the Siemens Xcelerator Marketplace is a one-stop shop of scalable products for all your business challenges.
