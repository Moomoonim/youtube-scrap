## 스크립트

I didn't know I was a brown woman until I entered the world of work. And everyone was like, you're a brown woman. I'm like, but I'm just a fresh graduate ready to get on with my life. Around the age of 13, girls start to think or believe that they're not good enough for X role, for Y role, or that they're different to their male counterparts. What are we doing to make sure that structures are equitable? And that's why community is so important. When I started Like Minded Females it was 2018. I invited all my contacts. I was like, I'm just going to share things in the news. And we want to talk about substance. We want to talk about how do you build your confidence? How do you overcome challenges, especially within the workplace? LinkedIn helped me build a community, because that's how Like Minded Females started. It was a LinkedIn group, which then turned into a brunch club, which is now a social enterprise still using LinkedIn as our main platform. Let's build communities. Let's enable confidence. And let's encourage everyone to ask questions.
