## 스크립트

I love this project with ZETA and LOBA because it really shows what is possible if you have companies that simply just want to be innovative, want to drive things forward, and it shows us that in Europe, we can make it. LOBA and pharma&amp; took on the challenge in ‘21 with the decision to build our own biotech with not a lot of experience in doing that. Having in mind always that in 2025 the world supply of interferon ceases because there is no more stock left over. We had here the chance with a Greenfield project to build the digital twin together on the basis of COMOS from the beginning of the project and bring it into real life. The plant we built for LOBA Biotech would not have happened in this short period of time without the Siemens Xcelerator. From the earliest design stages on we employed digital techniques, digital twins in order to be more robust, to be error-free, to accelerate the project, to enhance collaboration. Siemens Accelerator platform helped ZETA for the start of production at LOBA, 40% earlier. And in the end, it will save lots of time it will make processes much more efficient. I’m very proud of the team and the people that work for us every day and go the extra mile every day, and I’m so relieved for all the patients and the families worldwide that we can deliver this rare molecule and medicine to support their everyday quality of life.
