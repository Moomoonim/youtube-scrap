## 스크립트

The goals for the first-generation LEAF were very simple: fit five adults comfortably and drive 100 miles. I wanted to create a car that feels great to drive. We had to bring the battery’s energy density and powertrain efficiency to the top benchmark. Even the tires had to perform at a high level. To enhance the EV's quietness, we shaped the headlights like a canopy and controlled the airflow moving to the rear to eliminate noise. The motivation was clear: to create a new kind of mobility for a new era.
