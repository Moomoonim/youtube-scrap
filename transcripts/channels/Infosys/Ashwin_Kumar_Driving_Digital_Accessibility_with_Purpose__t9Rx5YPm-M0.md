## 스크립트

[Music] Hi, I am Ashwin Kumar and I'm working with Infosys from past 7 years as a quality engineering lead. My vision is to strengthen the culture of looking at accessibility as a best practice to make tech accessible for more than 1 billion people with disabilities living on this planet. technology and softwares became a barrier to me since I happened to become legally blind due to retinal dysfunction. When I adopted assistive technology, I used to face a lot of challenges due to accessibility gaps in the software applications. This episode made me take up digital accessibility as a very serious agenda and do something big in the space. It has given me an opportunity to set up a accessibility experience lab at Bangalore DC. The lab is equipped with the range of assistive devices to give an immersive understanding on digital inclusion through disability simulations, demonstration of challenges faced by assistive tech users due to accessibility gaps and how Infosys has built a futuristic platform using AI to identify accessibility gaps early in the development stage. Ashwin's work has extended to educating hundreds and thousands of people on this idea of accessibility and it also has created lot of content that is getting shared and learned by many other people. I reached out to enable India to take up courses in digital accessibility. Later I earned certification from IAP which paved my way to join Infosys as a visionary. Infosys has supported me in all possible ways and nominated me for NCPDP LTI M tree Helen Killer award in the year 2023. I also won the pride of Bangalore DC title at Infosys Pinnacle Awards at DC level. Technology is a fantastic enabler to build an inclusive world for everyone. So at Infosys, our diversity, equity, and inclusion stand is pencled into an important tenant of the Infosys code of conduct. It's called respecting each other. It becomes important to each one of us to be mindful about people with disabilities while crafting digital solutions and experiences. Digital accessibility needs to be built in and not bolted on the top. Let's together build an inclusive digital world where no one is left out. [Music]
