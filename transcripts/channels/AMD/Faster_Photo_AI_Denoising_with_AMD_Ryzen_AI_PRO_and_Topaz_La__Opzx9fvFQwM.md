## 스크립트

Photo editors, graphic designers and everyday enthusiasts are always looking for ways to make their images look crisper and clearer. Topaz Photo AI provides a range of tools like denoising, sharpening, focusing and face recovery. Today, we'll use AI to repair an overly noisy image without damaging the detail you'd want to keep. Traditional denoising can be slow and tedious, but with AMD Ryzen™ AI PRO processors running Topaz Photo AI, it's easier than ever. Topaz Photo AI uses NPU native denoising, which is optimized for AI tasks and offloads preview calculations from the CPU and GPU to maintain smooth system performance. Every time the user adjusts a setting, the NPU via AMD Ryzen™ AI PRO processors activates to handle the workload. As a native NPU process, rendering is nearly instantaneous and minimal CPU and GPU usage means the system remains responsive throughout the process. Despite the complex AI calculations. With AMD Ryzen™ AI PRO processors and Topaz Labs Photo AI software, photo editors can denoise their images faster and easier than ever while also leaving CPU and GPU based resources available for other editing tasks.
