Two come to mind in particular, Runway ML and Lightning AI. Runway has been a close partner with Nvidia. Nvidia has featured Runway and the CEO Crystal Ball has been getting a lot of advice and getting really close with Jensen and it's been an incredibly valuable experience. The NVIDIA VC Alliance program has allowed me personally to reach so many new founders whether it's speaking at the inception program at GTC or just working with the team and we always really appreciate that because founders care what Nvidia is doing. I have been working in this industry for over 30 years working with some of the most famous founders that you can think of. I worked with Mark Zuckerberg for about 10 years at Facebook and before that I worked with Mark Beni off for about 13 years at Salesforce. It is the excitement and energy that you get from working with a founder on their idea in this new way of doing things that is never old and I absolutely love that and I treasure it and I'm so grateful for it.
