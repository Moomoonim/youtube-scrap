## 스크립트

[music] &gt;&gt; What a great first day we've had at Mobile World Congress. We kicked things off on Sunday with our present analyst event where we showcased our growing ecosystem of customers and partners as we work together to shape the future of connectivity and AI native networks. Day one has been incredibly busy. We welcomed our customers for guided tours through more than 30 live demos on the stand giving people a hands-on look at how Nokia is bringing intelligence, automation, and performance into next-gen networks. Today we opened our studio theater with partners presenting throughout the afternoon. It's been fantastic to hear real deployment stories and future visions. And you can join us for more sessions during throughout the week. Today we shared major announcements. First, Nokia is accelerating AI run through new partnerships to drive the industry toward AI native 6G networks. Second, we launched our new RF 8 radio platform. We also welcomed our futurist in residence, Cathy Hackl, who toured the stand to explore how AI workloads are changing the nature of network traffic. In our performance area, visitors experienced live demonstrations across fiber, IP, optical, and radio solutions showcasing the power behind high-performance AI-ready networks. We'll be back soon with the latest updates from the Nokia booth. Follow us on social media and at nokia.com.
