## 스크립트

The Service Manager workspace is a Fusion Agility app designed to help organizations manage customer service operations at scale. Teams of specialist agents continuously reason across customer activity, operations, and workflows to highlight priority actions and summarize top customer service issues, organize escalations, identify stalled requests, flag performance issues, and service tickets at risk of violating service levels. Starting with escalations, agents have identified a severity one ticket involving an inverter failure. We see that the automated service agents interacted with the customer making three attempts to resolve the issue before escalating for human intervention rather than simply handing off the ticket. The system has preserved the full interaction history and coordinated across service, product, and knowledge agents to recommend the next priority action, add Abdel to the team. To better understand the agent's recommendation, we ask Oracle, "Why are you recommending Abdel?" It explains the strength of Abdel's product expertise, account familiarity, and case history. Satisfied with the recommendation, we confirm. Communication agents have prepared a message for the customer using the latest ticket and resolution context. We can review, edit, and send. Next, we look at service requests with a risk of delayed resolution. On the first ticket, agents have determined that the assigned technician is unavailable, so they recommend re-queuing the request so that the triage and assignment agents can coordinate the best available replacement technician. We confirm, and the system immediately advances the service request towards resolution. These are just some of the ways Fusion Agility apps help service organizations operate at scale. Specialist agents continuously reason across customer activity, operations, and workflows. They identify top issues and recommend priority actions so people spend time applying judgment and confirming recommendations that move work forward.
