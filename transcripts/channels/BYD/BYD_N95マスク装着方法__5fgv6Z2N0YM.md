## 스크립트

Here's how to wear the byd n95 particulate mask. Before putting on the mask, wash your hands and ensure they are clean. Take out the mask and hold it with the nose wire facing upwards. Spread the mask out so that the elastic bands hang down on the outside, creating a cup shape. Put your hands between the mask and the two elastic bands and place the mask on your face, wrapping it around your chin. While holding the mask in place, attach the lower elastic band to the back of your neck and the other elastic band near the top of your head. Press the nose wire along the shape of a flower with your fingers to make it fit snugly against your face and adjust the position of the elastic bands. Fitting Test The fitting test checks for air leakage from the gap between the n95 mask and your face to ensure it is properly fitted and should be performed every time you put on the mask. First, cover the entire mask with both hands and slowly and deeply inhale to check for positive pressure. Make sure the mask indents inwards towards your face. Then, slowly and deeply exhale to check for positive pressure. If you feel air leaking from between the mask and your face, tighten the nose wire and adjust the position of the mask. Repeat this fitting test until you can wear the mask correctly. How to remove the mask First, remove the elastic band at the back of the neck. Next, remove the elastic band at the top of the head. Dispose of the mask without touching its surface.
