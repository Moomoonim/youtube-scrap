## 스크립트

- Today we live in a society where systems haven't been designed with accessibility in mind. And what that means in practice is that one in four people living in the UK do not currently have an equal future. At Scope, what we're trying to achieve is disability equality. As a disability charity, it's really important that we live our values. Accessibility is at our very core. We wanted to make sure that all of our colleagues were empowered in the right way to be able to do their job to the best of their ability. - When I heard that Scope wanted to start using Copilot, I got really excited that Copilot could help streamline some processes. Oh wow. Then that can help more impact to start thinking about how we can use this data, what it means. - Recently we've been looking at agents that our colleagues at Scope can use. - That's so cool. Having an agent to get that data from Dynamics would be amazing. - Hargreaves is an agent that we created to allow our staff to load a document into Copilot and Hargreaves will assess the document for accessibility. It has our content on accessible writing, the Scope tone of voice. - So we're saving time, removing subjectivity and basing it solely on facts. What Copilot's done is democratize accessibility. - I'm about to chair a board meeting of Scope and I'm using my adaptive tech one keystroke, no mouse. The more inclusive you are and that includes employing more disabled people, the better your organization will do. And that is factually correct. And I'm a living, breathing, walking example. - One thing we're working on is agentic portal, that will really enable employers across the whole of the UK to have confidence in recruiting disabled people and supporting them as they thrive in their careers. - As people understand, adopt, integrate technology, I think there are more opportunities for disabled people than for almost anybody else. - This kind of technology, it doesn't change what I'm capable of doing, it just removes some of the barriers. My thinking and analysis can be judged for the work it produces rather than the effort it takes to produce.
