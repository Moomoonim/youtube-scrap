## 스크립트

The Swiss team now plays in blue &amp; white. I know why. Puma made the new jerseys. Swisscom colours. Can I help you? Blue, red, white. All jerseys in the Swisscom colours. I was in the same situation. I had to keep it a secret for 20 years that Swisscom is behind the slope colours at the Ski World Cup. OK. Now it’s happening again. Swisscom’s sponsoring the SFV, and poof! All jerseys in the Swisscom colours. The blue is actually no coincidence. Aha! Here’s the deal: the blue &amp; white away jersey is inspired by the Jungfraujoch. Huh? The blue symbolises eternal ice, and these lines here are the tracks that lead to the summit. Ah – the ‘Top of Europe’ Indeed! That’s the Swiss team’s goal. Hm. You’ve almost convinced me. But not quite. Blue, red, white. It really is quite nice. And the size? Yes... it’s good, yes.
