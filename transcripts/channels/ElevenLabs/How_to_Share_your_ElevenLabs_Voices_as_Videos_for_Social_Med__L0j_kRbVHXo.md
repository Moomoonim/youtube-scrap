## 스크립트

In this quick tutorial, I'm going to show you how to generate a sharable AI voice video like this with 11 Labs. These videos are a really fun way to share your best generations to social media or in group chats. To get started, either head to 11labs.io or download the mobile app on iOS and Android. In the texttospech editor, click generate speech and once you found a generation you're happy with, click the share icon. Choose between a colorful video or a black and white version.
