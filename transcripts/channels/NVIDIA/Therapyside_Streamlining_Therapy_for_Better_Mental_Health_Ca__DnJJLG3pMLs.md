## 스크립트

[Music] When we launched therapy side, we wanted the therapist to focus on what they know how to do best, which is providing therapy and taking care of their clients, their patients. Our philosophy in therapy side is we take care of everything else, right? From admin task, calendar management, payment as well as patient follow-ups. We leverage both the hardware and the software from Nvidia for our AI features within therapy side. We leverage the NIMS from Nvidia. We use ASR to get the therapy session recap automatically generated via our platform. This allow the therapist to fully focus on the patient during the session rather than taking notes again. They can save up to 20 minutes per session. This allows them to increase their patient portfolio, for example, by up to 40%. therapists can see many more patients, right? And in a situation like the one that we have in Europe where we're running out of therapist, it's quite an important point. The therapist can also speak with our AI agent which is called Maya. And Maya is leveraging the RAG, not only retrieving information from previous session, but is also helping you scheduling your session, making your bills and doing follow-ups with your patients. [Music] But the most valuable thing we received from inception is the technical support. We have also gained valuable connection from inception as well as mentorship. The whole architecture of our AI has been developed together with Nvidia. This has allow us to develop all the AI feature that we have today in therapy side and reach many more therapists and make mental health much more accessible and better. [Music]
