## 스크립트

My journey through ARM has been me starting as an apprentice four years ago. With the apprenticeship program, they pay for your degree and they pay you a salary and you work for ARM and you study at uni at the same time. Throughout those teams of rotations, I've learned different things. Um, gained so many skills. I'm now a bit more confident in my skills and myself as an engineer. What I'll tell anyone looking to work at ARM is that you are only as limited as you limit yourself.
