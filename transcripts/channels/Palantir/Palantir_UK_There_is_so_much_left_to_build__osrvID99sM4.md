## 스크립트

[MUSIC PLAYING] I joined our NHS deployment and we just finished a successful pilot with Chelsea and Westminster Trust and we were able to reduce the inpatient waitlist by 28%. And we were given the task of to take that solution and to deploy it to 30 trusts across England. The first six months in the job I worked on six different projects for six different government clients in six different countries. My very first deployment was with Ferrari and specifically Scuderia Ferrari, which is the Formula One team and the first race of the season is in Australia. But we won! I say we-- they-- but we felt like part of it. I was recently on a project with Airbus to build out a solution that helps them build more planes per year. Actually knocking out something that will help transform our pandemic response. That was, by far, the most incredible project I got to lead in my career. So at Palantir, we have really small, lean teams, and that allows us to have a huge amount of responsibility. It's the creative ground where you get to just apply your best efforts, fail gloriously, learn from it, try again, make stuff happen. Definitely being a forward-deployed software engineer, then using your technical skills to then solve a problem. You really do see a problem and solve it right away without thinking twice or asking for permission. I think Palantir is the only place where you can do the depth of engineering but then also have rapid impact in the high velocity, working over a weekend with, like, your friends and teammates to get something really important done. I get to work with customers and then I also get to build the solution. I would honestly say, like, the growth opportunity that I have within Palantir is something that I wouldn't be able to do elsewhere, take on the things that I think I can do and take them to the next level. There's an incredible pool of people here who are both extremely social, but also extremely talented and smart. Sort of a place where we do a lot of R&amp;D. We're pushing the product to its edge. In fact, I have found that Palantir, surprisingly, to me, is the chattiest company I have ever been at, and will probably ever be at as well. We have product development, we have business development, we have the internal teams, we have lawyers, we have all sorts. That creates a wonderful community. [MUSIC PLAYING]
