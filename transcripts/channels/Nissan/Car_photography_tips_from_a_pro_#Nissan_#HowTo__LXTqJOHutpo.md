## 스크립트

My name is Hirohiko Mochizuki I specialize in car photography I've been taking pictures of cars for more than 30 years I’d like to share some tips for taking good photos By shooting in an open area with nothing around you can capture the car's beautiful lines In general, a ratio showing 30 percent grille and 70 percent side body of the car is best Some sports cars are a little low but generally shooting from the height of the lights is safest Shooting from a low angle ﻿creates a powerful look If you find the right angle and lighting to highlight the car’s character lines you can emphasize its personality Dark colors are more difficult to shoot, especially black It’s like a mirror reflecting nearby objects In stronger light, it's easier to shoot brighter colors like silver It's easiest to photograph in cloudy weather If you work in strong daytime light, the contrast can become too strong You can take very beautiful photos in the soft, oblique light of morning or at dusk when the sun is low Dynamic shots are extremely difficult so you need a certain amount of experience and know-how The most important thing to pay attention to is the lens’ focal length I think using a more standard length or slightly telephoto lens is best One of my lenses is a 24-105mm standard zoom lens With smartphones so widely available it's possible for anyone, anywhere, to take great photos Recent smartphones come with three lenses: wide-angle, standard, and telephoto Using a wide-angle lens makes the car look distorted but a lens with a standard or slightly telephoto focal length can beautifully capture the car's shape The more photos you take, the better they’ll become It's important to try different angles and types of lighting That’s the first step to taking better pictures!
