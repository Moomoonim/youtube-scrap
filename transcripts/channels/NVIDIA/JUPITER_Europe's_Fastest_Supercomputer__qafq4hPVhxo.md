## 스크립트

Supercomputing makes modern science possible. Meet Jupiter, Europe's fastest supercomputer. 24,000 Gracehopper super chips form an exoscale AI system solving science's biggest challenges. At Germany's ULIC supercomputing center, pabytes of high-speed memory help researchers tackle climate, quantum computing, material science, physics, biology, and AI. Each GH200 pairs a GPU and CPU with 900 gigabytes per second bandwidth and 96 gigabytes of HBM3. Four GH200s form a memory coherent node via high-speed Nvink. Quantum 2 Infiniband connects the system for scalable performance. Researchers simulate planetary systems and model quantum states using AI co-pilots to analyze data and support real-time decisions. To grasp the scale of AI supercomputing, a single hopper GPU processes 50 tokens per second on Llama 3.3 Neatron 49B. Plenty for a single researcher. Multiply that by Jupiter's 24,000 chips. That's 1.2 million tokens per second. Jupiter at the ULIC Supercomputing Center. Ready for what's next?
